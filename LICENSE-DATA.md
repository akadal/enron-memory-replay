This covers:

- `dataset/manifest.csv`, `dataset/slices.csv`, `dataset/mailbox-stats.csv`
- the capture records in `dataset/capture/` and the inbox notes in `memory/archive/inbox/`
- the distilled memory: `memory/domains/`, `memory/people/`, `memory/state.md`,
  `memory/questions.md`, `memory/todo.md`, and the fact lines in `memory/archive/<domain>/`
- the ledgers, drops, run records and run transcripts in `dataset/ledger/`, `dataset/drops/`,
  `dataset/runs/`
- the design description, reviews and validation records in `dataset/DESIGN.md`,
  `dataset/SOURCE.md`, `dataset/audit/`, `dataset/validation/`

It does not cover the text of the Enron messages (`memory/archive/mail/`, and quotations from
them anywhere in the repository). That text comes from the Enron corpus as released by Carnegie
Mellon University (https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~enron/); no rights are claimed over it.

`LICENSE`.
