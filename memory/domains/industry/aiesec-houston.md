# AIESEC Houston and the owner

- [2000-01-25] Shelly Jones (analyst recruiting manager, Associate/Analyst Program): after the program's restructuring (new interview, pay and offer policies) Enron will not join the University of Houston AIESEC program this year; she sent the owner its literature, citing his past interest. ←[[m00442]]
- [2000-01-28] Biliana Pehlivanova (VP Incoming Exchange, AIESEC Houston) sent contacts of the Polish AIESEC trainees in College Station (Rutkowski, Dalecka, Mas, Olczyk, Lyczak) and invited the owner to a paintball team-building game Sat 5 Feb and to role-play a company representative at Mock Sales day Sat 12 Feb; times to follow. Owner forwarded it to his AOL. ←[[m00461]]
- [2000-01-31] Owner to the Polish trainees: "I used to work for AIESEC in Poland in the 1970s." and "I shall be glad to meet you when you visit Houston." ←[[m00500]]
- [2000-02-02] To Pehlivanova: no paintball ("I have prior commitments on Saturday, February the 5th."); glad to join Mock Sales day, wants details; "There is a remote possibility I shall have to go to London that weekend." ←[[m00530]]
- [2000-02-10] Pehlivanova: Mock Sales Day Sat 12 Feb, UH College of Business: owner plays a company representative in practice sales calls 1:00-3:30 (new members try to sign him up for an international intern; she supplies cases); breakfast 9-10, sales training 10-12 and lunch with the Board of Advisors 12-1 are open to him; recruitment brought 16 new members (10 experienced); she wants his arrival time. ←[[m00595]]
