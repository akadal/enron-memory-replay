# AIESEC Houston and the owner

- [2000-01-25] Shelly Jones (analyst recruiting manager, Associate/Analyst Program): after the program's restructuring (new interview, pay and offer policies) Enron will not join the University of Houston AIESEC program this year; she sent the owner its literature, citing his past interest. ←[[m00442]]
- [2000-01-28] AIESEC Houston (Biliana Pehlivanova) sent the Polish trainees' contacts and invited the owner to paintball 5 Feb (declined) and Mock Sales day 12 Feb. ←[[m00461]] ←[[m00530]]
- [2000-01-31] Owner to the Polish trainees: "I used to work for AIESEC in Poland in the 1970s." and "I shall be glad to meet you when you visit Houston." ←[[m00500]]
- [2000-02-10] Mock Sales Day Sat 12 Feb, UH: owner plays a company representative in practice sales calls 1:00-3:30; detail ←[[aiesec-houston-2000-02-w07]] ←[[m00595]]
- [2000-02-17] AIESEC Polska's EuroLDS 2000 (Warsaw, from 7 Mar; Andrzej Wodnicki, SGH, calls the owner the founder of AIESEC in Poland) is $12,000 short; owner asked Jarek Astramowicz (Enron Poland): "Czy Enron moze pomoc w organizacji tej imprezy?" To Wodnicki: only Enron Poland can fund it; "Mnie byloby bardzo trudno usprawiedliwic to z mojego budzetu." ←[[m00667]] ←[[m00699]]
