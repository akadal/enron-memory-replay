# AIESEC Houston

- [2000-01-25] Shelly Jones (analyst recruiting): Enron will not take part in the University of Houston AIESEC program this year, after the Associate/Analyst Program restructuring (new interview, pay and offer policies); literature sent to the owner given his past interest. ←[[m00442]]
- [2000-01-28] Biliana Pehlivanova (AIESEC Houston, VP Incoming Exchange) invited the owner to a paintball team-building game Sat 5 Feb and to Mock Sales day Sat 12 Feb (share his AIESEC and work experience); details to follow the week of 31 Jan. She also sent contacts for five Polish trainees working at Destia in College Station. Owner forwarded it to his AOL address. ←[[m00461]]
- [2000-01-31] Owner to the five Polish trainees: "I used to work for AIESEC in Poland in the 1970s." He will meet them in Houston. ←[[m00500]]
- [2000-02-02] Owner declined the 5 Feb paintball ("prior commitments") but will join Mock Sales day 12 Feb unless he is in London. ←[[m00530]]
