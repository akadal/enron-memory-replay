# Exchange-traded weather and insurance derivatives

The owner's weather panel at Risk 2000: memory/domains/industry/speaking-engagements-2000.md. London's weather pricing: memory/domains/organization/london-research.md

- [2000-04-05] Hrgovcic's background for the weather derivatives presentation they discussed: Pacific Decadal Oscillation sources (U. Washington, NOAA) and the London weather group's www.weather-risk.com (press presentations); per Enron's traders CME weather trading is down to a couple of deals a month, and CME press releases gloss over the low liquidity. ←[[m01253]]
- [2000-04-05] Business Insurance (Gavin Souter, 20 Mar; via Lucy Ortiz): CME weather futures since Sep 1999, 420 contracts, mostly between dealers; CATEX about 450 trades ($400 million premium, 160 subscribers), now chiefly "cash for cover" reinsurance; no CBOT cat option trades in 2000; the Bermuda Commodities Exchange suspended; a soft reinsurance market is blamed. Owner kept a copy on AOL: "Thanks." ←[[m01255]] ←[[m01256]] ←[[m01257]]
- [2000-05-11] Damian Goodburn (Rochester Partnership, London): weather risk business is picking up in Europe/UK; Socgen did a weather deal covering a Japanese company's flower crop against wind damage; Marsh 'Risk Solutions' page. Owner to Hrgovcic: "FYI". ←[[m01646]]
