# POWER 2000 conference, Houston, 9-10 May 2000

- [1999-12-14] [[emma-wolfin]] invited the owner to chair a day-2 stream or give a full presentation; some talks were already taken; she asked for a quick reply. ←[[m00021]]
- [1999-12-15] The owner left the choice to her (owner, verbatim: "It's your choice.") and offered three topics: effectiveness of insurance as a risk management tool; real option theory for valuing power plants; overcoming difficulties in estimating volatility. ←[[m00024]]
- [1999-12-16] The owner declined the M&A session (stream 3, day 2; "not my cup of tea") and prefers to chair stream 1 on day 1, "Pricing and Trading in the US Power Market" (speakers include Spyros Maragos of Dynegy, Sanjeev Khanna of PG&E, Gary Morsches of Southern, Blake Johnson of Stanford, Craig Pirrong of Washington University). Wolfin called it a good fit; his earlier topics were taken. ←[[m00030]]
- [1999-12-16] The owner corrected the draft brochure title to "VP, Research at Enron Corp." (the draft said VP, Head of Research at Enron North America). ←[[m00039]]
