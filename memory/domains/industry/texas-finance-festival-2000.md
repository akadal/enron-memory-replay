# Texas Finance Festival 2000, San Antonio

- [1999-12-28] John D. Martin (Baylor) announced TFF 2000 to a long list of finance academics that includes the owner: San Antonio in April, earlier than usual, program starting on a Friday; register early, and book the hotel at once because only a limited number of rooms are held at the reduced rate. ←[[m00107]]
- [1999-12-30] The owner forwarded it to [[shirley-crenshaw]]: register him and say whether there is a conflict (owner, verbatim: "Please, register me. Is there any  conflict?"). ←[[m00107]]
- [1999-12-30→] Open: Shirley's answer on conflicts and the registration are not in the mail so far. ←[[m00107]]
- [2000-01-06] Cindy Justice sent the owner and colleagues (Celeste Roberts, Charlene Jackson, Karen Marshall, Ken Reeves, Meryl Barnett) the TFF registration form: 7-8 April 2000, San Antonio, organised by John D. Martin (Baylor); space and reduced-rate rooms are limited. ←[[m00164]]
