# Speaking invitations the owner declined, 2000

Accepted roles: memory/domains/industry/speaking-engagements-2000.md

- [2000-01-10] Infocast "Market Price Volatility" (Chicago, 10-12 May 2000, a more technical follow-up to its Houston meeting; Ron Henderson, ronh@informationforecast.com, 818-888-4445 x28): offered the volatility case-study session (10 May) or "A Real Options Approach to Asset Valuation" (11 May); owner: "due to a scheduling conflict I cannot speak at this conference." ←[[m00205]]
- [2000-01-10] IIR Energy conference (Washington DC, 19-20 Jun 2000; Mark Thabet, VP Energy & Utilities, IIR New York) would have added "Forecasting and Measuring your Exposures to Energy Price Risk" for him; declined for the same reason. ←[[m00208]]
- [2000-01-12] Both then asked for a staff speaker; owner: "We are really swamped and I would like to keep our involvement in conferences to a reasonable minimum." To Infocast: "I can promise that we shall help you with a future conference if it happens to be in Houston." ←[[m00331]] ←[[m00332]]
