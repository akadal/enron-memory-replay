# Invitations and sponsorships the owner declined, 2000

Accepted roles: memory/domains/industry/speaking-engagements-2000.md. Detail ←[[declined-invitations-2000-04-w15]]

- [2000-01-10] Infocast "Market Price Volatility" (Chicago, 10-12 May 2000; Ron Henderson): offered a volatility case study or "A Real Options Approach to Asset Valuation"; owner: "due to a scheduling conflict I cannot speak at this conference." ←[[m00205]]
- [2000-01-10] IIR Energy conference (Washington DC, 19-20 Jun 2000; Mark Thabet, IIR New York) would have added "Forecasting and Measuring your Exposures to Energy Price Risk" for him; declined for the same reason. ←[[m00208]]
- [2000-01-12] Both then asked for a staff speaker; owner: "We are really swamped and I would like to keep our involvement in conferences to a reasonable minimum." To Infocast: "I can promise that we shall help you with a future conference if it happens to be in Houston." ←[[m00331]] ←[[m00332]]
- [2000-01-23] WorldPower's (Isherwood, Commodities Now) world power markets book: sponsorship at £19,000 met a "lukewarm" reaction from the power desks; Mark Palmer advised against the ad offer; owner: "They do not seem to have the market penetration we need"; "I shall notify them." ←[[m00271]] ←[[m00416]] ←[[m00424]]
- [2000-04-13] Infocast "Valuing Electric Power Assets and Companies: A Real Options Perspective" (Chicago, 31 Jul-2 Aug 2000; Britta Bothe): the owner declined to speak; she asks whether anyone else at Enron would present. ←[[m01324]] ←[[m01325]]
