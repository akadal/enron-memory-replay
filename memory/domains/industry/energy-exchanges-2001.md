# Competing online energy exchanges (2001)

- [2001-02-12] The owner forwards Kenneth Parkhill a Reuters story (8 Feb): TradeSpark has handled trades worth over $16 billion notional since trading began 2 Oct 2000 (over $9 billion gas, about $6 billion power); 125 companies, about 550 traders; non-founding partners made 58% of January trades (48% in December); founders eSpeed, Cantor Fitzgerald, Williams, TXU and Coral (Shell), Dynegy to join later. ←[[m05841]]
- [2001-05-25] Rice MBA students' report on competitive threats to EnronOnline, drafted by Kenneth Parkhill for the research newsletter's tech corner: owner to Bob Shults, "I would like to make sure that we don't cross the line." (screens can leak outside Enron); Kal Shah dislikes the heading and the EOL recommendations, wants a students/no-endorsement/internal-only note, to settle Tue; owner: it can be "a useful source of information about alternative electronic trading platforms", for employees only; Parkhill edits it with Shah. ←[[m08000]] ←[[m08008]]
