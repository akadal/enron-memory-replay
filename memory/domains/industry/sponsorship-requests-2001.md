# Sponsorship requests to the owner, 2001

Detail ←[[sponsorship-requests-2001-2001-03-w12]]

Earlier membership and sponsorship offers: memory/domains/industry/research-memberships-2000.md.

- [2001-03-01] Hill Huntington (Stanford EMF), on Frank Wolak's suggestion: a conference on real-time pricing for retail power customers ("the forgotten resource"), output aimed at policymakers; asks Enron for $25,000 as a major sponsor and for participant ideas, maybe the owner. ←[[m06024]]
- [2001-03-12] Huntington meeting Thu 15 Mar, 10 am, 408 Terman Center: "Thursday 10 a.m. works for me."; 15 Mar: RTP conference targeted for Thu-Fri 21-22 Jun at Stanford, revised retail notes sent. ←[[m06194]] ←[[m06238]]
- [2001-03-19] Owner sends the RTP outline to John Henderson and Krishna: "Please, let me know if you are personally interested and who else in your respective  organizations would like to attend."; Krishna counts himself and Osman in ("Please,  confirm with Hill Huntington."); Henderson names Anoush Farhangi; 20 Mar Krishna tells Huntington EES sends Farhangi, Sezgen and Krishnarao; Farhangi (VP Load Research, New Power) asks him to include New Power, which runs a major RTP and retail price-elasticity initiative. ←[[m06275]] ←[[m06277]] ←[[m06278]] ←[[m06282]] ←[[m06316]] ←[[m06318]]
- [2001-03-23] Huntington: several Enron people interested; would those groups sponsor, and should he ask them directly? Owner: "Yes, I think it can be done." and asks him to call. ←[[m06373]]
