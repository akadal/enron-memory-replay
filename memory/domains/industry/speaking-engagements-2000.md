# Owner's conference roles, 2000

## POWER 2000, Houston, 9-10 May (Risk, Emma Wolfin)

- [1999-12-14] Emma Wolfin (risk-ny@email.msn.com, 212 925 1864 x151) invited the owner to chair or speak. ←[[m00021]]
- [1999-12-15] Owner offered either; proposed talks: insurance as a risk tool, real options for power plants, volatility estimation. ←[[m00024]]
- [1999-12-16] His topics were taken; he chose chairing stream 1, day 1 "Pricing and Trading in the US Power Market" (Maragos, Khanna, Morsches, Blake Johnson, Pirrong) over M&A ("not my cup of tea"). ←[[m00030]]
- [1999-12-16] Wolfin confirmed him as chairman, stream 1, day 1 (9 May); brochure title "VP, Research at Enron Corp." ←[[m00039]]

## European Energy 2000, Amsterdam, 3-4 April (Risk, Angela Adedeji)

- [1999-12-16] Owner is a speaker and agreed to chair the stream of his talk. ←[[m00032]]

## Real Options conference, New York, mid-March 2000

- [1999-12-17] Owner accepted Lenos Trigeorgis's invitation to speak at "Real Options Valuation in the New Economy" (tentatively 13-15 Mar; chair Michael Brennan, keynote Stewart Myers; presenters pay no fee) and asked Shirley Crenshaw to schedule it. Owed: 10-line abstract + 3-4 bullets (deadline was 17 Dec), cc Gordon Sick. ←[[m00067]]
