# FERC cap on transmission resale

- [2000-03-07] Christi Nicolay (to the owner, Martin Lin, Grant Masson, Richard Shapiro and others): outside a few regions marketers cannot hedge transmission risk by reselling it above cost; FERC staff (Dick O'Neill) were not inclined last year without restrictions. ←[[m00894]]
- [2000-03-07] Martin Lin sits on a Harvard Electricity Group panel, "Market Turmoil, Trading and Risk Management", 16 Mar, with O'Neill, a Morgan Stanley rep and Ed Cazalet (APX); Nicolay asks him to use recent power and gas orders to win O'Neill's support, after which Enron may reapproach FERC. ←[[m00894]]
