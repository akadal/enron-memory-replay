# Infocast "Market Price Volatility", Chicago, 10-12 May 2000

His earlier Infocast panel: memory/domains/industry/infocast-volatility-conference-1999.md

- [2000-01-10] Ron Henderson (Infocast) had invited the owner or a staff member to a case-study session on modelling volatility (10 May) or a talk on a real options approach to asset valuation (11 May), wanting a technical case study: a model for one market, estimated on one dataset, with candidate models compared. The owner declined (owner, verbatim: "due to a scheduling conflict I cannot speak at this conference."). ←[[m00205]]
- [2000-01-12] The owner declined again: the group is swamped and keeps conference involvement to a minimum (owner, verbatim: "We are really swamped and I would like to keep our involvement in conferences to a reasonable minimum."); his group will help with a future conference if it is in Houston. Henderson's question whether a staff member could give the real options talk (11 May, 10:30-12:00) got no named replacement. ←[[m00331]]
