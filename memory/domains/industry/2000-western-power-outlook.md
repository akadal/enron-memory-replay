# Western / California power outlook for summer 2000

- [2000-01-20] CERA alert "Demand Overtakes Hydro in 2000": gas-fired generation demand up ~600 MMcf/d (Q3 up to 1,200); Topock premium over Henry Hub ~$0.25 in Q3 2000; California precipitation ~26% of average. Owner to Mike Roberts and Ronnie Chahal: "An area to focus on." ←[[m00406]]
- [2000-01-21] Owner to Rick Buy and Ted Murphy: "We see the possibility of higher prices and of spikes (bad hydro conditions in California, growing demand for power)." ←[[m00417]]
