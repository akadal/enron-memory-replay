# EPRM request for a new Enron speaker (Monte Carlo course)

- [1999-12-10] [[helen-evans]], producer of EPRM Conferences & Courses at RISK, had asked the owner on 1999-12-06 to recommend somebody new from Enron to speak at a training course she is producing on the Monte Carlo technique; the owner forwarded the request to [[stinson-gibner]] ("This is the request I got from RISK."). ←[[m00002]]
- [1999-12-10] The owner told Helen Evans he had forwarded her message to his associate Stinson Gibner, "whom I can wholeheartedly recommend"; Stinson was copied. ←[[m00003]]
- [1999-12-20] Benjamin Parsons wrote that EPRM (Angela Adedeji) invited him to speak at European Energy 2000, Amsterdam, 3-4 April, and that Stinson forwarded his name to EPRM for a Monte Carlo conference about a week later. He asked the owner whether to attend both or prioritise; no answer in the message. Adedeji wanted a reply before Christmas. ←[[m00076]]
- [1999-12-20→] Whether Stinson himself speaks is not in the mail; per Parsons, Stinson put Parsons forward. ←[[m00076]]
