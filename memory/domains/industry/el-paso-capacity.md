# El Paso firm capacity to California (Dec 1999)

- [1999-12-16] Lorna Brennan's ET&S memo (forwarded FYI by Kimberly Watson): a $38M one-year bid from 1 Jan topped El Paso's $30M negotiated deal for ~1.2 Bcf/d firm to the California border; the original party (reportedly Duke; Dynegy said it lost round one) may match. A source put mark-to-market value at $20-25M. SoCal border/San Juan spread fell from 29 to ~18 cents on storage withdrawals and cold. ←[[m00044]]
- [1999-12-22] Enerfax 21 Dec (forwarded by the owner to his AOL): after an open season El Paso contracted the 1.2 Bcf/d to Enron North America for $38M plus upside, one year; 2000 capacity revenue $45.5M incl. 99.3 MMcf/d to Williams, up 30% on the prior two-year average. Same issue: Kinder Morgan Energy Partners bought Columbia's third of Trailblazer Pipeline ($38M); Enron owns a third. ←[[m00087]]
