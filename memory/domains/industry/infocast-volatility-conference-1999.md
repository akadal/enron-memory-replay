# INFOCAST volatility conference panel, Houston, Dec 1999

- [1999-12-30] The owner thanked Todd Strauss (PHB Hagler Bailly) for the invitation to the panel on the state of US electricity markets at the INFOCAST volatility conference in Houston, held about ten days before Strauss's note of 1999-12-20 (owner, verbatim: "It was a real pleasure to join you and other leading professionals"). Strauss had praised his analysis of current electricity markets and trends. ←[[m00122]]
