# Market outlooks for 2000

- [2000-01-20] CERA alert "Demand Overtakes Hydro in 2000": western gas burn for power up 600 MMcf/d on average (1,200 in Q3); gas sets the floor for on-peak western power; narrow differentials, higher prices and volatility as California margins tighten; Topock ~$0.25 over Henry Hub in Q3 2000 (0.05 in Q3 1999). Owner to Mike Roberts (cc Ronnie Chahal): "An area to focus on." ←[[m00406]]
- [2000-01-21] Owner to Rick Buy and Ted Murphy: "One more thing to watch. California power prices next summer" — spikes possible from "bad hydro conditions in California, growing demand for power"; 24 Jan forwarded to Mike Roberts: "Please, stay on top of this development." ←[[m00417]] ←[[m00426]]
- [2000-01-20] Poten & Partners (George Gail, via Doug Leach and Margaret Carson, fyi): WTI back to $19-22 by 2Q 2000 (Brent $18-20), emotion-driven; weak cracks in 2000; Gulf naphtha term premiums ($8-11/t over Platt's AG); Koch closed its Rotterdam splitter; US demand growth held to 1%. ←[[m00407]]
- [2000-02-24] Leppard (FT piece): APX traders suspect some bid up prices to sway bilateral deals; owner sent it "FYI" to Mark Haedicke and Ted Murphy. ←[[m00756]]
