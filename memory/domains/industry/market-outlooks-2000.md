- [2000-01-21] Owner to Rick Buy and Ted Murphy: "One more thing to watch. California power prices next summer" — spikes possible from "bad hydro conditions in California, growing demand for power"; 24 Jan forwarded to Mike Roberts: "Please, stay on top of this development." ←[[m00417]] ←[[m00426]]
- [2000-06-19] To Whalley, Nowlan, Robert Johnston and Tholan: Iraq seeks US concessions and "threatens to shut down production as of Oct 1 (just in time for the US elections)"; source Phil Verlaeger, "very good but excessively concerned with Iraqi machinations"; on reports (Friedman, NYT) of massive Iraqi oil futures trading: "I personally discount this info" (markets too big and transparent; officials may trade on the side). ←[[m02017]]
- [2000-10-13] CERA gas and power roundtable (Margaret Carson's summary): the owner says Texas on-peak forwards ignore 5 GW of new capacity and should be very soft next summer unless 105 F returns, New England a year behind; CERA: 240,000 MW planned 2000-05 vs 13-15,000 MW a year needed (11% built in 3 years); gas short, prices high; California market broken, US peak demand +2.6% in 2001. ←[[m03637]]
