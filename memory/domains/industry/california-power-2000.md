# California power market: caps and FERC remedies, 2000

Earlier outside views (California warning): memory/domains/industry/market-outlooks-2000.md.

- [2000-11-01] Gorny forwards Tim Belden's 27 Oct memo: the CAISO Board voted 13-10 (26 Oct) for caps from 3 Nov or later = NYMEX last-3-day settlement x heat rate by load (<25 GW 10,775 up to 35-40 GW 27,225; >40 GW flat $250/MWh), demand bids exempt; FERC's delegation ends 15 Nov (extension to rule by 18 Nov); Belden's team mapped monthly maximum prices against 27 Oct forwards; spreadsheet not to leave Enron. ←[[m03961]]
- [2000-11-01] FERC's staff report (Part I, U.S. Bulk Power Markets; EEI's Tonya Queen, Jack Cashin): California's market structure 'seriously flawed'; proposed: no mandatory PX buy/sell, 95% scheduled day-ahead, independent ISO/PX boards, a temporary $150/MWh soft cap with weekly reporting, refund exposure from 2 Oct 2000, no retroactive refunds; comments by 22 Nov, conference 9 Nov; owner takes the alert to AOL. ←[[m03957]] ←[[m03963]]
