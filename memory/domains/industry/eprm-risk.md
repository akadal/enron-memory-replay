# RISK / EPRM conferences, courses and Risk Books

- [1999-12-10→] Helen Evans (helen@risk.co.uk) produces EPRM conferences and courses at RISK and wants to broaden the speaker base. ←[[m00002]]
- [1999-12-10] She asked the owner for a new Enron speaker for an EPRM Monte Carlo training course; the owner forwarded it to Stinson Gibner and "wholeheartedly" recommended him. ←[[m00002]] ←[[m00003]]
- [1999-12-20] Benjamin Parsons (LON/ECT) was invited by Angela Adedeji (aadedeji@risk.co.uk) to speak at European Energy 2000 (Amsterdam, 3-4 Apr) and put forward by Stinson for the EPRM Monte Carlo event a week later; he asked the owner whether to do both (similar topics to his earlier EPRM talk, little preparation) or prioritise. ←[[m00076]]
- [1999-12-30] Shan Millie (Risk Books, 17 Dec): Rob left Risk Books to edit Risk Management Operations; energy titles, especially MEPR2, got excellent reviews at contingencyanalysis.com; Risk Books wants a Utility Risk title next year and asked the owner's views; offered coffee when he is in London. Owner forwarded it to his AOL. ←[[m00123]]
