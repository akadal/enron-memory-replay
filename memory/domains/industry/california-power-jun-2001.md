# California briefings from 30 May 2001

Before: memory/domains/industry/california-power-may-2001.md.

- [2001-05-30] Jennifer Thome (5-3550, via Shirley) asks when and where the owner picked up his California crisis handouts. ←[[m08044]]
- [2001-06-01] Kristin Walsh to Lavorato and Kitchen (owner cc, copied to AOL): State Treasurer Angelides's 'Plan Z' (Sen. Burton backs it; early, no fast track): a California Public Power Authority issues bonds, builds plants, buys transmission, perhaps SoCal Edison for $3.5B (less than it owes); 5-15% reserve over peak in five years, public replacing private generation; $5B revenue bonds on top of $12B rate bonds; a further 45% residential and commercial rate rise on top of this year's 50-60%; open: shareholder dilution, creditor haircuts. ←[[m08170]] ←[[m08183]]
- [2001-06-05] Kristin Walsh: Davis's MOU to buy SoCal Edison's grid is essentially dead (CPUC to adjourn Friday without voting; Edison warns of creditor bankruptcy risk from 8 Jun); the state spends $79M a day, will spend all $12B before the August bonds; 3,500 MW of QFs offline; H.R. 1647 vote 6 Jun, Waxman's caps not expected to pass. ←[[m08326]]
- [2001-06-05] PBS Frontline 'Blackout' (California, the Houston players, Ken Lay interviewed) airs 5 Jun 9 pm, repeat 12 Jun (Mandelker's note); owner saves the link. ←[[m08332]] ←[[m08286]]
- [2001-06-05] Jan Johnson sends Evar Nering's NYT op-ed 'The Mirage of a Growing Fuel Supply': at 5% growth a 100-year supply lasts ~36 years; supply-side policy cannot work. ←[[m08298]]
