# Neptune, Alberta Energy MBA and Norsk Hydro, Jun 2001

- [2001-06-06] Neptune (Edward Krapels, ESAI; in dialogue with Paul Joskow): owner to Lance Cunningham: "Who would be the right person on the trading desk to take a look at it?" Lance had just talked with Walter Coffer about an undersea project Enron may do to bring Canadian power to New England. ←[[m08377]] ←[[m08378]]
- [2001-06-06] Owner to Kevin Presto: Neptune is "a merchant transmission DC line" (sub-sea, Eastern Canada and Maine to New York City, Boston, Connecticut and New Jersey shores). Presto read of it in MW Daily and Bloomberg; thinks it will be a good project. ←[[m08385]] ←[[m08387]]
- [2001-06-08] Joseph Doucet (University of Alberta), met at a conference the week before, sends Energy MBA materials; owner forwards them with his recommendation to Billy Lemmons, "who heads our Analyst / Associate program". ←[[m08471]]
- [2001-06-07] Norsk Hydro visit evaluations (Carrie Robert, Corp Marketing Services): visitors very satisfied. ←[[m08453]]
