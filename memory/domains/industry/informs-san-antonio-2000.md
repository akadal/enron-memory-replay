- [2000-10-01] Owner's abstract: "The last three years were characterized by exceptionally high volatility of the power prices in the US markets."; the standard measure (annualized std of log returns) "may not fit well electricity prices"; causes: market design flaws allowing market power abuse, unequal transmission access, temporary generation shortages; some "may be a necessary price to pay for increased market efficiency and expanded customer choice". Deng proposes 'Current Challenges in Modeling Power Price Volatility' (Dr. Vince Kaminski, Head of Quantitative Research, Enron Capital & Trade Resources); owner: "The title is fine.", ending revised: some factors "may reflect the flaws in power pools design and incomplete transition to fully deregulated markets". ←[[m03415]] ←[[m03416]] ←[[m03417]]
