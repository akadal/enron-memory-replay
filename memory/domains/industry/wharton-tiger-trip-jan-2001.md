# Wharton Tiger Team Houston trip and collaborative research

Kick-off, team split and Kunreuther's proposal: memory/domains/industry/wharton-visit-oct-2000.md.

- [2000-12-18] Christie Patrick: about 18 students on 3 Enron projects ("Tiger Program") arrive Thu 18 Jan, dinner at Churrasco's with the owner, Vasant Shanbhogue (not "Vance"), Jeff Shankman; Fri 19 Jan tour and unit meetings, home Friday evening; Enron pays Thursday hotel and transport; Melinda McCarty arranges; Heather Thorne and Jack Rejtman stay to Sunday at their own cost. ←[[m04899]] ←[[m04902]] ←[[m04903]]
- [2000-12-19] All at the Warwick by Herman Park ($115; downtown sold out), dinner 7 pm; tour split in two (SMU Energy Club of 19 tours the same day); Enron 1 Wholesale, Enron 2 Future shape of energy markets, Enron 3 Retail; TA Heather Thorne; Thorne, Rejtman, Deepa Mallik and Donna Piazze leave Sun 21 Jan, Dennis Feerick Sat; McCarty faxes the hotel memo. ←[[m04956]] ←[[m04963]]
- [2000-12-18] Shanbhogue's draft for the owner to edit: research on how much risk an enterprise can take vs chooses to take given capital and reporting structure (Enron as a hierarchy of companies), how to represent unit risks (volatility, catastrophic, liquidity), and the optimal insurance at unit and enterprise level. ←[[m04907]]
- [2000-12-22] The owner sends his home address (10 Snowbird, The Woodlands) to Tom Piazze at Wharton. ←[[m05007]]
