# Real options as a "fad": the Economist debate

Faiz's visit to the owner: memory/domains/industry/visitors-2000.md.

- [2000-04-25] Faiz circulated The Economist's "Fading Fads" (a Bain survey casting real options analysis as a fad): not a fad, though with Tim Luehrman's "language" and "practical application" issues; she proposes a counter-point article. List: PwC (Adam Borison, Gardner Walkup), SDG (J. Matheson), Martha Amram, Tom Copeland, Stern Stewart, Duke, Maryland, Bocconi, Cyprus (Trigeorgis). ←[[m01487]] ←[[m01517]]
- [2000-04-26] Gordon Sick (Calgary; Journal of Finance book reviews): Bain's graphs look flat and insignificant; real options rest on a sound model and interest keeps growing. ←[[m01517]]
- [2000-04-27] John McCormack (Stern Stewart): Bain is a strategy firm with very limited capacity for serious quantitative analysis. ←[[m01547]]
- [2000-04-28] Alberto Micalizzi (SDA Bocconi): a Bain senior partner wrote in Italy's largest financial paper that no recent methodology can value high-tech firms. ←[[m01556]]
