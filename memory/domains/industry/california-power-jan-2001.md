- [2001-05-22] Walsh (confidential, to Lavorato and Kitchen, cc owner): Treasurer Phil Angelides told a source SoCal will go bankrupt and the Davis/SoCal MOU is dead; the $12B bond (all the market can take; assumes $400/MWh peak) won't cover summer power, true cost $18-24B; the new California Public Power Authority will build to a 10-15% cushion; the state 5-10% short all summer. ←[[m07969]]
- [2001-06-01] Kristin Walsh's update (to Lavorato and Kitchen; owner cc, copies to AOL): Angelides, with Sen. Burton, is developing an early 'Plan Z', not fast-tracked: a California Public Power Authority issues bonds, builds plants, buys transmission, maybe SoCal Edison for $3.5B; 5-15% reserve over peak within five years; $5B revenue bonds on top of $12B rate bonds; a further 45% rate rise on top of this year's 50-60%; open: shareholder dilution, creditor haircuts. ←[[m08170]] ←[[m08183]]
