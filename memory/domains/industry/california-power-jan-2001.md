# California power crisis briefings (Jan 2001)

2000 background (CAISO caps, FERC remedies, class action): memory/domains/industry/california-power-2000.md.

- [2001-01-16] Robert Johnston (cc Scott Tholan) adds the owner to his daily updates after Kristin Walsh's tip; owner: "Absolutely." "This is a critical situation." 15 Jan: Davis's aides offer ~$50-55/MWh for state-bought power vs suppliers' ~$75; Summers suggested an auction, the state prefers negotiated long-term contracts backed by state credit; 10% rate rise beyond 90 days; $12bn back debt over a 90-day forbearance, no direct state money; an audit says half the $9-12bn is owed to the utilities' own affiliates; plant takeover threatened. ←[[m05330]] ←[[m05340]]
- [2001-01-17] Johnston (list incl. Hickerson, Shankman, Richard Shapiro, Tholan, Steffes): bankruptcy ever likelier, SCE first (suspended $230M bond, $151M renewables, $215M PX payments; 30-day cure); AB 1X (5.5 cents/kWh long-term) passed the assembly; BofA $215M and J.P. Morgan $202M of $12bn bank loans; PG&E $500M cash vs $580M to the ISO 1 Feb, $431M to the PX 15 Feb. Bush team keeps Clinton's 'broker' role (O'Neill: the Governor is the first line). The AG reviews the profiteering statute; generators want short cure periods to limit Ch.11 losses. ←[[m05358]] ←[[m05365]] ←[[m05368]]
- [2001-01-19] Kristin Walsh (to Lavorato, McConnell): SB-7X lets DWR buy power uncapped to 2 Feb ($400M, maybe $1bn); AB-1X likely amended; SB-6X power authority with bond and eminent-domain powers; SCE defaulted $215M to the PX, PG&E expects suspension; Bush team opposes caps, helps split off PG&E's gas business (electric into Ch.11); bankruptcy most likely. Johnston x39934, Walsh x39510. ←[[m05418]] ←[[m05423]]
