- [2001-05-07] Walsh: the Assembly votes on the bond bill today, likely by simple majority only, so no effect for 90 days and no bridge loan; without a rescue SoCal may file voluntary bankruptcy; the state spends ~$70M a day on power. ←[[m07753]]
- [2001-05-15] Jennifer Thome (Government Affairs) briefed the owner for that week's conference: "The materials were very useful and helped me to present better the position of Enron on  the issue."; he owes her other parties' papers. ←[[m07874]] ←[[m07890]]
- [2001-05-22] Walsh (confidential, to Lavorato and Kitchen, cc owner): Treasurer Phil Angelides told a source SoCal will go bankrupt and the Davis/SoCal MOU is dead; the $12B bond (all the market can take; assumes $400/MWh peak) won't cover summer power, true cost $18-24B; the new California Public Power Authority will build to a 10-15% cushion; the state 5-10% short all summer. ←[[m07969]]
