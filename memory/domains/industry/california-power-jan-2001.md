# California power crisis briefings (Jan 2001)

Detail ←[[california-power-jan-2001-2001-01-w04]] ←[[california-power-jan-2001-2001-02-w05]] ←[[california-power-jan-2001-2001-03-w10]]

2000 background (CAISO caps, FERC remedies, class action): memory/domains/industry/california-power-2000.md.

- [2001-01-26] Walsh (25 Jan): ~40 bids averaging 6.9 cents (state cap 7.4, Davis 5.5), Freeman negotiates; no state credit for DWR; warrants-for-loans bailout; deadlines 2 Feb (funds out), 7 Feb (DOE order ends), 13 Feb (Edison forbearance). Johnston: AB18X focal; Davis wants the parents to absorb 43%+ of the debt; with peak, bids ~9.5 cents. Owner copies both to AOL. ←[[m05587]] ←[[m05588]]
- [2001-01-31] Walsh: first long-term contracts maybe today; bankruptcy threat fading (rate pass-through, revenue bonds; creditor standstill to 13 Feb); Judge Lew's rate order stayed to 14 Feb ($12.7bn relief, bills +30-40%); CPUC audits: SCE sent ~$5bn to its parent, PG&E ~$4.7bn; DWR has spent its $400M, $45M a day on spot. ←[[m05698]]
- [2001-03-06] Walsh (to Lavorato, Kitchen): with no comprehensive deal by 9 Apr PG&E bankruptcy risk rises (one-day opt-outs in long-term contracts); PG&E wants ~4x book (~$10bn) for its grid, the legislature caps 2.3x; no sale unless freed of buyer-of-last-resort duty; of Davis's 8,800 MW only 6,000 MW serve this summer; permanent 10% rate rise plus 10% in 2002; FERC must approve a grid purchase (Massey, Hebert opposed). ←[[m06104]]
