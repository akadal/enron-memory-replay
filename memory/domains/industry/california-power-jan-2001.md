# California power crisis briefings (Jan 2001)

Detail ←[[california-power-jan-2001-2001-01-w04]]

2000 background (CAISO caps, FERC remedies, class action): memory/domains/industry/california-power-2000.md.

- [2001-01-19] Kristin Walsh (to Lavorato, McConnell): SB-7X lets DWR buy power uncapped to 2 Feb ($400M, maybe $1bn); AB-1X likely amended; SB-6X power authority with bond and eminent-domain powers; SCE defaulted $215M to the PX, PG&E expects suspension; Bush team opposes caps, helps split off PG&E's gas business (electric into Ch.11); bankruptcy most likely. Johnston x39934, Walsh x39510. ←[[m05418]] ←[[m05423]]
- [2001-01-22] Martin (Baylor) sends a former student's note, 'California is worth 50 basis points'. Walsh: AB1X's 5.5 cent cap "a fantasy" (Keeley); utility hydro or transmission for rate rises; long-term bids Wednesday (5.5-8 cents); state on credit watch; PG&E's utility sent ~$5B to the parent in 3 years; Davis would cover ~$4B of the $12B debt. ←[[m05466]] ←[[m05475]]
- [2001-01-26] Walsh (25 Jan): ~40 bids averaging 6.9 cents (state cap 7.4, Davis 5.5), Freeman negotiates; no state credit for DWR; warrants-for-loans bailout; deadlines 2 Feb (funds out), 7 Feb (DOE order ends), 13 Feb (Edison forbearance). Johnston: AB18X focal; Davis wants the parents to absorb 43%+ of the debt; with peak, bids ~9.5 cents. Owner copies both to AOL. ←[[m05587]] ←[[m05588]]
- [2001-01-26] Schilmoeller to Jason Sokolov: historical real-time prices are CalISO's (RDI PowerDat, LIM, CalISO site), not CalPX; Heizenrader's Westdesk Fundamentals can help. ←[[m05597]]
