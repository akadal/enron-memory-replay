- [2001-06-05] Walsh: Davis's MOU to buy Edison's grid is essentially dead, the CPUC to adjourn Friday without voting; Edison warns of a creditor-induced bankruptcy from 8 Jun; the state spends ~$79M a day, all $12B gone before the August bond auction; 3,500 MW of QFs offline for cash; Plan Z rate bill stalled; H.R. 1647 vote 6 Jun, Waxman's caps not expected to pass. ←[[m08326]]

"Very interesting."; 6 Jun to Mike Roberts: "Great appearance on Frontline." ←[[m08286]] ←[[m08332]] ←[[m08335]] ←[[m08337]] ←[[m08338]] ←[[m08371]]
- [2001-06-06] Walsh's 6/5 update: Sen. Polanco introduced the Davis-Edison MOU as SB 78XX, too little support to pass; the CPUC puts two needed MOU provisions on next week's agenda, support weak; House Energy and Commerce drops California relief over Western price caps. ←[[m08404]]
- [2001-06-08] Walsh's 6/8 update: the CPUC delays its vote on paying QFs 15% of undercollected debt (a possible bankruptcy 'preference payment', unclear implementation); paying QFs but not others could push non-QF creditors to an involuntary SoCal bankruptcy; many claims on SoCal's and PG&E's cash; owner copies it to AOL. ←[[m08518]] ←[[m08524]]
