# IIR energy conference, Washington DC, 19-20 June 2000

- [2000-01-10] Mark Thabet (VP, Energy & Utilities Division, IIR) had written on 6 Jan inviting the owner to the faculty, offering to add the topic "Forecasting and Measuring your Exposures to Energy Price Risk", and asking for colleague recommendations. The owner declined (owner, verbatim: "due to a scheduling conflict I cannot speak at this conference."); the capture note said the decision was unstated, the source text shows the decline. ←[[m00208]]
