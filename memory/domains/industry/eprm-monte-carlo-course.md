# EPRM (RISK) Monte Carlo training course — Enron speaker request

Helen Evans (Producer, EPRM Conferences & Courses, risk.co.uk) is producing a training course on
the Monte Carlo technique and wants to broaden EPRM's speaker base.

- [1999-12-10] Evans's request of Dec 6 asked the owner to recommend someone new from Enron to speak on Monte Carlo. ←[[m00002]] ←[[m00003]]
- [1999-12-10] The owner forwarded it to [[stinson-gibner]] ("This is the request I got from RISK") and told Evans he "can wholeheartedly recommend" Stinson. ←[[m00002]] ←[[m00003]]
- [1999-12-14] Evans thanked the owner for his help. ←[[m00018]]
