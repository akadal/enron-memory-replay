# EPRM (RISK) Monte Carlo training course — Enron speakers

Helen Evans (Producer, EPRM Conferences & Courses, risk.co.uk) is producing a Monte Carlo training
course and wants to broaden EPRM's speaker base.

- [1999-12-10] Evans asked the owner (Dec 6) to recommend someone new from Enron; he forwarded it to [[stinson-gibner]] and told Evans he "can wholeheartedly recommend" Stinson. ←[[m00002]] ←[[m00003]]
- [1999-12-14] Evans thanked the owner. ←[[m00018]]
- [1999-12-20] Benjamin Parsons (Enron London) says Stinson put his name forward for the course, about a week after European Energy 2000 (Amsterdam, 3–4 April), where Angela Adedeji invited him to speak (power price modelling or VaR seminars). Both talks reuse his earlier EPRM material; he asked the owner whether to do both or prioritise. No reply seen. ←[[m00076]]
