# El Paso firm capacity open season, Dec 1999

- [1999-12-16] Kimberly Watson (ET&S) sent the owner a Daily GPI item: a ~$30M negotiated deal for ~1.2 Bcf/d of El Paso firm capacity to the California border was topped by a $38M one-year bid from 1 Jan; Duke or Dynegy guessed as bidder. SoCal border/San Juan basis fell from 29c to ~18c. ←[[m00044]] ←[[m00048]]
- [1999-12-22] Enerfax (forwarded by Bob Brooks, 12/21): El Paso contracted its remaining 1.2 Bcf/d of firm capacity to Enron North America for $38M plus upside, one-year term from 2000. ←[[m00087]]
- [1999-12-22] Same report: Kinder Morgan Energy Partners bought Columbia Energy Group's one-third of Trailblazer Pipeline for $38M; Enron owns another third. ←[[m00087]]
- [2000-02-04] Deal terminated (Lorna Brennan via Kimberly Watson): after a mid-January FERC order limited delivery rights, Enron's 27 Jan letter sought to exit Block II (614 MMcf/d); both sides then ended all three blocks effective 31 Jan. El Paso planned an open season 7–14 Feb. Owner forwarded it to AOL. ←[[m00552]]
