# El Paso firm capacity open season, Dec 1999

- [1999-12-16] Kimberly Watson (ET&S) sent the owner an FYI Daily GPI item (by Lorna Brennan): a ~$30M negotiated deal for ~1.2 Bcf/d of El Paso firm capacity to the California border was topped by a $38M one-year bid from 1 Jan; the original party could match until the afternoon of 16 Dec. Duke or Dynegy guessed as bidder (Dynegy says it lost to Duke in round one). SoCal border/San Juan basis fell from a 29c December index to ~18c. ←[[m00044]] ←[[m00048]]
