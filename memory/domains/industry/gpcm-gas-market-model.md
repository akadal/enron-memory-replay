# GPCM gas market model (RBAC, Robert Brooks)

- [1999-12-23] Robert Brooks (RBAC) year-end letter to GPCM licensees, received by the owner: licensees doubled to 10 in 1999; RDI (Mike Farina) supplies pipeline/storage data and GASdat interfaces. ←[[m00099]]
- [1999-12-23] 2000 plans: move to Office 2000 / Windows 2000; more flexible transportation-zone price curves; modelling future FT contracting; upgraded Database Comparison with automatic updates; a licensee website (gpcm.net); licensee interviews within 30–60 days. ←[[m00099]]
- [2000-01-20] Brooks flagged Skipping Stone's capacity release data service (capacitycenter.com; pipeline EBB release postings as biddable deals); owner asked [[clayton-vernon]] to evaluate it for the traders. ←[[m00404]]
