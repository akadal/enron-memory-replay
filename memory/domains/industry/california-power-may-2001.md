# California power crisis briefings, May 2001

Jan-Apr 2001: memory/domains/industry/california-power-jan-2001.md. Detail ←[[california-power-may-2001-2001-05-w20]]

- [2001-05-04] Walsh (to Lavorato, Kitchen): no vote on the bond issuance and bridge loan authorization is expected by the 8 May deadline; both caucuses turning on Governor Davis; some long-term contracts reportedly need the bonds by 1 Jul or the state is in breach. ←[[m07671]] ←[[m07704]]
- [2001-05-04] SCE bankruptcy triggers: legislative rejection of the SCE-Davis MOU (deadline 15 Aug; SCE signed, the state has not) or Senator Bowen's language paying DWR before the utilities, possibly moved into AB 31X. The Senate passed SB 6X: $5B for a California Consumer Power and Conservation Authority, stalled until payment for power is settled; Republicans oppose. ←[[m07671]] ←[[m07704]]
- [2001-05-07] Walsh: the Assembly votes today on the bond bill, likely only by simple majority, so no effect for 90 days and no bridge loan; barring a rescue, SoCal could file voluntary bankruptcy while awaiting state help (transmission purchase); the state spends about $70M a day on power; Davis and Angelides press Republicans. ←[[m07753]]
- [2001-05-15] Jennifer Thome (Government Affairs)'s briefing for his conference: "The materials were very useful and helped me to present better the position of Enron on  the issue."; he owes her others' 'California solutions'. 16 May, to Thome (Fletcher 2000): "No, Fordham University in my case." ←[[m07874]] ←[[m07890]]
