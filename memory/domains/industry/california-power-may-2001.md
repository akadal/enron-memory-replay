# California power crisis briefings, May 2001

Jan-Apr 2001: memory/domains/industry/california-power-jan-2001.md. Detail ←[[california-power-may-2001-2001-05-w20]] ←[[california-power-may-2001-2001-05-w21]] (4 May: no vote by the 8 May deadline, SCE bankruptcy triggers, SB 6X)

- [2001-05-07] Walsh: the Assembly votes today on the bond bill, likely only by simple majority, so no effect for 90 days and no bridge loan; barring a rescue, SoCal could file voluntary bankruptcy while awaiting state help (transmission purchase); the state spends about $70M a day on power; Davis and Angelides press Republicans. ←[[m07753]]
- [2001-05-15] Jennifer Thome (Government Affairs)'s briefing for his conference: "The materials were very useful and helped me to present better the position of Enron on  the issue."; he owes her others' 'California solutions'. 16 May, to Thome (Fletcher 2000): "No, Fordham University in my case." ←[[m07874]] ←[[m07890]]
- [2001-05-22] Walsh (confidential, to Lavorato, Kitchen, cc the owner): a source met Treasurer Angelides: SoCal will go bankrupt, the Davis/SoCal MOU is dead; the $12B bond is all the market can take but summer power needs $18-24B (plan assumes $400/MWh peak, hence Comptroller Connell's higher estimates); the new Public Power Authority builds to a 10-15% cushion; California 5-10% short all summer. ←[[m07969]]
