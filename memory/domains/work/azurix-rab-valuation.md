# Azurix: UK water RAB multiples and deal valuation (Aug 2000)

- [2000-08-22] Michael Anderson (Azurix) sends Keith Harris's memo: UK water company market values since privatisation averaged 1.1x RAB, skewed by c1.5x from mid-1997 to end-1998; a premium appears only when political uncertainty is gone and companies can beat the regulatory deal, and those days are gone; CSFB works on CustomerCo (about 7 energy companies willing; a more modest fee proposal awaited). The owner forwards it to Robert Lee (graph from David Kitchener, 3 Aug). ←[[m02939]] ←[[m02941]]
- [2000-08-23] Zimin Lu and Bob Lee value the "Azuix" deal with the price sample simulator under different trend assumptions; results to the owner and Gibner by the end of the day. ←[[m02961]]
- [2000-08-24] Anderson: Keith Harris (CFO, Wessex Water) confirms Wessex originated the data; the graph starts at an index of 100% but the initial RAB multiple was about 80%, so the line drops 20 points (the late-98 high nears the 1.3x Anderson had targeted). Owner (FYI to Lee, Leppard, Gibner, Lu): "We shall have the results for you by tomorrow morning."; Lu: the simulation already uses the 80% start. ←[[m02976]] ←[[m02978]] ←[[m02980]]
