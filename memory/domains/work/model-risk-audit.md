# Model risk audit (Arthur Andersen Model Development Review, 1999)

Detail ←[[model-risk-audit-2000-12-w48]] ←[[model-risk-audit-2000-12-w51]] ←[[model-risk-audit-2001-01-w04]] ←[[model-risk-audit-2001-04-w14]] ←[[model-risk-audit-2001-04-w15b]]

Auditors: Derek Claybrook (Arthur Andersen), Patty Grutzmacher, Andrew Schuleman. ←[[m00126]] ←[[m00127]]

- [1999-12-30] Draft Model Development Report (Schuleman, for the 1 Oct Controller's Meeting). Owner (3-4 Oct): model risk is "really critical to Enron"; its main source is that no unit or person is empowered to approve, audit and monitor models, "The popular perception is that this is done by the Research Group, but this could not be further form the truth"; split misuse into wrong inputs (TVA) and wrong model; cite the Andersen and Duffie audits "initiated at our insistence"; leaving Risk Analytics and IT out makes the findings "highly inaccurate". ←[[m00125]] ←[[m00126]]
- [2001-04-02] Owner to Duffie on his audit of Enron's credit, VaR and option pricing models: "I am beating up on my group to accelerate preparation of documentation for the audit." 4 Apr Iris Mack compiles it; Ben Parsons sends the Enron Credit model docs (FMC, pricing, asset swap, placement, neural net, credit VaR, CDOs, correlations; FMC doc dated, ratings placement undocumented); owner could not open them. ←[[m06489]] ←[[m06512]] ←[[m06513]]
- [2001-04-12] Iris Mack is on the owner's calendar Tue 17 Apr, 2 pm, for the Duffie project; she forwards Tomas Valnek and Ben Parsons' parent-subsidiary model note (10 Apr), which the owner copies to AOL. ←[[m06801]] ←[[m06820]]
