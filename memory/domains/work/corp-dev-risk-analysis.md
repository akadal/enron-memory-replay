# Corporate Development's volatility, capital and P&L analysis

Earlier liquidity and capital work: memory/domains/work/var-credit-system-2001.md.

- [2001-12-04] Jayshree Desai and Mark Wadlington (Corporate Development) ask for volatilities per commodity (power, gas; crude, aluminium, copper via strip proxies; Treasuries, S&P 500, currencies), a cross-correlation matrix, power and gas vol vs Enron's trading earnings, ROVAR vs banks and hedge funds, economic capital vs cash for a given VaR; owner passes it to Lew, Raymond, Bharati, Tamarchenko, Shanbhogue; Sarah Taylor adds the fixed income market's size. ←[[m10197]] ←[[m10207]]
- [2001-12-04] Jaesoo Lew (RMS curve-shift P&L, prompt-month vols, Aug 2000-Aug 2001): power vol relates to VaR but hardly to P&L; gas vol to VaR more weakly, to P&L little; Bharati sends 'Correlations and vols - A comparison'. ←[[m10204]] ←[[m10206]] ←[[m10205]]
- [2001-12-06] Rabi De's margin capital simulation for Desai (with Rajan, Tamarchenko, Shanbhogue): gas flat at $3.0/MMBtu, 20 counterparties picked by MTM, one-month capital and VaR; margin calls = exposure times 0 (AAA) to 1 (D); the trend with volume holds, the level depends on the simulated VaR. ←[[m10221]]
- [2001-12-07] David Port's rough P&L breakout: position-report P&L minus big originations (peaker sales) and 'mistakes' (EES's $700MM loss), minus curve-shift P&L, leaves new-deal and realised P&L; 2000 was the prop trading year, 2001 mainly new deals; reserve movements poison it all. ←[[m10240]]
