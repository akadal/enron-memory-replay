# Enron Credit from 21 May 2001

Before: memory/domains/work/enron-credit-tensions-may-2001.md (Iris's London report, Albanis's ultimatum).

- [2001-05-21] Bryan Seyfried to ECT Enron Credit (cc the owner, Vasant, Iris, Dhar, Danilov, Leppard): George Albanis has left, effective immediately; owner forwards it to Jeff Kinneman. ←[[m07949]]
- [2001-05-25] Iris: the client (Scott Salmon's group) fired one of its two quants (she recalls the owner's warning against disparaging clients). Salmon: in her report to Professor Duffie ask his view on testing models with poor out-of-sample sets, a weak area; the testbed is in place, recalibration starts, stress testing after it worries him; talk Tue after Mike's D&B update. ←[[m08001]]
- [2001-05-25] Iris to Mark (k.wilson): meet Mark Braemer and the developers on the subjective inputs of his credit models (out this summer); Wilson lets her group use his data (6th floor files) to try Moody's RiskCalc (calibrated for US, Canada, Australia), Kyriakos Frantzeskakis to get access Tue; owner copies it to AOL. ←[[m08003]] ←[[m08009]]
- [2001-05-26] Iris to Scott Salmon: the London call (owner, Vasant, Amitava, Iris) raised issues with the public firm models and their data, most already covered by Albanis's Dec 2000 EnronCredit.com proposal (Movement Model, Phase IV) and his 25/01/2001 review (sent 11 May, resent); Jeff Kinneman will meet the owner and Bryan to repair them; future concerns go to Kinneman, the owner and Bryan. ←[[m08020]]
