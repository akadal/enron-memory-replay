# Enron India: Dabhol power sale and power trading

Detail ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2001-01-w02]]

Team: Sandeep Kohli with Rajesh Sivaraman, Vivek Kejriwal, Shubh Shrivastava, Anshuman Srivastav (same team as memory/domains/work/naphtha-caps-india.md). ←[[m00100]] Detail (m-t-m, press, Oct 2000 Houston training) ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2000-02-w06]] … ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2000-10-w41]] ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2000-12-w50]] ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2000-12-w51]]

- [1999-12-25] Kohli asked the owner and Vasant Shanbhogue to structure a sale of Dabhol power to another state: firm peak-period power (50 MW, Phase I) plus monsoon infirm power, tariff tracking the PPA. ←[[m00100]]
- [2000-12-21] LNG hedging (Anshuman's draft with Krishnarao, to Hughes): volumetric option (call on 100% of volume, put sold on 50%) kept, the ratio derivative rejected by traders; Mar 2001 cost-neutral: call $26, put $25.15; work with Global Markets on what MSEB would buy; owner copies it to AOL. ←[[m04994]] ←[[m05035]]
- [2001-01-14] Kohli's points for Wade Cline's meeting with N.K. Singh (Prime Minister's office): seek Central government intervention and a joint Central/State/SEB negotiating group, Enron will not give up value; Phase I tariff Rs 4.02/kWh at 90% despatch, Rs 3.30-3.70 with Phase II (1440 MW, end-2001); MSEB 45-60 days late, DPC outflows up to Rs 450 Cr/month vs MSEB collections ~Rs 900 Cr; fixes: absorb Phase II across the Western Region grid (42,000 MW), restructure guarantees, IOC/GAIL equity, back-ended tariffs. ←[[m05327]]
