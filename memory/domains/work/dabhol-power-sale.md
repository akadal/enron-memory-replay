Team: Sandeep Kohli with Rajesh Sivaraman, Vivek Kejriwal, Shubh Shrivastava, Anshuman Srivastav (same team as memory/domains/work/naphtha-caps-india.md). ←[[m00100]] Detail (m-t-m, press, Oct 2000 Houston training) ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2000-02-w06]] … ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2000-10-w41]] ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2000-12-w50]] ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2000-12-w51]]

- [2001-01-19] Press (18 Jan): DPC idle since early January after MSEB backed down fully; MSEB owes over Rs 300 crore for Nov-Dec; no sales-tax waiver on 1.2 Mt of IOC naphtha (Rs 11,050/t vs Glencore's 10,050), DPC passes the 4% on; Prabhu: no proposal for PTC to buy the surplus; MSEB declined its 15% Phase II stake. ←[[m05425]]
- [2001-01-29] Kohli proposes a trading company brokering short-term power for MSEB (MSEB stays liable) to lift Dabhol despatch, proceeds into an escrow paying DPC bills; Seethayya (to Wade Cline, McGregor): the Group of Ministers backs up to 100% foreign equity in power trading, the Union Cabinet decides (Power and Industry Ministers supportive). ←[[m05645]]
- [2001-02-06] Seethayya: Union Cabinet allows up to 100% foreign equity in power trading by the automatic route, without the Draft Electricity Bill 2001 (maybe hurried as DPC invokes the State Guarantee); FIPB to approve Enron LLC or send it the automatic route; Kohli credits Ramu, Mehta, Jane, Heidi, Seethayya. ←[[m05816]]
