# Dabhol Phase I firm power sale (Enron India)

Team: Sandeep Kohli with Rajesh Sivaraman, Vivek Kejriwal, Shubh Shrivastava, Anshuman Srivastav (same team as memory/domains/work/naphtha-caps-india.md). ←[[m00100]] Detail ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2000-02-w06]] ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2000-02-w08]]

- [1999-12-25] Kohli asked the owner and Vasant Shanbhogue to structure a sale of Dabhol power to another state: firm peak-period power (50 MW, Phase I) plus monsoon infirm power, tariff tracking the PPA. ←[[m00100]]
- [2000-01-05] Krishnarao took it on: in Bombay with Kohli 10-14 Jan, costs invoiced to Dabhol Power Co. ←[[m00144]] ←[[m00186]] ←[[m00431]]
- [2000-01-20] (?) "Krishna", in the Bombay office, urgently asked Harry for a Rupee/US$ forward curve to at least 2005 for the deal. Status quo: the approval question (is he Krishnarao?) closed unanswered 2000-02-27, so the line stays unattributed. ←[[m00397]]
- [2000-01-24] Kohli wants EIPL's fixed-price contract plus US swap marked to market on US GAAP consolidation; Krishnarao briefed Bob Butts. ←[[m00427]] ←[[m00429]]
- [2000-01-28] Butts and Wess: payments tied to one plant cannot be m-t-m and only 50% of DPC cashflows would qualify; options: no m-t-m, sell the contract, or securitise via an Indian party. ←[[m00468]] ←[[m00476]]
- [2000-02-11] "Sandeep" thanked the owner for a book and Krishna's help; training postponed some months. ←[[m00600]]
- [2000-02-22] Krishnarao sent "India: Enron aftermath" (Business Line): a review of Abhay Mehta's "Power Play", putting MSEB's 20-year payments for 2000 MW at $35 billion; owner forwarded it to his AOL. ←[[m00730]] ←[[m00738]] ←[[m00774]]
