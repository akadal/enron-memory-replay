- [2001-01-14] Kohli's points for Wade Cline's meeting with N.K. Singh (Prime Minister's office): seek Central government intervention and a joint Central/State/SEB negotiating group, Enron will not give up value; Phase I tariff Rs 4.02/kWh at 90% despatch, Rs 3.30-3.70 with Phase II (1440 MW, end-2001); MSEB 45-60 days late, DPC outflows up to Rs 450 Cr/month vs MSEB collections ~Rs 900 Cr; fixes: absorb Phase II across the Western Region grid (42,000 MW), restructure guarantees, IOC/GAIL equity, back-ended tariffs. ←[[m05327]]
- [2001-01-16] Kohli's note, at Wade Cline's request, for ex-MSEB chairman Basak's private meeting with the Governor of Maharashtra (the Federal government's man in the state), a route to engage the Centre; tariff data from Mohan's information sheet, with "the appropriate spin". ←[[m05331]]
- [2001-01-19] Press (18 Jan): DPC idle since early January after MSEB backed down fully; MSEB owes over Rs 300 crore for Nov-Dec; no sales-tax waiver on 1.2 Mt of IOC naphtha (Rs 11,050/t vs Glencore's 10,050), DPC passes the 4% on; Prabhu: no proposal for PTC to buy the surplus; MSEB declined its 15% Phase II stake. ←[[m05425]]
