# Enron India: Dabhol power sale and power trading

Team: Sandeep Kohli with Rajesh Sivaraman, Vivek Kejriwal, Shubh Shrivastava, Anshuman Srivastav (same team as memory/domains/work/naphtha-caps-india.md). ←[[m00100]] Detail (m-t-m, press, Oct 2000 Houston training) ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2000-02-w06]] … ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2000-10-w41]] ←[[dabhol-power-sale-2000-12-w50]]

- [1999-12-25] Kohli asked the owner and Vasant Shanbhogue to structure a sale of Dabhol power to another state: firm peak-period power (50 MW, Phase I) plus monsoon infirm power, tariff tracking the PPA. ←[[m00100]]
- [2000-01-20] (?) "Krishna", in the Bombay office, urgently asked Harry for a Rupee/US$ forward curve to at least 2005 for the deal. Status quo: the approval question (is he Krishnarao?) closed unanswered 2000-02-27, so the line stays unattributed. ←[[m00397]]
- [2000-12-09] Kohli reminds the owner to put in a word with Dave Duran, whom he asked for a meeting in the week of 17 Dec. ←[[m04703]]
- [2000-12-17] Kohli's draft memo to Wade on DPC's position and a possible workout (with his earlier Plan presentation); he has recommended the owner's team get involved on the DPC side; in the office Mon 18 Dec while on vacation. ←[[m04886]]
- [2000-12-17] Kohli, confidential: dispatch unused Dabhol capacity (700-1000 MW) merchant-style on MSEB's behalf to buyers outside MSEB (its credit is the core issue); an SEB bulletin board pricing 10-15 day power, cash and carry, from end Q1 2001; fuel hedging needs the Special Import Licence back or govt/RBI leave (Anshuman, Mukesh, IOC); against asking ENE for $75 million of Indian transmission lines until cost-benefit is shown. ←[[m04887]]
