# Hurricane-linked interest rate swap

- [2000-01-04] Gary Taylor (x31511), working on a hurricane-linked interest rate swap, asked for 10 minutes on how a category 5 hurricane hitting Miami would affect long and short-term interest rates. The owner agreed to stop by his office that afternoon. ←[[m00135]]
- [2000-01-04] The owner's view (owner, verbatim: "Such a hurricane would bankrupt a few insurance companies."). ←[[m00135]]
