# Enron Credit: Iris Mack's London work and conflicting priorities, May 2001

Plan outline and meetings: memory/domains/work/enron-credit-strategy-2001.md. Private-firm model: memory/domains/work/private-firm-credit-model-2001.md.

- [2001-05-02] Rakesh Bharati gets a 22% default probability from the martingale probability of default; the owner copies it to AOL. ←[[m07545]] ←[[m07592]]
- [2001-05-03] Ben Parsons sends ECquants, Iris, Vasant, Amitava Dhar and Katherine Siig Hull-White papers on CDS pricing; 4 May a link to 172 credit-risk papers (defaultrisk.com/papers.htm); Iris forwards both, the owner copies them to AOL. ←[[m07604]] ←[[m07631]] ←[[m07648]] ←[[m07682]]
- [2001-05-04] Credit Trading limit (approved at the December BOD, never signed): Cassandra Schultz has Jitendra Patel collect Bryan's, Ted's, News/Jordan's and Sherriff's signatures; then the owner signs for Research and it goes to Rick Buy and Jeff Skilling with last week's BOD limits. ←[[m07654]]
- [2001-05-04] Iris to Vasant: she learns Enron Credit's public-firm models, reviews PCA, PLS and cluster analysis for the private-firm models and, at Vince's and Slava's suggestion, writes the overall strategic plan (the 'Duffie document'); Amitava proposes a Tuesday call with Bryan, Markus and the owner, Anita to set it up. ←[[m07669]] ←[[m07701]]
- [2001-05-05] Vasant to Iris (4 May): he and Amitava are swamped; she leads the scoping and readies the data infrastructure; learning the econometric software with Ben and George matters more than the Duffie document now; Amitava joins once data arrives. ←[[m07719]] ←[[m07721]]
- [2001-05-05] Iris to the owner: conflicting messages, whom does she report to? The owner and Slava put the Duffie report first, Vasant does not; much chaos in Enron Credit, more in London (Slava: "a fish rots from the head"); should the report say what Enron Credit wants to hear (a private-firm model for its infrastructure) or her real findings, perhaps as two reports? ←[[m07719]] ←[[m07721]]
