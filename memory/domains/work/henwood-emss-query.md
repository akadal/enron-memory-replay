# Henwood EMSS query: London's Dutch market runs (Aug 2000)

- [2000-08-24] Karolina Potter (Paul Mead's Continental Power Trading, London) runs cost-based Henwood EMSS simulations of the Dutch market for traders' hourly stack and marginal-cost graphs; her marginal station (generation cost per MWh minus VOM) does not match EMSS's TransArea Marginal Units query. Steven Leppard refers her to Michael Schilmoeller: marginal cost is set by the capacity increment on the margin (incremental heat rates strictly increasing) but EMSS outputs only plant aggregates; he sends an MS Access SQL query (from a Homer City study) and proposes a Friday call. ←[[m02982]] ←[[m02987]]
- [2000-08-25] Schilmoeller: ask Grant Masson who else in Enron uses Henwood; sends 'Details of Jan and July.xls' and 'Supply Curve.xls' (an average curve from fuel and VOM costs); the EMSS supply curve is nonsense at points (reliability commitment, aggregate output). Henwood, Sacramento 916-569-0985: Tao Guo (the key one), Ajit Kulkarni, Cosimo Coscia, Wade Schauer (EMSS questions); escalate to VP Eric Toolson. ←[[m03007]] ←[[m03010]]
