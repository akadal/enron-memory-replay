# India power price and dispatch study with Henwood

Detail ←[[india-power-study-henwood-2001-01-w01]] ←[[india-power-study-henwood-2001-01-w02]]

India team and Dabhol: memory/domains/work/dabhol-power-sale.md. Henwood's EMSS in London: memory/domains/work/henwood-emss-query.md.

- [2001-01-08] Gibner asks Bonnie Nelson (legal) for a CA: Enron supplies much of the input (its view of the Indian market, detailed Dabhol data) via DPC, Enron India, maybe ENA; inputs and results confidential; Henwood contact Robert Schenck (Wayville, Australia; head office Sacramento). ←[[m05187]]
- [2001-01-10] Henwood (David Yomogida) to Kohli: India modelled as regional 'bubbles' with inter-regional links; 84,103 MW in the EMSS database vs 86,120 MW reported; 11 Jan: no data yet on hourly load shapes, hydro dispatch or transactions, Henwood builds load shaping and hydro allocation and asks Kohli for sources. ←[[m05260]] ←[[m05275]]
- [2001-01-11] Nelson's draft consulting agreement (Henwood's proposal, Enron terms, FCPA): Houston arbitration under Texas law; New York acceptable, Singapore/UK only if pressed, anything else (Australian too) needs foreign counsel; subcontracting only with Enron's written consent (cost plus 15%). ←[[m05281]]
- [2001-01-12] Kohli: data on ~1500 plants collected (all plants targeted by 15 Jan); getting power out of Maharashtra is the key to Dabhol; the delayed Henwood team reaches India Sunday 14 Jan (Krishna joins in Mumbai), meets Vivek, Anshuman, Ravi; call with Gibner Monday morning Houston; MSEB's rate case sets its 2001/02 payment capacity; first runs by end-January. ←[[m05297]] ←[[m05298]]
- [2001-01-14] Kohli, in India with Henwood: most data in place, meeting transmission officials for more; back in Houston possibly by the end of the week. ←[[m05327]]
