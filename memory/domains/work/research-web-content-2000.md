# Research know-how on intranet and web sites (2000)

The London team: memory/domains/organization/london-research.md. Research content for EnronOnline: memory/domains/work/eol-content-2000.md.

- [2000-06-15] Anjam Ahmad's Research Group intranet site on the Enron Europe home page (derivatives pricing, risk management, weather derivatives, credit risk, a links database), announced 14 Jun to a very positive response. Surbey: Research writes the content, IT only publishes; Ahmad wants plain introductions (VaR, credit risk) and collects material from Hewitt, Parsons, Leppard, Williams. ←[[m01980]] ←[[m01981]] ←[[m01982]]
- [2000-06-16] Sven Becker (Frankfurt; Joe Gold's group) adds the web as a sales and communication channel for Enron's European business units, with a wide energy risk management training section (know-how, educating the market, stickiness, maybe sold later); he wants Research's non-proprietary know-how. Ahmad offered help and set up Becker's talk with the owner on 16 Jun; Becker sent the sign-off presentation. ←[[m01979]] ←[[m01999]]
