# South America gas and power curves (Remi Collonges)

- [2000-02-24] Remi Collonges (Enron Development, (55) 11 5503 1200), new on the job and responsible for all South American gas and power curves plus books development and reporting, asked the owner and Grant Masson for advice and a meeting next Thu or Fri while in Houston. Owner: "My assistant Shirley Crenshaw will call you Friday to set up a meeting." To Crenshaw: "Please, include Grant as well." ←[[m00769]] ←[[m00771]]
- [2000-03-03] Collonges cancelled Friday's meeting with the owner and Grant; in Houston from Monday mid-day, he asks to meet next week. With new responsibilities he wants the owner and Grant to free up some team members' time to help his team, and to show them his cash position book ("far from perfect"): keep improving it or copy existing ENA models? ←[[m00855]]
