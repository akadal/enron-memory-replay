# Clayton Vernon's gas flow/price model

Clayton Vernon works on it with Vasant Shanbhogue. Inputs (Chris Germany, EIA's Roy Kass) and the simplified no-arbitrage version ←[[gas-flow-price-model-2000-03-w09]] ←[[gas-flow-price-model-2000-03-w13]] ←[[gas-flow-price-model-2000-04-w14]] ←[[m00042]]

- [1999-12-15] Proposal: from interconnect flows and spot prices, detect capacity hoarding, market power and storage (exotic calls at the consumption hub) → a stochastic basis model in weather. ←[[m00025]]
- [2000-01-17] Basis "uncoupling" when pipeline capacity binds: "one of the reasons we want to have the model". ←[[m00353]]
- [2000-03-03] Vernon: "the model" converged "beautifully"; which model is not said; filed here (?). ←[[m00861]]
- [2000-03-28] Vernon to Kevin Presto: the gas model is "completely untested and unvalidated"; only the owner decides on its release. ←[[m01116]] ←[[m01117]]
- [2000-04-04] GPG (John Goodpasture, "Kim Watson", Sarabeth Smith) wants strategic gas modelling for pipeline development; the owner answers in the first half of April after reviewing the group's model.
