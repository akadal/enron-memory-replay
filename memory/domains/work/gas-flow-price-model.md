# Clayton Vernon's gas flow/price model

Clayton Vernon works on it with Vasant Shanbhogue. ←[[m00042]]

- [1999-12-15] Proposal: from interconnect flows and spot prices, detect capacity hoarding, market power and storage (both as exotic calls at the consumption hub); flows imputable from prices → a stochastic basis model in weather. ←[[m00025]]
- [1999-12-16] Chris Germany (East Desk): firm capacity is a fixed monthly lease (South Texas–Brooklyn $12/month per 10,000 Dth); variable 6% fuel + 6.5% overhead; willingness to pay = delivered spread minus variable cost, often at tariff max. ←[[m00042]]
- [1999-12-22] Simplified version of Brad's model: no-arbitrage equalises margins; congestion raises transmission cost; storage a perpetual call; wellhead supply near price-elastic, burner-tip demand near inelastic; 8 parameters per node from 3 days' prices. Write-up, then a presentation to the owner. ←[[m00086]]
- [1999-12-27] Roy Kass (EIA): state wellhead estimates are not timely; field weather moves production and wellhead prices far more than Northeast weather; agrees with the owner on strictly increasing marginal production cost. ←[[m00102]]
- [2000-01-06] Vernon (from a class): pipelines are adopting Operational Balancing Agreements, so shippers book scheduled, not actual, flows; producing-region storage protects system integrity (parking, weekday-weekend and wellhead smoothing); estimated marketed production is the better proxy for receipt-meter flow. ←[[m00172]]
