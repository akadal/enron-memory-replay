# Clayton Vernon's gas flow/price model

Clayton Vernon works on it with Vasant Shanbhogue. ←[[m00042]]

- [1999-12-15] Vernon proposed a model from two series, telemetry interconnect flows and spot prices: detect capacity hoarding (basis vs inflow), market power (gas cost vs transmission basis), storage (inflow minus consumption); calibrate demand elasticities, treat storage and hoarding as exotic calls at the consumption hub; flows imputable from prices, giving a stochastic basis model in weather. Wants to proceed. ←[[m00025]]
- [1999-12-16] Input from Chris Germany (East Desk capacity trader): firm capacity leased as a fixed monthly payment (South Texas–Brooklyn $12/month per 10,000 Dth); variable 6% fuel + 6.5% overhead; willingness to pay = delivered spread minus variable cost ($0.35 − $0.125); many pipelines charge tariff max; multiple routes exist. ←[[m00042]]
- [1999-12-22] Vernon's simplified version of Brad's model: no-arbitrage equalises trading margins across the country; transmission cost rises with congestion; storage is a perpetual call; wellhead supply near fully price-elastic, burner-tip demand near inelastic. 8 parameters per node, fitted from 3 days' prices or point-calibrated on today's; storage costs vary least, margins and storage option values most. Write-up in Scientific Word to follow, then a presentation to the owner. ←[[m00086]]
