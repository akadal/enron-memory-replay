# Clayton Vernon's gas flow/price model

Clayton Vernon works on it with Vasant Shanbhogue. ←[[m00042]]

- [1999-12-15] Proposal: from interconnect flows and spot prices, detect capacity hoarding, market power and storage; storage and hoarding as exotic calls at the consumption hub; flows imputable from prices → a stochastic basis model in weather. ←[[m00025]]
- [1999-12-16] Chris Germany (East Desk capacity): firm capacity is a fixed monthly lease (South Texas–Brooklyn $12/month per 10,000 Dth); variable 6% fuel + 6.5% overhead; willingness to pay = delivered spread minus variable cost, often at tariff max. ←[[m00042]]
- [1999-12-22] Simplified version of Brad's model: no-arbitrage equalises margins; congestion raises transmission cost; storage a perpetual call; wellhead supply near price-elastic, burner-tip demand near inelastic; 8 parameters per node from 3 days' prices. Scientific Word write-up, then a presentation to the owner. ←[[m00086]]
- [1999-12-27] Roy Kass (EIA) told Vernon: state wellhead production estimates are not timely; field weather (freezes, hurricanes) moves production and wellhead prices far more than Northeast weather; he agrees with the owner that production has strictly increasing marginal costs. ←[[m00102]]
