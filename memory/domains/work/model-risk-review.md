# Model Development Review and model-risk audit

The owner re-sent this Sep–Oct 1999 thread on 1999-12-30. ←[[m00125]] ←[[m00126]] ←[[m00127]]

- [1999-12-30] Phase two (testing), 1 Sep 1999: Derek Claybrook (AA) asked [[stinson-gibner]] to rate Research's involvement (low → complete development) in Aruba, Oglethorpe, NPI (JEDI), Peakers, Bug, Snake River, Structured Credit, and listed exotic options valued outside the Research library (FPL PV Effect, basis options, LCRA spread, NOX, others). ←[[m00127]]
- [1999-12-30] Owner, 21 Sep: Research typically acts as internal consultant on narrow, complex problems; it does not control models it develops, end users change spreadsheets without its knowledge, so involvement must not read as endorsement. ←[[m00127]]
- [1999-12-30] Owner, 3–4 Oct, on Andrew Schuleman's draft report: "a wrong model results in bad decisions and eventually leads to accounting errors and incorrect financial statements"; the main risk is dispersed approval/validation, no unit authorised to audit and monitor models; perceived Research validation is far from the truth. ←[[m00125]] ←[[m00126]]
- [1999-12-30] He asked to split inappropriate application into wrong inputs (TVA, inputs stale) and wrong model; to include Risk Analytics and IT in scope; and to note that AA and Darrell Duffie audits of the Research model were his initiative. ←[[m00125]] ←[[m00126]]
