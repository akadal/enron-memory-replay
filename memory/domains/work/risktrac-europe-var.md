# UK and European positions in RisktRac (Jan 2001)

VaR methodology: memory/domains/work/var-backtesting-2000.md.

- [2001-01-03] Tamarchenko, reviewing UK positions against VaR in RisktRac (to Naveen Andrews, Matthew Adams, cc the owner): portfolio E1SB1-NBP (delta 239,021,655, gamma -211,031,450) shows zero VaR, as do E1SB2-NBP, E1SB3-NBP, E2XX1-NBP; book E1SB1 also sits under E1SBP1-PPP; asks RAC to check all UK portfolios, books and curves; to Port: books sit in both UK-GAS and UK-POWER. ←[[m05126]] ←[[m05127]]
- [2001-01-03] Oliver Gaylard: the UK books were set up wrongly with the spreadsheet feeds, now fixed (gas in UK gas, power in UK power); European RisktRac VaR does not yet feed corporate VaR; he proposes a regular project meeting, to raise at the weekly RAC/Research meeting. ←[[m05138]]
- [2001-01-10] Naveen Andrews to Tamarchenko (cc owner, Port): spread option, LOLP and SMP mappings for UK portfolios are right (E1S1B holds power PPP and gas NBP; deltas and gammas tie out); UK power shows 25 TWh too much (Enpower-RiskTrac communication), gas ties out in aggregate (off one Bcf); other VaR gaps: RiskTrac maps UK power to R4 (Cinergy) where the spreadsheet uses US-NG, and the Gas-Power and EFA-EFA correlations differ; Matthew Adams works on it with Oliver and London IT. ←[[m05247]]
