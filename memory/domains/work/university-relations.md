- [2000-06-03] To Jeff Skilling: "I would like to get on your calendar (together with Jeff Shankman) for 15 - 30 minutes" on his Wharton visit with Tom Piazze; a note on the visit to follow. ←[[m01903]]
- [2000-06-03] His case: Enron has been a passive customer of universities; competition for top students is hand-to-hand combat; "Practically every presentation I have made so far at different universities resulted in a number of resumes"; universities shape opinion on deregulation and market design; "we are currently underwriting two research projects at Stanford University" (Bambos' students). ←[[m01903]]
- [2000-06-03] "We should rethink the way we work with universities."; "I want to recommend a creation of a special function (VP or MD level) responsible for coordinating" research projects, lectures by Enron staff and faculty visits, apart from the Analyst/Associate program. ←[[m01903]]
- [2000-06-14] Carnegie Mellon: Richard Bryant (computational finance program director) wants Enron in the program's design and campus recruiting; Alex Huang, an alumnus, gave him the owner's contacts. ←[[m01971]]
