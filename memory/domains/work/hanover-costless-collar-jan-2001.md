# Hanover (HC) costless collar valuation (Jan 2001)

Other desk work: memory/domains/work/desk-analysis-requests.md.

- [2001-01-09] Lu to Andrea Reed (cc Bob Lee, Gibner, the owner): collar valued; Bloomberg 100-day vol ~50%, but a 3-year option deserves less, so results run for 20-60%; 3-year historical vol 47.2% gives a call strike of 83.32. ←[[m05223]] ←[[m05224]]
- [2001-01-11] Bob Lee: at 47.2% the call strike is 97.978, the swap does not affect the collar's value, 40-50% vol is defensible (traded HC options, long-dated fall-off). Chris Loehr and Ryan Siurek used treasury 5.127% (12/28/00; Research 4.6%) and maturity 6/30/03 (2.5 years, not 3): ceiling 92 103/256, floor 34 7/8, Bloomberg and Research agree. Also on it: Ron Baker, Wes Colwell, Anne Yaeger. ←[[m05273]]
