# Promigas TVCable deal: loan guarantee for call options

- [2000-01-28] Promigas and partners want to buy an $80 MM TVCable company in Mexico; Promigas would own 25% ($20 MM, $10 MM of it debt). Enron would guarantee Promigas's loan in exchange for call options on 50% of Promigas's TVCable equity. ←[[m00459]]
- [2000-01-28] Zimin Lu's plan (to the owner, cc Gibner, Shanbhogue): price the guarantee from a market quote and the internal credit model (ask Vasant); TVCable volatility from a comparable company and a lognormal fit to the economics model's Crystal Ball value histogram; then the call value and break-even strike. He asked the owner for input. ←[[m00459]]
- [2000-01-29] Maria E Garcia gave comparables (Cablevision, Comcast, Cox, MediaOne, Rogers, Time Warner; Morgan Stanley Dean Witter cable memo, 5 Jan 2000); her team sends the histogram after the weekend and gets guarantee quotes Monday. ←[[m00470]]
