# KMI database licence (EBS research)

- [2000-02-15] Ravi's EBS research buys KMI Corp.'s spreadsheet database (Richard Mack), to sit in Research; price agreed, EULA of 14 Feb, other terms via Russell Iorio (PennWell). On the owner's word it went straight to lawyer Mark Holsworth. Owner on clause (ii), no online bandwidth exchange or auction: "At some point we would like to include bandwidth under AOL. This clause may be very restrictive." ←[[m00634]]
- [2000-02-17] Holsworth redlined it; Ravi sent it to KMI/PennWell to sign. ←[[m00690]]
- [2000-02-19] Iorio proposes a counsel call (Toni Turner Budd for PennWell) Mon 21 Feb 2:00 CST; Ravi, away all week, asks Holsworth to set it up and the owner to join; the database is needed "to move forward on a number of things". ←[[m00714]]
- [2000-02-21] Owner, in London: "Maybe Stinson can get on this conference call. I shall be at the office on Thursday only this week." ←[[m00721]]
- [2000-02-23] Ravi to Gene Diers (handles it on Enron's standard vendor agreement for project services): KMI wants Enron barred from reselling or misrepresenting the database and from using it for trading or an exchange, which "stretches" Enron's use; Ravi calls the issues minor and wants the deal closed. ←[[m00747]]
