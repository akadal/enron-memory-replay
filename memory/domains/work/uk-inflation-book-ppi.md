# UK Inflation Book: PPI model

US inflation work: memory/domains/work/tips-inflation-model.md. London team: memory/domains/organization/london-research.md. Detail ←[[uk-inflation-book-ppi-2000-03-w13]] ←[[uk-inflation-book-ppi-2000-04-w14]] ←[[uk-inflation-book-ppi-2000-10-w43]]

- [2000-03-12] Exposure: PPI output indexes PLLU and DZCV to 2010 (they differ mainly by energy, 7.6% in PLLU), escalated by the same factors. Ahmad regresses PLLU on RPI and Brent deviations, DZCV without Brent (r-squared 0.47-0.49; PPI leads RPI by ~8 months). ←[[m00949]] ←[[m00954]]
- [2000-04-04] Martina's "very simple" UK RPI model, to Zimin: bootstraps mid RPI swaps (YoY = (RPI(p-2)/RPI(p-14) - 1)/2; Feb 2000 fixing 2.32%), constant cashflows between quoted years, Ahmad's smoothing macro, LIBOR discounting, a coupon per tenor. ←[[m01235]]
- [2000-10-24] RAC's final Inflation Model Review (Rodrigo Lamas, agreed with Anjam; to Port, owner, Anjam): freeze the RPI curve short term; combine GILTS and RPI swaps; the short-term (PLLU, DZCV) weakness is common to Enron's econometric models; a new econometrics hire is needed for longer-term work. Owner takes it to AOL 27 Oct. ←[[m03809]] ←[[m03889]]
