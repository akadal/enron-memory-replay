# UK Inflation Book: PPI model

US inflation work: memory/domains/work/tips-inflation-model.md. London team: memory/domains/organization/london-research.md

- [2000-03-10] Martina Angelova: the book's main exposure is to PPI output indexes PLLU and DZCV to 2010; they differ mainly by energy in PLLU (7.6%), yet the current model escalates both by the same factors. ←[[m00949]]
- [2000-03-10] Ahmad's final PLLU model: PLLU[t] = a.RPI[t] + b.RPI[t+15] + c.BrentDeviations[t] + constant (Brent around a long-term $18.80; Global Products' forward curve). Less sensitive to its coefficients, so steadier monthly P&L. DZCV next, same method without Brent. ←[[m00949]]
- [2000-03-12] Corrected update to the owner, Maureen Raymond, Gibner and Zimin Lu after a conference call: fits around r-squared 0.47-0.49; PPI leads RPI by about 8 months. ←[[m00954]] ←[[m00949]]
