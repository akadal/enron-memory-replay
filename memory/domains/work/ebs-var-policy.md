# EBS V@R transaction policy (2000)

Network modelling: memory/domains/work/ebs-network-planning.md. VaR methods: memory/domains/work/var-backtesting-2000.md.

- [2000-06-29] Barry Pearce (EBS) circulates John Echols' draft (28 Jun, to Jim Fallon and Pearce; Lou Casari asked too; limits such as approval of server relocations, maybe Rick Paiste or Marquardt) to Gibner, Surbey and Ted Murphy: step 1 configure and value the network (accrual and operational assets plus MTM lambdas), step 2 its VaR, step 3 incremental VaR of new deals, amendments and reconfigurations; mixing MTM and accrual is hard to model. ←[[m02186]] ←[[m02207]]
- [2000-06-29] Owner: "I am meeting Ted Murphy today and I shall bring it up with him."; "We have unit at Research (Tanya Tamarchenko, reporting to Grant Mason) who is responsible for V@R support."; Pearce: "That would be great". ←[[m02186]] ←[[m02207]]
- [2000-06-30] Echols: really a volumetric control (network changes over set Megabytes of storage or OC-3 equivalents need Risk Management approval) until the V@R math exists; Kevin Hannon gave him authority to move control of significant network changes from the field to the trading floor, draft to Hannon and Rice ASAP; e.g. the primary TeraPop (three servers, 165 GB each) moved from Portland to LA with no economic analysis; later the metrics should rest on Research's V@R; Murphy backs it conceptually (Transaction Approval Process, TAP) but wants it integrated with other units' metrics. ←[[m02208]]
