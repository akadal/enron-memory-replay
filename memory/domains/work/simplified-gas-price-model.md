# Vernon's simplified gas price model and Scientific Word

Earlier proposal and capacity notes: memory/domains/work/gas-transmission-price-model.md

- [1999-12-22] [[clayton-vernon]] described a simplified version of Brad's gas price model: a no-arbitrage condition equates trading margins across the country. Assumptions: transmission cost rises with congestion; wellhead supply almost fully price-elastic; burner-tip demand almost fully inelastic; storage is a perpetual call option. ←[[m00086]]
- [1999-12-22] It has 8 parameters per major node. Costs vary least over time (injection/withdrawal, then delivery, then pipeline fees); trading margins and storage option values most. ←[[m00086]]
- [1999-12-22] Estimated from the last 3 days of prices, or by point calibration on today's data. ←[[m00086]]
- [1999-12-22→] Vernon will write it up in Scientific Word and present it at the owner's earliest convenience (no date); [[vasant-shanbhogue]] copied. ←[[m00086]]
