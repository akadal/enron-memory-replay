# UK power volatility curve generator (Anjam Ahmad, London)

- [1999-12-22] Anjam Ahmad announced a new UK power volatility curve generator: EFA/monthly detail, marked to market up to 6 years, then decaying to a long-term level. Swaption traders fit the first 5–6 years to baseload swaption implied vols (3–12 month tenors) and price EFA-slot swaptions consistently. ←[[m00098]]
- [1999-12-22] Curves reset as the market moves, so the vol book can be marked to market; daily VaR may be affected. Model: T:\READWRTE\ELEC_UK\MODELS\VOLCURVEGENERATORNEW.XLS. ←[[m00098]]
- [1999-12-22] Ahmad asked [[stinson-gibner]] to audit it; the owner and Dale Surbey copied. ←[[m00098]] [2000-01-18] Owner asked Gibner: "Did you have the chance to audit the UK vol generator?" ←[[m00383]]
