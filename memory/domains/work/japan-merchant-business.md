# Japan merchant business

- [1999-12-13] Joseph Hirl is evaluating an Enron merchant business in Japan; after Whalley said at the SA offsite that the owner knew someone with relevant talent, he asked to talk about the candidate (Sydney that day, Tokyo the next week; mobile 81 90 4073 6761). ←[[m00012]]
- [1999-12-17] Owner forwarded Hirl the details of Taiichi Hoshino: Stanford PhD candidate (EES/OR), ex-McKinsey Tokyo, must return to Japan/East Asia after graduating, offers risk-analytics help in Tokyo. In Oct the owner had passed him to the Analyst/Associate program (cc Celeste Roberts, Greg Whalley). ←[[m00066]]
- [1999-12-22] Asked by Hirl for his view, the owner said Hoshino is Prof. Darrell Duffie's research assistant at Stanford, where the owner met him: "A very bright fellow", but he could not assess his commercial skills. ←[[m00089]]
- [2000-05-31] LTCB (via Rousseau Anai, Enron Development; cc Hirl; through Trena McFarland): indicative offers for a client long oil: a 1-year at-the-money WTI quanto (strike about 3,030 yen/bbl, 3 million bbl) and a 1-year $ put/yen call at 107.5 knocking in at 80% of WTI ($90 million); owner to Anjam Ahmad: "Please, talk to Trena about the quanto and barrier options we have in Exotica." (Zimin checks the fit). ←[[m01857]]
