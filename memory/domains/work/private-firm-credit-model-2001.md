# Private-firm credit model: RiskCalc test and data vendors (Apr 2001)

Duffie's audit and comparative study: memory/domains/work/model-risk-audit.md.

- [2001-04-16] Mark K Wilson's Moody's/FAMAS scoring meeting, 17 Apr, 2-4, EB 2831 (owner, Bradford cc): Moody's just bought FAMAS/LA Encore; Enron reviews all scoring vendors for predictive power and mapping to Moody's/S&P/Enron ratings. ←[[m06886]]
- [2001-04-17] Iris Mack (60-day trial, for Chaney and Kim Detiveaux): RiskCalc is calibrated for the US, Canada and Australia on a year of statements; Europe's data came from London, she asks Houston for North American private counterparties' financials. ←[[m06938]] ←[[m06950]]
- [2001-04-17] Valnek and Parsons' parent-subsidiary model (paper 10 Apr): Amadeus inputs, 5-year PD, London web trial ~2 more weeks (Mumford); 72 of ~105 UK power and gas desk names had data (the rest too new); all Enron Europe names by end-July. ←[[m06939]] ←[[m06951]] ←[[m06954]] ←[[m06958]]
- [2001-04-17] Mack's data-vendor and model plan goes to Salmon, Mumford, Eric Kirkpatrick; Salmon praises it, scope document to follow. ←[[m06940]] ←[[m06952]]
- [2001-04-18] Valnek: European data good bar low-reporting countries and new firms; S&P's model covers only S&P-rated firms over USD 100m, so RiskCalc fits better; US private data may decide the model. CreditDimensions (PwC spin-off) offers a free data test (Mumford). ←[[m06965]] ←[[m06964]]
- [2001-04-19] Moody's Neal Clark offers a quants meeting on using fewer than 17 inputs; Salmon asks Mack and Dhar to set it up, plans once D&B or Experian data arrive; Mumford: DUNS on ~13k of 18.5k counterparties, buy D&B data, a second model for thin names. Owner copies it to AOL. ←[[m07001]] ←[[m07012]] ←[[m07040]]
