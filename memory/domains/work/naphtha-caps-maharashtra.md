# Naphtha cap pricing — Maharashtra power deal

- [1999-12-10] Pinnamaneni Krishnarao told the owner the Indian side asked for help pricing naphtha caps: naphtha prices more than doubled in 1999, fuel cost is a pass-through, and Maharashtra politicians attack the power prices paid to Enron. Owner forwarded it to his personal address. ←[[m00007]]
- [1999-12-10] Indicative 2000 monthly cap premiums (1999 prices as forwards, % of average price): ATM 10.4%, 5–20% OTM 8.4%→4.4%, $168 cap 15%, $200 cap 9%, $225 cap 6%; historical 2-year vol 34%. Forwards are guesses; Anshuman Srivastav, Rajesh Sivaraman and Sandeep Kohli to get 2000 forwards/vols from Singapore naphtha trader John L Chismar. ←[[m00007]]
- [1999-12-10] Krishnarao asked for the Exotic Options Library on a common drive (G, J, M, O or P) for the Enron Development team. ←[[m00007]]
