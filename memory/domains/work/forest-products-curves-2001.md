# Forest products and newsprint price curves (EIM fundamentals, 2001)

Petrochemical curves: memory/domains/work/petrochem-forward-curves-2001.md. JCC-Brent: memory/domains/work/jcc-brent-hedge.md.

- [2001-09-27] Nelson Neale to the owner and Vasant Shanbhogue on Texas A&M's (Bessler's group) proposal to EIM fundamentals: a vector autoregression (VAR) model of Enron forest products prices, capped at 25 series; no problem with approach or scope, but the cap is easily exceeded given the many P&P commodities, each with its own supply and demand variables. If EIM proceeds, Research should give input along the way. ←[[m09213]]
- [2001-09-27] Research's own practice per Neale: cointegration/ECM (diesel-heating oil, JCC-Brent crude), no VAR yet but likely for plastics (polypropylene, propylene, ethylene, HDPE) and tech-sector price indexes. With EIM fundamentals it builds a newsprint curve: cointegration with lumber and pulp futures was weak, so price is modelled on newsprint supply and demand (production, inventories, US demand, days of supply). ←[[m09213]]
