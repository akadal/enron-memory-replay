# Cuneo matter

- [2000-01-15] Bonnie White told the owner the court signed an order dismissing the plaintiff's case on 1/06/00; Enron requested a copy, which she will send to Michelle Cash, the owner and Gary Hickerson. The owner thanked her for her help in the difficult matter and said he would stop by to introduce himself (owner, verbatim: "I shall try to stop by one day to formally introduce myself."). ←[[m00345]]
