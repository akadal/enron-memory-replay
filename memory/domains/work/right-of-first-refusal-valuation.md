# Valuing an incumbent shipper's right of first refusal (ROFR)

- [2000-01-18] [[clayton-vernon]]: price-match promises deter price cuts; a ROFR deters outside bids and makes partners' shares effectively illiquid. ←[[m00384]]
- [2000-01-20] Owner to Shelley Corman: the incumbent "has something of value he never paid for"; a numerical estimate could help argue against it, and since value is case specific "we shall rather produce a template you can use for valuation case by case". Vernon's essay: ROFR harms efficiency (deters bids, lets an inefficient incumbent win). ←[[m00394]]
- [2000-01-21] Zimin Lu's template (to Corman and Chris Gaskill): ROFR = series of forward-start options struck at the money, mimicking "matching the best bid"; underlying is the best bid (tied to the two hubs' price differential), vol from best-bid history. ←[[m00414]]
- [2000-02-04] After a meeting, Lu added a switch letting the pipeline accept or reject the best bid when it is not the cap rate (to Gaskill and Corman, owner cc). ←[[m00543]]
