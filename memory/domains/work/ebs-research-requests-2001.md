- [2001-04-04] Randall Hicks (Director Marketing, EBS Digital Content Services; via Rita Hartfield) wants Research to check StyleLogic's (Brent Rosenkranz) movie recommendation model for the next Entertainment on Demand: "The person supporting EBS in the Research Group is Martin Lin."; "I shall ask him to give you a call and will be glad to join you at the meeting". ←[[m06508]] ←[[m06509]]
- [2001-04-24] Movie trading (Enron as market maker for futures on box-office grosses; Rich DiMichele's FAQ draft, 23 Apr, to Fallon, Racicot and others): Chonawee Supatgiat (to the owner, Gibner) proposes a weekly dynamic bid-ask process so Enron earns the spread at zero net position, fit for illiquid EOL products too; let prices float, as studios and exhibitors hold inside information (Russ, Martin); weekly mark-to-market with margin calls; maybe options; most of Rich's draft matches his mid-April idea. ←[[m07150]] ←[[m07151]] ←[[m07183]] ←[[m07184]]
- [2001-04-25] Movie futures, Supatgiat to DiMichele: a film carries high unique and low market risk, so customers trade to diversify, not hedge; Enron should spread positions over 15+ films; a 1-day bidding window (e.g. Mondays) for his bid-ask scheme, which he believes is new; memo to follow. DiMichele: prices indicative, Enron first seeks the other side, slates may add liquidity. ←[[m07212]] ←[[m07238]]
