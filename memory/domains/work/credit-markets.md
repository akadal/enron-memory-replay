# Credit risk and Enron's Credit Markets Group

Detail ←[[credit-markets-2000-02-w06]]

- [1999-12-17] John Sherriff (Enron Europe) formed the Credit Markets Group under Bryan Seyfried: bankruptcy protection on credits in Enron's portfolio, traded on EnronOnline, external credit web site; London-based, Houston desk; launch late Jan. ←[[m00072]] ←[[m00117]]
- [1999-12-17] Seyfried asked for "Actively Managing Credit Risk" (Shanbhogue co-author, Risk Publications); LSE intern Bijoya Banerjea turns it into a credit-trading White Paper crediting the owner's group; Katja Schilling (portal) wants Shanbhogue's help. ←[[m00052]] ←[[m00118]]
- [2000-01-03] Website and publishing: CreditManager demo (7 Jan); Parsons' "Measuring Credit Risk" posted unless the owner objects; Shanbhogue's 1998 article: "RISK magazine has the copyright". ←[[m00040]] ←[[m00124]] ←[[m00129]] ←[[m00170]] ←[[m00175]]
- [2000-01-17] Benjamin Parsons sent the owner, Masson and Shanbhogue the EnronCredit.com pricing paper for the "Bankruptcy Swaps" launching next week, "intentionally simplistic" (transparent, quick models, no "rocket science"), asking for feedback; Seyfried to cut it for the website. ←[[m00362]]
- [2000-02-09] Seyfried's credit trading, for approval under an interim trading policy: RAC goes to Jeff only with e-mail concurrence from the owner, Steve Young, Fernley Dyson, Michael R Brown, Bill Bradford and John Sherriff (Rick Buy wants all comfortable); Brown and Dyson signed off. ←[[m00579]] ←[[m00582]]
