# Credit risk and Enron's Credit Markets Group

- [1999-12-16] Owner accepted a RiskMetrics CreditManager net-meeting with Aidan Mc Nulty for Fri 7 Jan 2000, 9:30 (owner's time); Masson, Kindall, Shanbhogue copied; confirmed 30 Dec. ←[[m00040]] ←[[m00124]]
- [1999-12-17] John Sherriff (Enron Europe) formed the Credit Markets Group under Bryan Seyfried (ex-RAC Europe): markets in bankruptcy protection on credits in Enron's portfolio, EnronOnline execution, external credit web site (prices, models, research); London-based, Houston desk; launch expected late Jan. ←[[m00072]] ←[[m00117]]
- [1999-12-17] Seyfried asked Krishnarao and the owner for a soft copy of "Actively Managing Credit Risk" (Shanbhogue co-author, Risk Publications); LSE intern Bijoya Banerjea will update it into a credit-trading White Paper, crediting the owner's group; Katja Schilling (building the portal) asked Shanbhogue to work with her. ←[[m00052]] ←[[m00118]]
- [2000-01-03] Benjamin Parsons asked to post his "Measuring Credit Risk" on the credit-trading website Banerjea is building; owner will check it for proprietary content: "If you don't hear from me by Wednesday, it's OK to post it." ←[[m00129]]
- [2000-01-06] Schilling and Banerjea want to publish Shanbhogue's credit article (Risk Books, 1998) externally; owner: "RISK magazine has the copyright" (even minor changes need consent); Schilling will ask Shân Millie at Risk Books. ←[[m00170]] ←[[m00175]]
