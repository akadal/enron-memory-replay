- [2000-06-19] Seyfried asks for Amitava Dhar or Vasant Shanbhogue in London for 8-12 weeks to jumpstart credit scoring and modelling on his and Ben Parsons' project, guide the CLO side and help hire (a City hire means long lead times and high cost); the owner proposes a call, Tue 9:30 Houston time. ←[[m02009]] ←[[m02019]]
- [2000-06-19] Parsons: automated pricing for thousands of mostly illiquid credits (5,000 by year-end) from FMC (Bloomberg curves), Bond, CDS and KMV EDF models (KMV being installed), illiquid names as weighted averages of liquid ones (McKinsey: 6-10 man-months). Shanbhogue: map them to liquid names by attributes, refine with traders; "What do we calibrate?" ←[[m02015]] ←[[m02021]]
- [2000-07-06] Tamarchenko (to Masson, owner) fitted price-process models to NG prompt-month prices, some fit well; proposes mean-reverting jump-diffusion for NG in the Credit Model; other contracts next. ←[[m02259]]
