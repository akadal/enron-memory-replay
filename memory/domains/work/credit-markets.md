- [2001-03-30] Seyfried forwards Martin McDermott's 23 Mar note: asset swaps and CDS carry the same senior unsecured risk, so the gap is supply and demand; scarce bonds keep asset-swap spreads lower; per JP Morgan, convertible hedgers pairing a cheap equity call with CDS bid CDS up; a fuller note to follow; owner copies it to AOL 5 Apr. ←[[m06436]] ←[[m06540]]
- [2001-04-02] Owner's Newsletter article (written overnight; to Shanbhogue: "Can you, please, review this Newsletter article."), worked out with Seyfried: convertible-arbitrage hedge funds lay off credit risk through asset swaps or CDS, pushing protection costs up with no change in Enron's credit; "Enron issues recently a$1.9 billion convertible bond".
