# Credit risk and Enron's Credit Markets Group

- [1999-12-16] Owner accepted a RiskMetrics CreditManager net-meeting with Aidan Mc Nulty for Fri 7 Jan 2000, 9:30 (owner's time); Masson, Kindall, Shanbhogue, Crenshaw copied; on 30 Dec he forwarded them Mc Nulty's confirmation (212 981 7422). ←[[m00040]] ←[[m00124]]
- [1999-12-17] John Sherriff (Enron Europe) formed the Credit Markets Group, led by Bryan Seyfried (ex-RAC Europe trading controls/research): markets in bankruptcy protection on credits in Enron's portfolio, EnronOnline execution, external credit web site (prices, models, research); London-based with a Houston desk; public launch expected late Jan. Anjam Ahmad had forwarded it to the owner, Gibner and Shanbhogue. ←[[m00072]] ←[[m00117]]
- [1999-12-17] Seyfried asked Krishnarao and the owner for a soft copy of "Actively Managing Credit Risk" (Shanbhogue co-author, Risk Publications); LSE intern Bijoya Banerjea will update it into a credit-trading White Paper, crediting the owner's group; Katja Schilling (building the group's portal) asked Shanbhogue to work with her. ←[[m00052]] ←[[m00118]]
