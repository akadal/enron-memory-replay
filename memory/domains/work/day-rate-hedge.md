# Amerada Hess day-rate hedge (semi-submersible rigs)

- [2000-01-04] John Peyton sent historical day rates for 4th/5th-generation semi-submersibles: Amerada Hess pays 155,000/day for the next 4 years; proposed floor $100,000/day, 1 Jan 2001-31 Dec 2004; he asked for the call strike that makes the collar costless, what else the statistics need, and how to treat highs and lows; structures to show Hess that Friday. Copied: Chris Hilgert, Fred Lagrasta, Randal Maffett, W. Tom Byargeon. ←[[m00141]]
