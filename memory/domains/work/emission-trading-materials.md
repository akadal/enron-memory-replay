# Emission trading materials for Adam Brulinski (Poland)

- [1999-12-14] [[adam-brulinski]] (Warsaw) asked the owner to email materials on emission trading (sulfur/pollution allowance trading), mainly the substantive side, to start promoting trading of emission rights in Poland; he called it urgent. The owner replied he would gather some information (owner, verbatim: "Let me gather some information for you."). ←[[m00016]]
- [1999-12-16] The owner asked Joseph Hrgovcic whether Enron has any materials (owner, verbatim: "Do we have any materials about it?"). ←[[m00034]]
- [1999-12-16] Hrgovcic: Susan Wood, who heads Enron's emissions marketing, says the group sends out no emissions trading materials. She recommends the Emissions Trading Handbook ($50, link on etei.org, about three weeks to arrive); etei.org and its parent emissions.org are good sources. He offered to buy the handbook if the owner asks. ←[[m00035]]
- [1999-12-16→] Open: whether anything was sent to Brulinski or the handbook ordered is not in the mail so far. ←[[m00035]]
