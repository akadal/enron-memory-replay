# Credit markets white paper

Background: the new London group is described in [[credit-markets-group]].

- [1999-12-17] Katja Schilling (London Credit Markets/Trading, with [[bryan-seyfried]]) is designing a website as an information portal on credit markets. She asked [[vasant-shanbhogue]] to work with intern Bijoya Banerjea (LSE, on Xmas break) to update the paper "Actively Managing Credit Risk" (Risk Publications) into a White Paper on the current credit market, for the website and later publication; a draft is hoped for within a few weeks. ←[[m00052]]
- [1999-12-17] Seyfried (in Houston 1999-12-17, London the following week) asked the owner and Pinnamaneni Krishnarao for a soft copy of the credit paper; his group will do the work and give credit. The owner forwarded the thread to his personal address, no comment. ←[[m00052]]
