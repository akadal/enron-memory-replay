# Credit markets white paper

Background: the new London group is described in [[credit-markets-group]].

- [1999-12-17] Katja Schilling (London Credit Markets/Trading, with [[bryan-seyfried]]) is designing a credit-markets information website. She asked [[vasant-shanbhogue]] to work with LSE intern Bijoya Banerjea to update "Actively Managing Credit Risk" (Risk Publications) into a White Paper for the website and later publication; a draft is hoped for within weeks. ←[[m00052]]
- [1999-12-17] Seyfried asked the owner and Pinnamaneni Krishnarao for a soft copy of the paper; his group will do the work and give credit. The owner forwarded the thread to his personal address, no comment. ←[[m00052]]
- [2000-01-03] Benjamin Parsons asked to post his article "Measuring Credit Risk" on the new credit trading website (Banerjea builds it; Ross Prevatt copied). The owner will check it for proprietary content once more (owner, verbatim: "If you don't hear from me by Wednesday,"): no word by Wednesday means he may post it. ←[[m00129]]
- [2000-01-06] Schilling wants to use the Risk article co-authored by Vasant (also in the 1998 credit derivatives book). The owner: RISK holds the copyright (owner, verbatim: "RISK magazine has the copyright. You have to contact them to get the permission to use this"); contact Shan Millie at Risk Books, London. ←[[m00170]]
- [2000-01-07] Schilling will contact Risk that day and report back; the outcome is not in the mail so far. ←[[m00175]]
