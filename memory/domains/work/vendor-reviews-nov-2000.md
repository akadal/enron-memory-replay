- [2000-11-21] Owner's outline for a letter to Nesbitt (via Goodpasture): his case-study offer, "The cost of a weekly project  would be $12,000 that would be applied to the purchase price"; "This project would allow us to evaluate the model" and the staff to support it; "Please, let me know more about this option." Goodpasture: ETS wants the long-term NA gas model and database, access in Omaha and Houston; Steve Hotte (ETS CIO) installs; then a modified licence. ←[[m04370]] ←[[m04378]]
- [2000-11-27] Owner on the $12,000 test week: "Please, let me know more about this option.", "Of course, a trading desk may be interested in the short term version of the model."; Nesbitt: T&M contract capped at $12K; long- and short-run gas models share the software; in Houston from ~4 Dec to settle it; 28 Nov sends the consulting agreement and a weeklong onsite test statement (early January), forwarded to Goodpasture. ←[[m04442]] ←[[m04453]] ←[[m04490]] ←[[m04499]]
- [2000-11-30] Bob Lee compares Enron's spread option model with SciFinance's: very accurate (an accuracy knob costs time), much slower at high correlation; Lu asks the owner and Gibner to look. ←[[m04541]]
- [2000-12-12] Halliburton on Adaptive Trade (Enron weighs investing; a GUI over ILOG Solver): no manual, small market, many competitors, early stage, so "very little prospect" of funding; SAP or i2 scheduling tools likelier to succeed; a demo might still help modelling work. ←[[m04761]]
