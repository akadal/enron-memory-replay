# TW Capacity (Transport) Options FERC filing

Other FERC matters: memory/domains/industry/ferc-transmission-resale.md.

- [2000-07-12] Jeffery Fawcett asks the owner and Zimin Lu to review Susan Scott's draft FERC filing for TW Capacity (Transport) Options: product definition, position management, and the footnote on how the market would value option fees; comments by Fri 14 Jul; customers and informal FERC talks 17-26 Jul, final internal review 27 Jul, filing Mon 31 Jul; TW Commercial team (Steven Harris, Fawcett, Kevin Hyatt, Lorraine Lindberg, TK Lohman, Michele Lokay, Christine Stokes). ←[[m02377]]
- [2000-10-12] Live deal (Fawcett, value by Fri 13 Oct): 32,000 Dth/d, Jan 2002-Oct 2006, $0.2175/Dth plus fuel, La Plata Pool to Waha, with alternate firm delivery to the California Border (floor: rate plus 4.75% fuel plus 50% of the SoCal minus TW Ignacio spread); Zimin Lu values the switch option at 20 cents/Dth (IF-WAHA, IF-EPSO/SJ, NGI-SOCAL; Waha-SJ 95%, Socal-SJ 90%). ←[[m03581]]
- [2001-01-12] Youyi Feng to Kimberly Watson (cc Don Powell, Jim Wiltfong, Sean Bolks): model zone EF (maybe wider) assuming a positive spread between two locations always implies a swaption, a reasonable approximation per Jim and Sean; build the prototype on it if Kim agrees. ←[[m05295]]
