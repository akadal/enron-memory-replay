# Synfuel option valuation (Section 29 tax credit)

Other desk work: memory/domains/work/desk-analysis-requests.md.

- [2001-02-12] Zimin Lu to Lenny Hochschild: the deal's economics hinge on the tax credit ($25.03); if Congress removes it (the owner's warning that it might act fast), AIG will not deliver the synfuel and pays Enron $4.55, so Zimin suggests a reserve. Hochschild: Section 29 is likelier to be extended beyond 2007 than repealed, and Enron can extend the contract; against valuing repeal risk, he will ask his supervisor. The credit sits in the strike of a digital option, with a second strike added (Hochschild accepts the amendment); exercise is most likely. Paulo Issler built the original model (its standard deviations), back on Wednesday. ←[[m05831]]
