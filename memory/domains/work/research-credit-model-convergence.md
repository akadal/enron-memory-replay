- [2000-01-26] Tanya Tamarchenko to Mike Fowler (cc owner, Grant Masson, Tanya Rohauer, Vincent Tang, Wenyao Jia, William Bradford): simulation accuracy matters little for the credit reserve itself (price-process and correlation assumptions are rough) but matters for reserve sensitivities when they are comparable to the calculation error. ←[[m00446]]
