# Promigas TVCable call option

- [2000-01-28] Zimin Lu to the owner (cc [[stinson-gibner]], [[vasant-shanbhogue]]): Promigas and partners want to buy an $80MM TVCable company in Mexico; Promigas's 25% is $20MM, half of it debt. Enron would guarantee the loan in exchange for calls on 50% of Promigas's TVCable equity. ←[[m00459]]
- [2000-01-28] Lu's plan: price the guarantee from a market quote and/or an internal credit-risk model (consult Vasant); value the call from comparables, with volatility from a lognormal fit to the company-value histogram of the economics model (Crystal Ball), then the call value and break-even strike. "Any inputs are extremely welcome." ←[[m00459]]
- [2000-01-29] Maria Garcia (after meeting Lu on 28 Jan): comparables Cablevision, Comcast, Cox, MediaOne, Rogers, Time Warner (data: Morgan Stanley Dean Witter cable research, 5 Jan 2000); her team builds the volatility histogram over the weekend; guarantee quotes on Mon 31 Jan. ←[[m00470]]
- [2000-02-04] Tanya Rohauer (credit) gave default probabilities for E-rating 7 as of 3 Feb; Lu asked Vincent Tang to put Promigas's rating (BB) and those probabilities into the loan guarantee model. ←[[m00541]]
