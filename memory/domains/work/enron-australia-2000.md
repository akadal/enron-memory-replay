- [2000-08-07] Owner to Paul Quilkey and Raymond Yeow: "I learned a lot about Australian markets and was greatly impressed with the quality of the people at the Sydney office."; he proposes Christian Werner (Sydney weather) spend a week in Houston with Mike Roberts and Hrgovcic on forecasting technology: "I shall be glad to underwrite the cost of this trip." ←[[m02711]]
- [2000-08-08] Yeow: Quilkey's wife had a baby, the Werner visit waits for his return. Hrgovcic: replicating Werner's climate models is long-term; he promised RAC and the PJM weather traders a VAR model of daily temperatures (Weather Book daily V@R, week-ahead CDD/HDD); a simplified MM5 takes months (Jacobsen, Stanford); AER (Massachusetts) runs MM5 daily, for a fee. ←[[m02722]] ←[[m02723]]
- [2000-08-11] Yeow: Werner is Sydney's only weather person, so perhaps Mike Roberts comes to Sydney before the Olympics; Mike prefers Werner in Houston; owner: "It may be necessary to go to Sydney." ←[[m02797]] ←[[m02812]]
- [2000-08-19] Werner: has Research applied extreme value theory (the owner raised it in Sydney) to power and weather? Customers want t_max, t_min and precipitation structures, priced so far by burn analysis; EVT (hydrology, ETH Zurich VaR work) suits extremes. ←[[m02915]]
