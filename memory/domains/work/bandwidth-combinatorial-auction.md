# Combinatorial auction for surplus bandwidth

Bandwidth work generally: memory/domains/work/broadband-services.md, memory/domains/work/ebs-network-planning.md.

- [2000-12-28] Greg Woulfe sends the owner, Jim Fallon, Marcello Romano, Paul Racicot and Russell Woody background for a meeting on 11 Jan: Supatgiat's review of Steinberg and Kelly, "Combinatorial Auction with Multiple Winners for Universal Service" (Management Science): it suits bandwidth, where bidders value combinations of links, but the method is untested. ←[[m05060]]
- [2000-12-28] Supatgiat's proposal: a combinatorial auction or exchange to dispose of unused or unsold bandwidth from Enron's long position (he knows of no bandwidth exchange taking combinatorial bids); Enron bids as a dummy bidder, overbidding low winning bids to keep the product, an indirect reserve price; precedent: Alberta's PPA auction, in which Enron Canada took part 3-4 months earlier. ←[[m05060]]
