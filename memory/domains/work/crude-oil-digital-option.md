# Crude oil downside insurance model (Zimin Lu)

- [2000-01-13] Zimin Lu finished, for Fred Lagrasta (cc the owner, Stinson Gibner), a model insuring against crude oil downside risk: a digital option on an Asian strip. If the average prompt-month crude price over a set window falls below the strike, it pays a lump sum (the "digital payout" input); a European option on the Asian strip is priced for comparison. ←[[m00338]]
- [2000-11-03] Gillian Lockwood (London; via Jarek Astramowicz) wants the owner's view on digitals: rationale, other markets, setting the strike, what hit probability keeps a deal arm's length; her idea: guaranteed year-one income (to use expiring tax losses) offset by a year-two expense, tied to a commodity exposure. Pavel Zadorozhny: digitals are extremely rare in crude, never quoted OTC; a year ago US producers (Venoco, Titan, Magnum Hunter and others) sold knock-outable swaps with an embedded digital put, which he hedged with a digital Cal 00 $16 swaption to Elf and strips of digital puts. ←[[m04014]]
