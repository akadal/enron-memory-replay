# Crude oil downside insurance model (Zimin Lu)

- [2000-01-13] Zimin Lu finished, for Fred Lagrasta (cc the owner, Stinson Gibner), a model insuring against crude oil downside risk: a digital option on an Asian strip. If the average prompt-month crude price over a set window falls below the strike, it pays a lump sum (the "digital payout" input); a European option on the Asian strip is priced for comparison. ←[[m00338]]
