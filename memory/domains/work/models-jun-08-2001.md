- [2001-06-06] P Plus: Zimin Lu sends the pricing model and documentation prepared by Stinson Gibner; Gibner: an early draft that needs revision, but it explains the spread-option structure. Owner copies both to AOL (6-7 Jun). ←[[m08408]] ←[[m08410]] ←[[m08415]] ←[[m08448]]
- [2001-06-08] Tanya Tamarchenko and Rakesh Bharati send a document on "Convolution VAR" issues for the owner's review; owner copies it to AOL. ←[[m08517]] ←[[m08523]]
- [2001-06-08] Storage model: Zimin Lu to Tom Halliburton (owner, Alex Huang cc): Lukens' model (paper from its developer Dr. Hugh Li) is very similar to Tom's; Zimin uses dynamic programming, Tom optimization; proposes one combined product, perhaps for sale if the owner agrees. ←[[m08491]]
- [2001-06-06] Brown Bag Lunch on Option Pricing (Alex Huang, Zimin): Fri 8 Jun noon, 19C2; Sevil demos the PJM Bid Profiler (Delphi and Perl), which North East traders use for unit-specific bids in the PJM and NePool auctions. ←[[m08402]]
- [2001-06-08] Nelson Neale to Eugenio Perez: Dickey-Fuller and ADF tests on housing starts (1959-2001, 1984-2001, 1996-2001): stationary, except possibly 1984-2001; fine for an AR model on the full series. ←[[m08487]]
- [2001-06-06] August Board revaluation of international assets: Maureen Raymond's group (Gwyn Koepke) makes FX and inflation curves for RAC (Cindy Hudler, David Gorte). London Q2 (Paddy Thuraisingham): US CPI, GBP/TKL/ITL/EUR/CHF/PLN vs USD, CPI for Italy, Switzerland, Poland. Hudler adds India, Philippines, Bolivia, Jamaica, Guatemala, Venezuela, Colombia, Panama, Puerto Rico, Euro inflation; Andre Cangucu prunes unused curves. Owner copies it to AOL 8 Jun. ←[[m08419]] ←[[m08526]]
