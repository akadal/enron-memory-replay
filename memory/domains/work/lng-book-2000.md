# LNG book, Elba Island and LNG tankers (2000)

- [2000-05-15] John Sherriff (Eastern Hemisphere trading plus LNG for now) wants the owner "in the loop on all the" LNG issues: curves, ship values. ←[[m01679]] ←[[m01681]]
- [2000-05-15] Elba Island (17 years plus 5): the owner sets up its book with Sally Beck's people and RAC, later all LNG positions, and values Enron's options and the hedge. View: a tolling deal on the LNG-gas spread, "significant logistical and operational risk"; "I would not recommend locking-in only one leg of the spread."; cover ~50% of volumes for 5 years; "big prudence reserve in case we mark it to market." ←[[m01681]] ←[[m01689]]
- [2000-05-22] Tankers and EXMAR: Gorte's DASH for the used Mystic Lady (Hoegh Galleon), then a new-build second tanker; Sherriff wants the EXMAR ships valued fast as stand-alone longs (historical vol, daily VaR); Joe Gold: $140M a ship is a good price, do the deal. ←[[m01682]] ←[[m01701]] ←[[m01753]] ←[[m01755]]
- [2000-06-16] Marg's Enron LNG Project NPV model (via Eric Groves, who wants to meet the owner, then Shankman, Tue): inflow (Henry Hub + Elba basis) x volumes from Qatar, Algeria, Venezuela; outflow Elba COS, Newbuild and Hoegh Galleon charters, terminaling, voyages, FOB. HH $3.55 to 2003, then $2.90; FOB $1.00 (Qatar, Algeria), $2.30 (Venezuela); 7.6 cargoes/yr Qatar, 13.9 Algeria (Newbuild from 1/1/03, capped by Elba). Gap: Newbuild voyage costs should fall 1/1/04 with Venezuela; Lake Charles left out. ←[[m02002]]
