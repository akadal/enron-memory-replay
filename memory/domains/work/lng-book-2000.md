- [2000-08-28] Floating LNG terminal for California (Scott Neal's question): Charlie Weldon finds none exists (Todd Peterson, Enron Global LNG); Tecnomare (Italy) has an unbuilt design, 2-3 years to build, heavy operational and environmental hurdles. ←[[m03032]]
- [2000-08-29] Hyundai Merchant Marine LNG draft DASH (Rick Buy's group; Michael Tribolet: positive NPV, but many trials negative on defaults; per Frevert, LNG goes with project Summer, else to Shankman). Owner to Shankman: "I don't think this transaction makes sense." (no more exposure in one area; capital works better without illiquid long-term positions). ←[[m03056]]
- [2000-08-30] LNG "Book" meeting set for Wed 6 Sep 2:00 (Beck, Groves, Doug Arnell, Gibner, Hagelmann, owner). 6 Sep owner to Groves: "I think we can skip the meeting and discuss any issues between us."; Arnell had asked for it, and Shankman sees no need for formal meetings: "we can ask them for the information directly on as needed basis." ←[[m03066]] ←[[m03073]] ←[[m03080]] ←[[m03118]]
- [2000-09-06] Groves hands over his LNG models "as I left them" (30 Aug; data from the LNG curves file); the owner passes them to Gibner. Groves' new LNG shipping model (Merritt Thomas adds ports and nautical miles) awaits Research's review; owner: "We are looking at the model. I shall get back to you next week." ←[[m03129]] ←[[m03137]]
