# LNG book, Elba Island and LNG tankers (2000)

Sherriff's role: memory/domains/organization/corporate-announcements-2000.md.

- [2000-05-15] John Sherriff (Eastern Hemisphere trading plus LNG for now, after Greg's move) wants status on LNG curves and ship values before a 19 May ship decision and the owner "in the loop on all the" LNG issues. ←[[m01679]] ←[[m01681]]
- [2000-05-15] Elba Island (17 years plus a 5-year extension; marginal fob cargos): the owner sets up its book with Doug Rotenberbg, Brad Hitch, Scott Earnest (Sally Beck's organisation) and RAC, later all Enron LNG positions; his group values Enron's options in the deal and the hedging strategy. View: essentially a tolling deal on the LNG-gas spread, "we have a significant logistical and operational risk in this transaction."; "I would not recommend locking-in only one leg of the spread."; cover about 50% of LNG volumes for 5 years with the US gas leg locked; "big prudence reserve in case we mark it to market."; long-term LNG prices follow the Japanese Crude Cocktail, close to Brent. Copied to Gorte, Rick Buy, Ted Murphy. ←[[m01681]] ←[[m01689]]
- [2000-05-16] Tankers: Gorte sends the used Mystic Lady's (now Hoegh Galleon) DASH, not detailed (short time; Enron may end the charter for convenience). Sherriff thought it and Elba were approved and executed; the 19 May decision is a second, new-build tanker: "There is no DASH on this 2nd tanker yet."; facts to Joe Sutton before he leaves for Paris; call with Sherriff 16 May, 1 pm. ←[[m01682]] ←[[m01689]] ←[[m01690]] ←[[m01701]]
- [2000-05-16] EXMAR: owner asks Farhad to cc Sherriff (London) on its DASH once official; Sherriff's EXMAR credit-rating question went to Debbie Brackett, "Bill Bradford" being on vacation ((?) alias of William Bradford unconfirmed; question closed by status quo 2000-05-21, kept apart). Bjorn Hagelmann's "LNG Analysis" forwarded to Masson and Gibner. ←[[m01705]] ←[[m01706]] ←[[m01697]]
