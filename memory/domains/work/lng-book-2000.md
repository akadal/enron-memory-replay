- [2000-07-07] Shankman asks the owner and Eric Groves to review LNG models (from Rotenberg's LNG group via Merritt Thomas, unreviewed) and show them to Farzad. ←[[m02280]]
- [2000-07-19] Eric Groves' contract summaries (to owner, Farzad Farhangnia, Shankman, Scott Earnest): Elba Island term sheet, Hoegh Galleon and EXMAR charters, the option on vessels 3 and 4; Eco-Electrica and Jose next. ←[[m02421]]
- [2000-08-28] Floating LNG terminal for California (Scott Neal's question): Charlie Weldon finds none exists (Todd Peterson, Enron Global LNG); Tecnomare (Italy) has an unbuilt design, 2-3 years to build, heavy operational and environmental hurdles. ←[[m03032]]
- [2000-08-29] Hyundai Merchant Marine LNG draft DASH (Rick Buy's group; Michael Tribolet: positive NPV, but many trials negative on defaults; per Frevert, LNG goes with project Summer, else to Shankman). Owner to Shankman: "I don't think this transaction makes sense." (no more exposure in one area; capital works better without illiquid long-term positions). ←[[m03056]]
- [2000-08-30] LNG "Book" meeting set for Wed 6 Sep 2:00, EB19C2 (Beck, Eric Groves, Doug Arnell, Gibner, owner; Bjorn Hagelmann added at the owner's request; 30 Aug not feasible); Groves asks for an earlier hour so London can join (8 pm there). ←[[m03066]] ←[[m03073]] ←[[m03080]]
