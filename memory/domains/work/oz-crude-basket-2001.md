# Oz crude basket option model (Aug 2001)

Basket option models: memory/domains/work/crude-oil-digital-option.md.

- [2001-08-09] Randy O'Connor (Director, Origination, Enron Global Markets) wants Research (not fundamentals) to build an options valuation model for the Oz crude basket: under the MOU Enron supplies ~100 MBD, 25 MBD in each of 4 classes at fixed differentials to WTI (Light Sweet/Domestic Sweet, Heavy Sweet/Cabinda, Light Sour/Basrah Light or Mesa 30, Heavy Sour/Maya; other grades by relative refining value), any grade substitutable within its class; FOB offers on an 8-year basis; data may be thin. Marc De la Roche calls it huge, asks Kohli and Shanbhogue to kick off; owner: "We already have a basket option model that we can adapt for this transaction." ←[[m08896]]
- [2001-08-30] Shanbhogue discusses the Oz Deal in Sandeep's office, then sees the owner at 5:30 (Anita DuPont arranging): "5:30 is fine." ←[[m09134]]
