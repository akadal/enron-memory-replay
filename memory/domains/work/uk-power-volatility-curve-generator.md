# UK power volatility curve generator

- [1999-12-22] Anjam Ahmad reported a new UK power volatility curve generator, EFA/monthly detail, marked to market up to 6 years out; beyond that volatility decays to an assumed long-term level. ←[[m00098]]
- [1999-12-22] Swaption traders can fit the first 5-6 years to baseload swaption implied vols (3-12 month underlying swaps) and price other swaptions, including single EFA slots, consistently. Curves reset with the market, enabling mark-to-market of the vol book; they may change daily VaR. ←[[m00098]]
- [1999-12-22] Model: T:\READWRTE\ELEC_UK\MODELS\VOLCURVEGENERATORNEW.XLS, sent to Houston for review. Ahmad asked [[stinson-gibner]] for an opinion or audit; the owner was copied. ←[[m00098]]
