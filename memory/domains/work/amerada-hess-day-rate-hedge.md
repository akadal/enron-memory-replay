# Amerada Hess day rate hedge

- [2000-01-04] John Peyton sent the owner historical 4th and 5th generation semi-submersible day rates. The rig price is 155,000/day for the next 4 years; he suggested a 100,000/day floor for 1 Jan 2001 to 31 Dec 2004 and asked for the call strike that makes the hedge costless. He planned illustrative examples for Amerada Hess on Friday and asked how to treat highs and lows in the quotes and what else the statistical analysis needs. Copied: Chris Hilgert, Fred Lagrasta, Randal Maffett, W. Byargeon. ←[[m00141]]
- [2000-01-07] Peyton sent the remaining Gulf of Mexico day rates (adding Sept-Dec 1999); he and Hilgert await the owner's analysis, to be discussed Tuesday. ←[[m00187]]
- [2000-01-07→] Open: the owner's analysis and the strike are not in the mail so far. ←[[m00187]]
