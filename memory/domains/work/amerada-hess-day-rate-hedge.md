# Amerada Hess day rate hedge

- [2000-01-04] John Peyton (cc Chris Hilgert, Fred Lagrasta, Randal Maffett, W Tom Byargeon) sent historical 4th/5th-generation semi-submersible day rates: current 155,000/day for the next 4 years; proposed a $100,000/day floor for 1 Jan 2001–31 Dec 2004 and asked for the call strike making it costless, and how to treat highs and lows in the data. Examples for Amerada Hess on Friday. ←[[m00141]]
