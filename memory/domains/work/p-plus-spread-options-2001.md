# Koch P+ spread options book: correlation review (Jan 2001)

Desk analysis generally: memory/domains/work/desk-analysis-requests.md.

- [2001-01-29] Erac Koch P+ spread options booked in O:\research\common\projects\exotic2001_0125.xls, sheet 'index deals' (Bob Lee, Issler, Zimin Lu). Mark Fondren: P+ = WTI Cushing cash (Nymex) minus Koch's West Texas/New Mexico Intermediate posting; posting = WTI settle minus Koch/NYMEX basis, 66.6% of the Apr/May and 33.3% of the Apr/Jun spread (24 Jan example: posting 24.794, P+ 3.516). ←[[m05621]]
- [2001-01-29] Gibner to Shankman (cc the owner): booking correlations have dropped sharply from a year ago; John Mee wanted 1.0, the model allowed 0.999; Research computes historical ones. Shankman (cc Nowlan, Schroeder): meet in the next couple of days. ←[[m05626]]
- [2001-01-31] Gibner's chart: calendar-spread correlations over four horizons usually above 0.98, often near 0.99; re-marking the current "index P+" book at 98% would cut M-T-M by about $78 million. ←[[m05730]]
- [2001-04-11] Gibner to Mark Fondren (cc Scott Earnest, owner) after reviewing the booking: P+ options use a two-commodity spread option model (correlated GBM, Black-76 extended); call payoff Max(0, A - B - K), A = NXWTI, B = WTI swap minus posting basis (a basket, handled through leg B's vol and correlation), K = posting bonus; simpler than a 3-4 commodity model and appropriate if inputs are set right. ←[[m06734]] ←[[m06770]]
