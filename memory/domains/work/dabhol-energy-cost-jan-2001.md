# Dabhol energy cost vs captive generation (Jan 2001)

India team and DPC: memory/domains/work/dabhol-power-sale.md. Henwood dispatch study: memory/domains/work/india-power-study-henwood.md.

- [2001-01-04] Owner to Jeff Skilling: Sandeep's numbers to follow; "It is obvious that the value of lost production exceeds the cost of power produced by the Dhabol plant". FT, 3 Jan: Maharashtra reviews the tariff (MSEB off-take 60% vs a 90% obligation; Rs4.94/kWh average, Rs6.91 in October); phase 2 (1,624MW, LNG, $1.87bn) due this year; MSEB took half its 30% equity option, Enron seeks a buyer; 2 Jan northern grid failure: "The power cut affected 230 million people." ←[[m05150]]
- [2001-01-04] Kohli's spreadsheet: savings of Dabhol power over diesel gensets (higher heat rate, diesel-naphtha differential; DPC's September bill); 610 MW of captive generation in Maharashtra (Mohan Gurunath); press note without actual numbers, as the press would seize on Dabhol's 6 cent/kWh. ←[[m05160]]
- [2001-01-05] Kohli's more detailed genset numbers and revised press statement; they understate the issue (no genset capital costs, no small home and commercial units). ←[[m05166]]
- [2001-01-05] Gibner sends Skilling and Katherine Brown (cc the owner) Kohli's analysis, reviewed by Gibner and the owner: Dabhol's energy cost is several cents below that of ~600 MW of Maharashtra distributed generation; innumerable smaller generators cost more still. ←[[m05171]]
