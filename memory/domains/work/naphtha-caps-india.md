# Naphtha cap pricing for Enron India

Background: naphtha prices more than doubled in 1999; fuel cost is a pass-through, so Maharashtra's
power bill to Enron drew press and political fire. The India team (Anshuman Srivastav, Rajesh
Sivaraman, Sandeep Kohli) asked the group to price naphtha caps. ←[[m00007]]

- [1999-12-10] Krishna Krishnarao's results, 1999 prices as forwards, 2-yr historical vol 34%: ATM cap 10.4% of avg 2000 price (13% if FP=$224/MT), 10% OTM 6.8%, 20% OTM 4.4%, $200 flat cap 9%; i.e. 10-15% to cap at current levels, indicative only. ←[[m00007]]
- [1999-12-10→] Open: real 2000 naphtha forwards and vols from John L Chismar (naphtha trader, Singapore); Exotic Options Library to be installed on the India office's shared drive. ←[[m00007]]
