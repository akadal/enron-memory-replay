# Naphtha cap pricing for Enron India

Detail ←[[naphtha-caps-india-2001-01-w01]]

Background: naphtha prices more than doubled in 1999; fuel cost is a pass-through, so Maharashtra's
power bill to Enron drew press and political fire. The India team (Anshuman Srivastav, Rajesh
Sivaraman, Sandeep Kohli) asked the group to price naphtha caps. ←[[m00007]]

- [1999-12-10] Krishna Krishnarao's results, 1999 prices as forwards, 2-yr historical vol 34%: ATM cap 10.4% of avg 2000 price (13% if FP=$224/MT), 10% OTM 6.8%, 20% OTM 4.4%, $200 flat cap 9%; i.e. 10-15% to cap at current levels, indicative only. ←[[m00007]]
- [2001-01-03] Kohli to James Hughes and Wade Cline: DPC Phase II Blocks B and C must first run on naphtha for 3-6 months in 2001 (longer if the regas terminal slips), so naphtha is more urgent than LNG; 50% of the DPC tariff is fuel-driven; naphtha is more volatile and thinner than crude. The DPC Board's Risk Management Policy allows hedging: start with costless options (sell put, buy call), small while only Block A runs, with Global Markets, seconding someone like Anshuman. EFI (Tushar Dhruv, 2 Jan): an opportune time to hedge 2001 naphtha (backwardated crude). ←[[m05139]]
