- [2001-08-16] Brian Mihura's lab sensor boards compute current flow on HVDC lines (six into WSCC) to detect inter-regional flows; he could test at the HVDC tie north of Dallas if there is a market; owner: Martin Lin "knows infinitely more about transmission than I do."; 17 Aug Lin: NY ISO posts hourly tie flows (good for calibration), ERCOT only long after; of great value once field-proven; owner passes it to Mihura. ←[[m08956]] ←[[m08958]] ←[[m08970]]
- [2001-10-19] Frank Wolak (Stanford) hears Enron endorses PJM's nodal pricing: he backs locational pricing, but PJM's tariff lets the ISO mitigate any bid it deems out of merit to cost plus 10%, which suppresses volatility and transparency and kills the value of Enron's risk management; FERC's big East Coast RTO would extend that. Owner to Martin Lin and Lance Cunningham: "What do you think?" ←[[m09400]] ←[[m09403]] ←[[m09412]]
- [2001-10-19] Lin: in ERCOT Enron backs nodal prices with unilateral bids (unbalanced schedules) and congestion priced by differentials, not PJM's bid mitigation, which it has not advocated; the rumour likely comes from PJM being the stock example of locational pricing; Wolak makes good points. ←[[m09411]] ←[[m09412]]
