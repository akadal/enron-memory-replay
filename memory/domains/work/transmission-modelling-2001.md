- [2001-10-19] Lin: in ERCOT Enron backs nodal prices with unilateral bids (unbalanced schedules) and congestion priced by differentials, not PJM's bid mitigation, which it has not advocated; the rumour likely comes from PJM being the stock example of locational pricing; Wolak makes good points. ←[[m09411]] ←[[m09412]]
- [2001-10-25] ERCOT TCR derating: Lance Cunningham and Martin Lin's Enron position paper for the ROS meeting 30 Oct (cc the owner, Shanbhogue, Michael Roan); Doug Gilbert-Smith and David Portz (Legal) to comment by day's end so it reaches the WMS a week ahead; Lin: a revised version replaces it; owner copies it to AOL. ←[[m09597]] ←[[m09604]] ←[[m09648]]
- [2001-11-02] Lin (with Lance) at ERCOT's WMS on TCR design, 30 Oct: most votes Enron's way: 100% of each CSC limit as non-deratable TCRs (60% annual auction, rest monthly), CREs set ahead like CSCs; lost: one entity may not buy all TCRs in a primary auction (little market effect). ←[[m09794]]
