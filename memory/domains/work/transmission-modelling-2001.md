- [2001-12-19] Owner copies to AOL the Alliance of Energy Suppliers' (EEI) call for comments, by COB 19 Dec, on Bill Hogan and Steve Harvey's draft on the market impacts of FERC's Market Based Rate initiatives and remedies (for the EEI/Alliance filing); Tonja Wicks: it opposes price caps (against some wires members), she dislikes its divestiture and minimum interconnection proposals and wants the theory in an appendix; one comment: incremental cost should include accelerated depreciation. ←[[m10337]]
- [2001-12-21] PJM: PJM West will not start on 1 Jan 2002, as FERC has not given final approval; it starts on the first of a month at least 30 days after approval (Allegheny's lead time); FERC rejected the 'cleanup' filing on procedure on 19 Dec but endorsed the concept. ←[[m10415]]
