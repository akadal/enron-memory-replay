# Mark Lay's distributed computing question (Aug 2000)

- [2000-08-22] Mark Lay (Enron Investment Partners), 'Seeking Intelligent Insight', to the owner and Philippe Bibi: distributed computing will displace heavy iron within several years, with openings for Enron in bandwidth and electricity; greatest hindrances and possibilities? Owner: "I see  great progress in software applications that facilitate the process and make it almost painless to the end-user."; "With you permission, I can show the material you sent me to my son" ("my son, who studies computer science"); the request goes to his AOL (again 24 Aug). ←[[m02942]] ←[[m02946]] ←[[m02947]] ←[[m02996]]
- [2000-08-23] Lay agrees; his only risk is potential embarrassment. ←[[m02953]]
