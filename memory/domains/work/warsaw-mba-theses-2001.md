# Warsaw MBA diploma theses supervised by the owner (2001)

People: memory/people/tomasz-blach.md. Older Polish MBA leads: memory/domains/organization/recruiting-candidates.md.

- [2001-01-29] Tomasz Błach (scarbeko@entropia.com.pl), 5th-edition MBA at Wyższa Szkoła Handlu i Finansów Międzynarodowych, Warsaw: the owner supervises his international finance thesis with Jerzy Seremak's and Waldemar Mróz's; sent in Nov 2000 without confirmation. His consulting firm, preparing a 150 MW plant with Lahmeyer International, asks whether Enron would invest in a power plant in Ukraine. ←[[m05642]] ←[[m05647]]
- [2001-01-30] Theses resent: Błach's (rtf), Mróz's (Kontroling.doc) and Seremak's (first sent 28 Nov 2000; asks for a review, defence in February); the owner copies them to AOL. ←[[m05661]] ←[[m05662]] ←[[m05663]] ←[[m05664]] ←[[m05674]]
- [2001-01-31] Owner to Błach: "Prosze poinformowac kolegow, ze przesle moje uwagi do niedzieli." (comments by Sunday). ←[[m05686]] ←[[m05711]]
- [2001-02-12] Owner confirms in writing (to msh_isc@it.com.pl, cc Seremak): "Niniejszym potwierdzam, ze nastepujacy studenci zlozyli prace dyplomowe"; Seremak's (financial analysis tools, short and long term), Mróz's 'Controlling w przedsiębiorstwie' and T. Blach's 'Analiza wskaźnikowa' all graded 'Bardzo dobra'. ←[[m05836]]
