# GRMS, the Global Risk Monitoring System

- [2000-01-28] Ted Murphy (from Bjorn Hagelmann's account; to William Bradford, Jonathan Le, Gary Hickerson, Philippe Bibi and the owner, cc Rick Buy, Mike McConnell) objected to a meeting meant to divert resources from GRMS. GRMS is a multi-phase risk warehouse built mainly for RAC, because front-office systems cannot give timely aggregated risk; he and Bradford need it to do their jobs. ←[[m00456]] ←[[m00475]]
- [2000-01-28] Priorities: Debbie Brackett and Rudi Zipter (delegated authority) meet Jonathan's team and Rick Buy. Deadlines that relied only on RAC, Jonathan's team and Research were hit; slips came from diverted resources and outside dependencies, lately London IT. Other groups should send detailed requirements to RAC's queue or fund their own project. ←[[m00456]] ←[[m00475]]
- [2000-01-28] Murphy: Europe's systems could not aggregate risk, causing cash losses well above the GRMS budget; the core problem is lack of management support, and several employees resigned over it. Owner forwarded it to his AOL 30 Jan. ←[[m00456]] ←[[m00475]]
