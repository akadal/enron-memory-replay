# Crude oil downside-insurance model

- [2000-01-13] [[zimin-lu]] told Fred Lagrasta (Stinson Gibner and the owner copied) he finished the model discussed days earlier: a digital option on an Asian strip that pays a lump sum if the average prompt-month crude price over a window ends below the strike, plus a European option on the same strip for comparison.
