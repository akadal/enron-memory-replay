# Argentina modelling meeting with Michael Guerriero

- [1999-12-30] Michael Guerriero (Enron Development), who says he is responsible for Enron's Argentina operation, had asked on 1999-12-20 for a meeting on modelling issues. The owner, back from the holidays, answered he would meet him in the first week of January (owner, verbatim: "I shall be glad to meet with you in the 1st week of January.") and gave his own number 3-3848 and [[shirley-crenshaw]]'s 3-5290 for scheduling. ←[[m00121]]
- [2000-01-05] The owner proposed 1:00 p.m. Thursday (owner, verbatim: "What about 1:00 p.m. Thursday?"). ←[[m00161]]
- [2000-01-06] Thursday turned out full; the owner offered Friday 1 to 2 (owner, verbatim: "Sorry, Thursday schedule is full. What about Fri, 1 to 2?"). ←[[m00169]]
- [2000-01-07] The owner declined Friday too, all his slots that day being taken, and asked Guerriero to call [[shirley-crenshaw]] (3-5290) to set another day. ←[[m00177]]
- [2000-01-07→] Open: no date fixed; the modelling issues themselves are not in the mail so far. ←[[m00177]]
