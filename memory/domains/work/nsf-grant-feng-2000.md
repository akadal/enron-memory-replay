# NSF grant proposal: Youyi Feng and Baichun Xiao (2000)

Feng's group presentations: memory/domains/work/research-presentations.md.

- [2000-09-08] Youyi Feng prepares an NSF grant proposal with co-PI Prof. Baichun Xiao (Long Island University); the owner, he says, agreed to help seek the funding. Feng asks that Enron register with NSF as an institution (IBM and Lucent are): Enron's external-grants person calls FastLane User Support (1-800-673-6188), free per Xiao; every proposal goes through FastLane after 1 Oct 2000. An Enron official then sends each researcher's name, degree, institution, address, SSN, e-mail, phone and fax, and NSF issues a FastLane password. The owner forwards it to his AOL (8 and 9 Sep). ←[[m03171]] ←[[m03172]] ←[[m03180]]
- [2000-09-06] Feng sends Xiao the revised first four pages (Project Summary, Project Description). ←[[m03171]]
