# EnronOnline Trading Algorithm effort and Research (Apr 2001)

Detail ←[[eol-trading-algorithms-2001-2001-04-w17b]]

- [2001-04-23] Bob Lee's draft EOL data request on the P&L of the "George" pricing algorithm (to the owner, Shanbhogue, Tom Barkley), for Andy Zipper and Jay Webb that afternoon; Vasant: ask Enron's position after limit orders too, not only market orders (the desk's target position is not stored), and the bid-mid-ask path from order to execution or rejection. ←[[m07075]] ←[[m07102]]
- [2001-04-23] Clayton Vernon, after talking to Tom, hands all EOL work to the owner: full physical and administrative custody of the EOL data, kept confidential; an unused EBS Sun server in Martin Lin's office (more RAM, newest Solaris, Tibco, GNU, SAS) to listen to the EOL feeds and keep the database; Vernon gives SAS, the EOL software and the RAM free, the owner finds a SysAdmin (Unix admin Ben Thompson). ←[[m07076]] ←[[m07103]]
- [2001-04-26] Tom Barkley pulls the EOL crude trades from Clayton's database (O:\Research\Common\From_BobLee); Bob Lee asks the owner, Gibner, Shanbhogue and Zimin Lu for analysis ideas. 27 Apr Barkley's 'Crude Data' file (O:\_Dropbox\Tom Barkley): WTI swaps only, US delivery, from 1 Jan 2001, 24,000+ trades; no failed or limit-order trades, no spread or basis options. ←[[m07248]] ←[[m07262]] ←[[m07302]] ←[[m07328]]
