- [2001-05-11] Zimin Lu's literature search on bid-offer spread formation (67 journals via Elsevier's Econ search; e.g. Bollerslev-Domowitz-Wang 1997, Chung-Van Ness 1999) to Bob Lee: very relevant; Lee to find out how to subscribe; owner copies it to AOL. ←[[m07844]]
- [2001-05-29] Zimin Lu's option approach to EOL price setting: the market maker is paid for liquidity as if selling options (hit ask = call exercised, lifted bid = put), premium inside the spread; charged carry at the risk-free rate and risk capital at a RAROC rate; bid and ask as a function of last price, inventory, volatility, trade size and order arrival rate; limit orders ignored (few on EOL); write-up for the owner to come. ←[[m08032]]
