- [2001-05-29] Zimin Lu's option approach to EOL price setting: the market maker is paid for liquidity as if selling options (hit ask = call exercised, lifted bid = put), premium inside the spread; charged carry at the risk-free rate and risk capital at a RAROC rate; bid and ask as a function of last price, inventory, volatility, trade size and order arrival rate; limit orders ignored (few on EOL); write-up for the owner to come. ←[[m08032]]
- [2001-06-04] Lu sends Bob Lee an 'Update on EOL bid-ask formula' (cc owner, Alex Huang); owner copies it to AOL. ←[[m08287]]
- [2001-06-08] Bob Lee's market-maker discussions on bid-ask spread models (owner, Huang, Supatgiat, Rabi De, Bharati, Barkley, Shanbhogue, Lu): next Fri 15 Jun 2-3 pm, 19C2, Jinbaek Kim presents papers. Owner sends Kim's summary of the 7 Jun session to Whalley ("Greg Whalley, my boss"): "notes from a regular weekly meeting with one of our summer interns", a literature review on the drivers of bid-offer spreads; then tells Kim "There is still an error in the definition of the bid-offer spread."; Kim had confused bid and ask and corrects it. ←[[m08479]] ←[[m08481]] ←[[m08493]] ←[[m08496]] ←[[m08504]]
