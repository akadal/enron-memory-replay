# EnronOnline Trading Algorithm effort and Research (Apr 2001)

- [2001-04-19] Andy Zipper's meeting summary (18 Apr, to Jay Webb and the owner): Research keeps custom reporting and market analysis off EnronOnline data and gives one resource to the Trading Algorithm effort (Zipper would prefer Vasant), run by the EOL team with Research reviewing its product; such projects need "a firm commercial hand" early. Owner: "Vasant is swamped with work coordinating several high profile projects."; Bob takes it, backed by the group: "Bob is very productive and thorough"; direct, frequent interaction with the customer is the key condition of success. ←[[m06989]] ←[[m07018]]
