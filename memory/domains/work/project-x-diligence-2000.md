# Project X: trading-system diligence in Sydney, Dec 2000

Enron Australia generally: memory/domains/work/enron-australia-2000.md.

- [2000-11-29] Gary Hickerson (after talking to the owner) wants Gibner in Sydney on Monday to review the 'X' system; Paul Quilkey to line up the contacts and a signed confidentiality agreement (cc Shankman); owner: "Stinson will contact you in the morning, Thu, regarding the trip."; 30 Nov John Norden joins Monday to assess the IT side. ←[[m04525]] ←[[m04526]] ←[[m04536]]
- [2000-12-01] Gibner's and the owner's two-day diligence plan (to Quilkey): audited history of the strategies (profit, P/L volatility); working and risk capital against projected income; market access after the change of ownership; new markets (commodity synergy doubtful, thin electronic liquidity) and transfer to Enron's less liquid markets; how fast strategies decay once copied, size against slippage, how new ones are found; with more time, run the system on test data. ←[[m04587]]
