# Ken Lay's speech: energy costs vs the Bush tax cut

- [2001-01-02] For Ken Lay's speech (Alhamd Alkhayat, Steven Kean, Margaret Carson; the owner copied), Maureen Raymond: Bush's tax cut is $460bn over five years, part of the press's $1.3tn ten-year figure; the $213bn 'energy tax' of higher energy prices 1999-2001 is about half the five-year cut; she also sends Alkhayat PPI data. ←[[m05095]] ←[[m05096]] ←[[m05097]] ←[[m05098]]
- [2001-06-06] After the Research PRC, Maureen Raymond disputes the owner's comments on her Ken Lay item: her group briefed Lay on Bush's tax proposal for a White House trip with Steve Kean (presentation material, a briefing rather than a speech); Kean and Lay were pleased; she rejects his view that calling it a 'speech' undermines her credibility and "prides" herself on honesty. ←[[m08414]] ←[[m08417]]
