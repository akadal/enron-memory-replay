# Enron Credit: Iris Mack's London report and tensions in EC, mid-May 2001

Iris's London work before the trip: memory/domains/work/enron-credit-london-may-2001.md. Private-firm model: memory/domains/work/private-firm-credit-model-2001.md.

- [2001-05-16] Jeff Kinneman asks Iris for a summary of her London trip, Friday 10 am (subject: 10:30); who from Research attends? Owner: "Vasant, Amitava, myself." ←[[m07893]]
- [2001-05-18] Iris's observations (owner forwards them): 2-3 quants under many non-quant managers, the head quant is not a quant, a data team without data; credit models estimate CDS prices for 10,000 names on the EC website from fewer than 692 trader-made prices, rating maps and confidence intervals unanalysed; quant Ben admits fudging under trader pressure, fearing the 30 Jul deadline (1000 firm, 4000 indicative names); 10 May trader Nick calls the asset swap model wrong, Ilyan overwrites the FMC model each morning; big marketing budget, rude treatment of strategy critics. ←[[m07930]]
- [2001-05-18] George Albanis ('No deal - Time to move !'): starts his own public-firm model in early June, own methodology, common 15 Jul deadline, first credit models early July; reports to Scott Salmon; takes technical feedback only from Iris, Leppard, Dhaliwal, Danilov, Vasant, Seyfried, Dhar and the owner; otherwise rotation or he leaves at once, and a complaint against those who asked him to slow down. Owner to Kinneman: "Another indication of tensions in EC." ←[[m07926]] ←[[m07929]]
- [2001-05-17] Iris meets John Dowling (VP, Loan Pricing Corporation, Reuters): US loan database since 1988 with default and non-default loans, starting Europe; public and private firm models (PDs at 1-5 years), $75,000 a year; details to follow. ←[[m07919]]
