# Pricing naphtha caps for Maharashtra (India)

Context: Indian press and politicians object to the high power prices Maharashtra's state
government pays Enron; naphtha more than doubled in 1999 and fuel cost is a floating pass-through.
Maharashtra asked Enron to help price caps on naphtha.

- [1999-12-10] [[pinnamaneni-krishnarao]] forwarded the analysis to the owner ("I can't believe they let it floating!"). ←[[m00007]]
- [1999-12-10] Indicative 2000 monthly cap premiums, % of average price: ATM 10.4% (13% if forward $224/MT); OTM 5/10/15/20% = 8.4/6.8/5.5/4.4%; fixed caps $168 15%, $200 9%, $225 6%. 1999 prices used as forwards; 2-yr historical vol 34%. ←[[m00007]]
- [1999-12-10] Advice: 10-15% premium to protect against moves up from the current level; indicative until real forwards are obtained. ←[[m00007]]
- [1999-12-10] Next steps asked of Anshuman Srivastav, Rajesh Sivaraman, Sandeep Kohli (Enron Development): get 2000 forwards and vols from John L Chismar (Singapore naphtha trader); install the Exotic Options Library on a common drive. Anshuman supplied 1987-99 monthly FOB naphtha prices. ←[[m00007]]
