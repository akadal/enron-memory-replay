# Valuing an incumbent shipper's right of first refusal (ROFR)

Detail ←[[rofr-valuation-2000-09-w37]]

- [2000-01-20] Owner to Shelley Corman (Vernon's essay against ROFR): the value is case specific, "so we shall rather produce a template you can use for valuation case by case." ←[[m00394]]
- [2000-01-21] Zimin Lu delivered the template to Corman and Chris Gaskill: the ROFR as a series of forward-start options, ATM strike mimicking "matching the best bid"; the underlying is the best bid (tied to the spread between the two hubs the pipeline links), volatility from best-bid history; contacts Zimin Lu and the owner. ←[[m00414]]
- [2000-02-04] After a meeting with Gaskill and Corman, Zimin Lu added a switch: the pipeline can accept or reject the best bid when it is not the cap rate. ←[[m00543]]
- [2000-09-11] Zimin Lu to Lorraine Lindberg and Michelle Lokay (cc owner, Gibner): a ROFR locking in the max transport rate for years is a spread swaption; priced for strips from 1 Nov 2000 (54 days to decision) and 1 Nov 2001 (419 days, more time value). ←[[m03190]]
