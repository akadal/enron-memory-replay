# Valuing an incumbent shipper's right of first refusal (ROFR)

- [2000-01-18] Clayton Vernon's argument: price-matching and right-of-first-refusal clauses kill the incentive to bid; outsiders skip due diligence because good offers get matched, so shares or capacity become illiquid. ←[[m00384]]
- [2000-01-20] Owner sent Shelley Corman Vernon's summary against ROFR (cc Gibner, Zimin Lu): the incumbent "has something of value he never paid for"; the value is case specific, "so we shall rather produce a template you can use for valuation case by case." Essay: competitors low-ball, the incumbent matches by rule of thumb and keeps below-market contracts; a winning challenger has almost surely overbid (winner's curse). ←[[m00394]]
- [2000-01-21] Zimin Lu delivered the template to Corman and Chris Gaskill: the ROFR as a series of forward-start options, ATM strike mimicking "matching the best bid"; the underlying is the best bid (tied to the spread between the two hubs the pipeline links), volatility from best-bid history; contacts Zimin Lu and the owner. ←[[m00414]]
