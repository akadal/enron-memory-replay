# Cairn gas purchase bid, India (Aug 2000)

Naphtha caps for Enron India: memory/domains/work/naphtha-caps-india.md

- [2000-08-11] Douglas S Parsons (Enron Development, for Metgas) offers Cairn (India), non-binding, via Bobby Farris (cc F B Virani): six-year plateau, 85% take-or-pay, $3.67/MMBtu net at $18/bbl Brent, +/- $0.145 per $1/bbl (3-month average, fixed quarterly), floor/cap $15.50-$27.00/bbl; guarantees as for the LNG suppliers under the Trust Retention Account. ←[[m02837]] ←[[m02878]]
- [2000-08-14] Doug Leach (cc Marc De La Roche, Farris): "a bad deal", like the Brazil debacle (Cuiba); Parsons should have asked the owner's group, Michael Popkin's Southern Cone structurers, or used a price collar. Parsons: the owner only suggested calling Sandeep Kohli; he waits for Cairn's feedback; Farris sees no harm in asking Research. ←[[m02837]] ←[[m02838]] ←[[m02877]] ←[[m02890]]
- [2000-08-17] Owner to Parsons: "I shall be glad to meet you and help you with the project.", "My assistant will call you to set up a meeting latter this week."; the thread goes to Gibner ("FYI") and to his AOL. ←[[m02879]] ←[[m02880]]
