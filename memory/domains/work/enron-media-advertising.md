# Enron Media / Advertising concept (Mike Horning)

- [2000-03-16] Mike Horning met the owner on Enron Media / Advertising, developed under EBS but meant to become its own business unit (his Ken Rice deck via Simon Shih): an advertising risk book, physical trades of excess ad inventory (cable, radio, network TV), a media buying service, agency alliances selling Enron "eyeballs", Enron capital for content, an EnronOnline B2B play (see DoubleClick.com, Agency.com, AdAuction.com). Follow-up Tue 28 Mar 3:00 PM with Mark Palmer and Kal Shah. ←[[m01009]]
