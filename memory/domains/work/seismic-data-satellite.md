# Seismic data via satellite (EBS, SpaceData), Oct 2000

Broadband generally: memory/domains/work/broadband-services.md.

- [2000-10-10] Reichardt (EBS) to Mike McConnell (cc Larry Lawyer, Tom Gros): NASA satellite links and a Houston data centre for 3D seismic, over $300M a year industry savings; SpaceData's case (William K. Aylor, $22/bbl: over $26M per site from surveys 30 days sooner); "Vince's team" values exclusive data for oil futures; with Jon Adler he asks to present 17-20 Oct. Kevin Garland: maybe a business, but no investment. Owner sends it to Bob Lee. ←[[m03500]] ←[[m03501]] ←[[m03511]]
- [2000-10-10] John Bloomer (Enron Communications) pitches exploration data as the start of the oil supply chain, a hedge for trades; owner: "We asked our geophysicists for help and are shooting for a meeting on  Thursday to run our ideas by them." ←[[m03502]]
- [2000-10-13] Bob Lee drafts a summary of Thursday's talk (Gregory Smith, Reichardt, owner) for brainstorming with oil traders; asks about Enron's past speculative Gulf of Mexico survey (terms, return, why stopped) and the oil price deep-water projects need (most 3-D surveys are deep water). ←[[m03609]]
