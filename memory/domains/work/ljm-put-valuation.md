# LJM valuation (RTHM put, LJM options, credit loss)

- [1999-12-27] Stinson Gibner sent Wes Colwell (owner copied) a spreadsheet valuing the RTHM put position, subject "LJM put valuation". ←[[m00103]]
- [2000-02-14] Paulo Issler sent the owner LJM pricing without and with credit risk (two-factor model); Ryan Siurek (Accounting) is to use the updated option spreadsheet (same results, code moved to the "o" drive). ←[[m00618]] ←[[m00630]]
- [2000-02-16] Issler showed the credit model to Accounting (Siurek) and Credit (Rod Nelson, positive; to brief Bill Bradford). Year-end value gap without vs with credit risk: a credit loss of about 100 MM. Accounting meets AA on the credit reserve 17 Feb without him. ←[[m00648]]
- [2000-02-24] Issler: call of AA (Jitendra) with Accounting and Credit (Wes, Bill Bradford) on year-end credit reserves for the LJM puts made little progress. Accounting argues for no reserve, as the position could not be liquidated by year-end, and seemed not to want Research's two-factor credit valuation shown; Jitendra suggested a simpler two-factor Monte Carlo. Enron is unwinding its LJM position. ←[[m00754]]
