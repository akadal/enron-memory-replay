# Clayton Vernon's gas price / transmission (basis) model

- [1999-12-15] [[clayton-vernon]] proposed a gas price model from interconnect flows (telemetry) and spot prices: a weather-driven stochastic basis model; hoarding shows as elevated basis vs. inflow, storage as inflow minus consumption, both like exotic calls at the hub. He asked the owner's agreement to start. ←[[m00025]]
- [1999-12-16] Vernon's notes from Chris Germany (East Desk capacity trader), to the owner and [[vasant-shanbhogue]]: capacity is leased monthly, e.g. South Texas→Brooklyn ~$12/month per 10,000 Dth plus 6% fuel and 6.5% overhead; with a $0.35 spread and $0.125 variable cost a trader could pay up to $0.225 for next-day firm; many pipelines don't discount firm maximums. ←[[m00042]]
- [1999-12-17] The owner forwarded the proposal to his AOL address. ←[[m00064]]
- [1999-12-22] Vernon now has a simplified version of Brad's model: no-arbitrage equates trading margins across the country; transmission cost rises with congestion; wellhead supply ~fully price-elastic, burner-tip demand ~inelastic; storage a perpetual call. ←[[m00086]]
- [1999-12-22] 8 parameters per major node, from least to most time-variant: storage injection/withdrawal costs, wellhead-to-pipeline costs, capacity-dependent transmission charges, trading margins and storage option values. Identified from the last 3 days' prices or from today's data alone (node-based pipeline competition). He will write it up in Scientific Word for the owner; Vasant copied. ←[[m00086]]
- [1999-12-21] For Scientific Word, the owner told Vernon that [[shirley-crenshaw]] must submit a formal request, which the owner approves and forwards to IT (a day or two). ←[[m00084]]
