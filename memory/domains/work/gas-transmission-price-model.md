# Clayton Vernon's gas price / transmission (basis) model

- [1999-12-15] [[clayton-vernon]] proposed a gas price model from two series — interconnect flows (telemetry) and spot prices — giving a weather-driven stochastic basis model. Capacity hoarding shows as elevated basis vs. inflow, storage as inflow minus consumption; storage and hoarding incentives behave like exotic calls at the consumption hub. He thinks it tractable and asked the owner's agreement to start. ←[[m00025]]
- [1999-12-16] Vernon's notes from Chris Germany (East Desk capacity trader), sent to the owner and [[vasant-shanbhogue]]: capacity is leased monthly, e.g. South Texas→Brooklyn ~$12/month per 10,000 Dth (~$0.40/day) plus 6% fuel and 6.5% overhead; with a $0.35 spread and $0.125 variable cost a trader could pay up to $0.225 for next-day firm capacity; many pipelines don't discount firm tariff maximums. ←[[m00042]]
- [1999-12-17] The owner forwarded the model proposal to his AOL address; no reply on the go-ahead is recorded. ←[[m00064]]
