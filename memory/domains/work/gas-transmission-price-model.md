# Clayton Vernon's gas price and transmission model

- [1999-12-15] [[clayton-vernon]] proposed a gas price model on two series, interconnect flows (telemetry) and spot prices: hoarding = elevated basis against actual inflow, storage = inflow minus consumption, each incentive an exotic call at the consumption hub. He wants to proceed (verbatim: "I believe the problem is quite tractable, and I would like to proceed with a model."). ←[[m00025]]
- [1999-12-16] Vernon met Chris Germany (East Desk): capacity is leased monthly (South Texas to Brooklyn $12.00/month per 10,000 decatherms plus fuel and overhead). Detail ←[[vernon-pipeline-capacity-notes]] ←[[m00042]]
- [1999-12-16] The owner asked Vernon to register for the Wellhead to BurnerTip course, 4-5 January 2000 (Shanbhogue copied). ←[[m00031]]
- [1999-12-27] Vernon spoke with Roy Kass of the EIA: EIA scientists see severe field weather (freezes, hurricanes) as far more significant for production and wellhead prices than Northeast weather; he clarified the lag of state wellhead estimates and agreed with the owner that marginal production costs strictly increase and wells differ widely in efficiency and investment. ←[[m00102]]
