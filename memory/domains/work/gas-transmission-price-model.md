# Clayton Vernon's gas price and transmission model

- [1999-12-15] [[clayton-vernon]] proposed a gas price model on interconnect flows (telemetry) and spot prices: hoarding = elevated basis against actual inflow, storage = inflow minus consumption, each an exotic call at the consumption hub (verbatim: "I believe the problem is quite tractable, and I would like to proceed with a model."). ←[[m00025]]
- [1999-12-16] Vernon met Chris Germany (East Desk): capacity is leased monthly (South Texas to Brooklyn $12.00/month per 10,000 decatherms plus fuel). Detail ←[[vernon-pipeline-capacity-notes]] ←[[m00042]]
- [1999-12-16] The owner asked Vernon to take the Wellhead to BurnerTip course, 4-5 January 2000 (Shanbhogue copied). ←[[m00031]]
- [1999-12-27] Roy Kass (EIA) told Vernon severe field weather (freezes, hurricanes) matters far more for production and wellhead prices than Northeast weather. ←[[m00102]]
- [2000-01-06] Vernon's storage note (from a class): Operational Balancing Agreements are spreading (pipeline handles imbalances, shippers book scheduled flows); producing-region storage smooths weekday-weekend swings; estimated marketed production likely beats production less local storage as receipt-meter flow. ←[[m00172]]
