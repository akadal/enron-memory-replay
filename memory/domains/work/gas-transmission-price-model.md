# Clayton Vernon's gas price and transmission model

- [1999-12-15] [[clayton-vernon]] proposed a gas price model built on two series, interconnect flows (telemetry) and spot prices: hoarding = elevated basis against actual inflow, storage = inflow minus consumption, each storage or hoarding incentive an exotic call at the consumption hub; flows can be imputed from prices, giving a weather-driven stochastic basis model. He wants to proceed (verbatim: "I believe the problem is quite tractable, and I would like to proceed with a model."). ←[[m00025]]
- [1999-12-16] Vernon met Chris Germany (Capacity Trader, East Desk) and reported to the owner and [[vasant-shanbhogue]]: capacity is leased monthly; South Texas to Brooklyn is $12.00/month per 10,000 decatherms fixed plus 6% fuel and 6.5% overhead. Example: spread $0.35 less $0.125 variable cost, so a trader could bid up to $0.225 for firm capacity. ←[[m00042]]
- [1999-12-16] Per Germany: leaseholders know the firm equivalent of interruptible capacity; many pipelines do not discount firm below tariff maximum; secondary markets come later; traders can cover Enron positions at each pipeline end. ←[[m00042]]
- [1999-12-16] The owner asked Vernon to register for the Wellhead to BurnerTip course, 4-5 January 2000 (Shanbhogue copied); from January most Development Center courses are charged to the employee's company and RC. ←[[m00031]]
- [1999-12-16→] The owner's answer to the model proposal is not in the mail so far. ←[[m00025]]
