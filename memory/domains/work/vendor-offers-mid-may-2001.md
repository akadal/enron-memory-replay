# Vendor offers to Research, mid-May 2001: ICAST and FEA

Week of 7 May (Limperis call, Kalotay, DRI-WEFA): memory/domains/work/vendor-offers-may-2001.md. FEA licences: memory/domains/organization/software-licenses.md.

- [2001-05-15] Tom Limperis (CEO, ICAST) drafts an approach to the two pattern recognition problems discussed by phone with the owner and Vasant: a week on Enron's data at ICAST's cost to size the job, then proof-of-concept software shown at Enron, then a joint production spec; $150K plus expenses and about 4 months per problem, possibly both at once; cites Eli Lilly and GM work; will call before the week ends, (517) 423 5617. ←[[m07882]]
- [2001-05-14] FEA (Erin Hopkins) releases @ENERGY 2.1 for licensed user the owner: Hull (1997) control variate for American options, parallel-displacement delta/gamma, a faster spread-option method (SPREADOPT and related), Swing out of beta, Weather 30-35% faster; ErgLib calls may break (ergnote.txt). 15 May the owner forwards it to Zimin Lu, who asks Christopher Jeska (cc Bob Lee) to install it. ←[[m07868]] ←[[m07876]] ←[[m07925]]
- [2001-05-17] Michelle Vasquez (FEA accounts receivable, Berkeley) asks the owner's EID number for invoices under Enron's new address; owner: "Please send this info Michelle" (to Crenshaw). ←[[m07913]]
