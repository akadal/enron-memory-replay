# Gas price / transmission model (Clayton Vernon)

- [1999-12-15] Clayton Vernon proposed to the owner a gas price model from two series, interconnect flows (telemetry) and spot prices: hoarding seen as elevated basis vs inflow, storage = inflow − consumption, elasticities calibrated from flows/prices, storage and hoarding incentives as exotic calls at the hub; flows imputable from prices, giving a weather-driven stochastic basis model. He calls it "quite tractable" and wants to proceed. ←[[m00025]]
- [1999-12-16] After meeting East Desk capacity trader Chris Germany, Vernon reported to the owner and Vasant Shanbhogue: capacity is leased monthly (e.g. South Texas→Brooklyn $12/month per 10,000 Dth fixed, plus 6% fuel and 6.5% overhead); with a $0.35 basis spread and $0.125 variable cost a trader could pay up to $0.225 for firm capacity; many pipelines do not discount firm capacity from tariff maximum; secondary markets deferred. ←[[m00042]]
