# Naphtha cap pricing for the Maharashtra power deal

- [1999-12-10] The owner forwarded to his personal address ("FYI.") a thread from [[pinnamaneni-krishnarao]]: Indian papers and politicians complain about the high power prices the Maharashtra state government pays Enron; naphtha prices more than doubled in 1999 and fuel cost is a pass-through; the Indian side asked Enron for help pricing naphtha caps. ←[[m00007]]
- [1999-12-10] Krishnarao's indicative monthly cap premiums for 2000 (sent 12-09 to Anshuman Srivastav, Rajesh Sivaraman and Sandeep Kohli of Enron Development), as % of average 2000 naphtha price, using 1999 prices as forwards: ATM 10.4% (13% if forward = $224/MT); 5/10/15/20% OTM 8.4/6.8/5.5/4.4%; flat caps $168 15%, $200 9%, $225 6% (his scenario list says $250). 2-year historical vol 34%, above the 10-year level. ←[[m00007]]
- [1999-12-10] The forward prices are guesses; he asked the three to get 2000 forwards and vols from John L Chismar, the Singapore naphtha trader, and asked for the Exotic Options Library to be installed on a common drive (G, J, M, O or P; other letters need reprogramming first). ←[[m00007]]
