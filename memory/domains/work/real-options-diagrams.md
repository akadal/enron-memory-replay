# Steven Leppard's diagrammatic real options

How he got the slot: memory/domains/industry/declined-invitations-2000.md. The paper's earlier history (Shan Millie, Risk Books): memory/domains/organization/london-research.md.

- [2000-04-25] Notation and "diagrammatic grammar" let originators, managers and quants describe a real-option deal unambiguously, pricing following from the diagram; extended to forward recursive dynamic programming. Used on maintenance scheduling, gas storage and plant development with static optimisation over price-curve scenarios; people accept his deterministic DP, he still sells full stochastic DP. ←[[m01475]] ←[[m01477]] ←[[m01499]]
- [2000-04-25] Cambridge talk proposed as "Diagrammatic Representation of Real Options in Enron"; Lenos Trigeorgis wants it less technical for practitioners, suggests "Real Options at Enron?" and asks for his job title. ←[[m01482]] ←[[m01496]]
- [2000-04-26] Owner sends his "standard real option spin presentation" plus an Enron analyst deck: "I would prefer not to disseminate the information about the  use of AMC." (American Monte Carlo). 27 Apr, two internal decks using a few of Blake's slides: "He developed it under a contract with Enron, but we don't want to use it verbatim." ←[[m01499]] ←[[m01545]]
- [2000-04-25] Publishing: Risk's "Game Choices" book (June) likely missed after Shan Millie left ("I think they are firing people at RISK"); owner: "RISK will give you more exposure in the peer group faster." than Phelim Boyle's journal. 28 Apr: the full ~6000-word internal paper went to Navroz Patel (Risk; ~4000 words, full references). ←[[m01477]] ←[[m01564]]
