# ExIm Bank vs OPIC guarantees

- [2000-01-06] The owner told [[clayton-vernon]], who had forwarded a CNN story on Russian projects using Ex-Im loan guarantees as project insurance, that ExIm Bank guarantees bank and commercial transactions and he thinks it covers commercial risk. ←[[m00171]]
- [2000-01-06] OPIC insures physical assets owned by US investors in foreign countries (owner, verbatim: "OPIC offers protection against political risk."). Brad Blesie would know the exact difference. ←[[m00171]]
