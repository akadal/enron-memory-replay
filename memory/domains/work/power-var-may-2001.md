# Power VaR: price jumps and West Power backtesting, May 2001

Methodology and the Reg. VaR meetings: memory/domains/work/var-credit-system-2001.md.

- [2001-05-02] Hayden to the owner and Tamarchenko, VaR for 1 May with vs without jumps: AGG-PWR-II 50,494,023 vs 48,061,924 (95%), 72,905,916 vs 66,560,550 (99%); jumps add 1.24M/3.82M to EAST-DPR-VAR and 0.97M/1.15M to WEST-DPR-VAR; CAND-DPR-VAR falls with jumps (by 92,771 and 1,026,900). ←[[m07551]]
- [2001-05-03] Tamarchenko to Hayden (cc owner): R8 price history says traders' posted West Power vols are too high, inflating VaR; POWER-NW-LT backtests badly; its EPMI-LT-NW-EX1 carries about 29M VaR yet shows no component VaR.
