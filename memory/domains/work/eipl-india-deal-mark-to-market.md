# Mark-to-market for the EIPL India deal

Background: Dabhol power sale structuring → memory/domains/work/dabhol-firm-power-sale.md

- [2000-01-24] Sandeep Kohli (letter of 23 Jan) asked Robert (Bob) Butts how the deal would be marked to market: EIPL reports under Indian GAAP and is held via Mauritius and the Cayman Islands; US consolidation is US GAAP. Issues: US tax accrues in 2000 while Indian income shows only in 2002–03 (tax credit? subpart F?); Indian corporates may not use a US fixed-for-floating swap, hence a fixed-price contract. No such deals yet, but likely repeats. [[pinnamaneni-krishnarao]] to brief Butts on three structures; meeting Thu 27 Jan, EB1906, owner invited. ←[[m00427]] ←[[m00429]] ←[[m00476]]
- [2000-01-28] Krishnarao (report of 27 Jan): Butts and Wess say payments tied to one plant cannot be marked; power sold to an outside party and bought back at several unrelated points can (the US peaking-plant workaround). At most 50% of DPC cash flows could qualify (outsiders own 50%); a plain loan to an affiliate cannot. Butts suggested EIPL buy options from DPC and other plants and sell to AP or Karnataka; DPC selling to EIPL may be politically hard. ←[[m00468]] ←[[m00476]]
- [2000-01-28] Options listed: (1) proceed unmarked, income in 2002–03; (2) sell the contract to a third party at a profit; (3) securitise the revenue through an outside party in India. Wade Cline asked whether DPC could sell to MSEB with EIPL buying back at another grid point. Owner forwarded the chain to AOL. ←[[m00468]] ←[[m00476]]
- [2000-01-24] [[shirley-crenshaw]]: Krishnarao's recent trip was charged to Research's cost centre; accounting proposes invoicing Dabhol Power Co.; she asked Kohli whom to bill. ←[[m00431]]
