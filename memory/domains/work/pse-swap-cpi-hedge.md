# PSE swap: USD/euro volatility and CPI correlation request

- [1999-12-17] Thread the owner forwarded to his AOL address: Sidney Cox (7 Dec) asked research for a quick 20-year USD/euro volatility estimate — credit reserves at a fixed 9.5% (1-yr implied; USD/DM ranged 7–11%) — and for a model of US CPI vs. $/euro correlation (credit assumes zero, possibly double-counting the reserve). The deal revived after the forex desk found a way to price CPI exposure. ←[[m00058]]
- [1999-12-17] Jarek Astramowicz (12 Dec): the FX and interest-rate desks had quoted high prices and claimed no CPI-hedge market, costing credibility with PSE; PSE holds binding quotes from JP Morgan and Morgan Stanley; closing may slip to January 2000 and the deal's NPV is near zero. ←[[m00058]]
