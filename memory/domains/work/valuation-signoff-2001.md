# Research sign-off on valuation (Jan 2001)

Earlier sign-off cases (Andersen review, EnergyDesk.com): memory/domains/work/model-risk-audit.md. London Research: memory/domains/organization/london-research.md.

- [2001-01-23] Stephen Stock: UK traders get valuation code signed off by Research and use a home-grown tool, DOVE, whose results differ from the US portcalc (there Research supplies code his team wraps); he needs the owner's guidance against the "not built here" syndrome. Owner: divergence comes from "the absence of formal rules for sign-off on the valuation techniques", turf wars and originators wanting control; "Please, let me know when you come back." ←[[m05505]] ←[[m05513]]
- [2001-01-24] Leppard (after Sharad was asked to sign off another group's calculation): Research can state a method's shortfalls and risks but not whether Enron accepts them, which is RAC's call; he proposes technical notes instead of sign-off. Owner: "we were asked on many occasions to sign - off on partial results of valuation, without the benefits of a full picture."; "I shall talk to Rick Buy and David Port about setting up more formal rules for the Research sign-off." ←[[m05520]]
