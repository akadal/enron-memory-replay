# Regressions on the drivers of Enron (ENE) stock

- [2000-05-12] Vincent Tang to Bradley Eixmann (cc the owner): ~30 comparable stocks, 10 capital market indices and three energy commodity indices, with lags and leads; most correlations unstable over time. Only the S&P Utility Index (SPXU) and Dow Jones Utility Index (UTIL) are stable and significant, with R2 below 30% and Durbin-Watson warning of autocorrelation. A real driver is hard, if not impossible, to find: since Enron added a new core business several months ago the stock behaves more like a telecom stock; more data is needed; more variables on request. ←[[m01667]]
