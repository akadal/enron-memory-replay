# Research Intelligence newsletter and its Technical Corner

- [1999-12-26] The Research Group publishes "Research Intelligence" weekly on the Intranet, with archived issues; its Technical Corner covers quantitative techniques for Enron business units. ←[[m00101]]
- [1999-12-26] The owner asked [[stinson-gibner]] to review a memo on the Technical Corner and forward it, with changes, to Ross Prevat; Stinson also gets a binder of past articles to judge their use in his area. ←[[m00101]]
- [1999-12-26] Binder recipients (partial list): Skilling, Sutton, Hannon, Baxter, Delainey, Bowen, W. Curry, Buy, Overdyke, Whalley, Shankman, Pai. The owner: "I think we should ask the head of each unit to make recommendations." ←[[m00101]]
