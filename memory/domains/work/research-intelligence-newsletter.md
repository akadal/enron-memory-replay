# Research Intelligence newsletter

- [1999-12-26] The Enron Corp. Research Group publishes the weekly newsletter "Research Intelligence" on the Intranet, with archived issues; each issue has a "Technical Corner" on quantitative techniques usable across business units. ←[[m00101]]
- [1999-12-26] The owner asked [[stinson-gibner]] to review a memo and forward it with changes, if any, to Ross Prevat (owner, verbatim: "Please, take a look at this memo and forward with changes, if any, to Ross Prevat."). ←[[m00101]]
- [1999-12-26] A binder of archived Technical Corner articles goes to Gibner, asked whether the techniques fit his area. Proposed binder recipients (partial): Skilling, Sutton, Hannon, Baxter, Delainey, Bowen, W. Curry, Buy, Overdyke, Whalley, Shankman, Pai; the owner suggested asking each unit head for recommendations. ←[[m00101]]
