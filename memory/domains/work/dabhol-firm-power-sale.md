# Dabhol Phase I firm power sale

- [1999-12-25] Sandeep Kohli wrote to the owner and [[vasant-shanbhogue]]: a proposal to sell Dabhol power to another state, a firm component over the peak period plus infirm power in the monsoon. He asked the group for structuring help and will meet the owner in Houston in the New Year. ←[[m00100]]
- [1999-12-25] Rajesh Sivaraman (24 Dec) had sent an issue list and tariff computation for a 50 MW firm sale from Phase I, to be resolved with MSEB (Vivek Kejriwal, Shubh Shrivastava copied). Sandeep asked his team (also Anshuman Srivastav) for sensitivities and said he would try to get structuring expertise. ←[[m00100]]
- [1999-12-25→] Open: the owner's reply and the New Year meeting date are not in the mail so far. ←[[m00100]]
