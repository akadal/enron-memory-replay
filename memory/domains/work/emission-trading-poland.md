# Emission-trading materials for Enron Poland

- [1999-12-14] Adam Brulinski (Warsaw office) asked the owner for material on the substance of emission-allowance trading, to start promoting it in Poland; "quite urgent". The owner: "Let me gather some information for you." ←[[m00016]] ←[[m00059]]
- [1999-12-16] The owner asked [[joseph-hrgovcic]] whether the group has any materials. ←[[m00034]]
- [1999-12-16] Hrgovcic: Susan Wood (head of Enron's emissions marketing) says Enron has nothing it routinely sends out; she recommends the Emissions Trading Handbook ($50, etei.org, ~3 weeks delivery); etei.org and emissions.org are good sources. He offered to buy the handbook. ←[[m00035]]
- [1999-12-17] The owner forwarded the Hrgovcic reply to Brulinski ("FYI") and a copy of the thread to his AOL address; the handbook offer got no recorded answer. ←[[m00060]] ←[[m00050]]
