# Emission trading materials for Enron Warsaw

- [1999-12-14] Adam Brulinski (Enron Warsaw, WAR/ECT) asked, in Polish, for substantive material on emission rights trading to start promoting it in Poland; owner: "Let me gather some information for you." ←[[m00016]]
- [1999-12-16] Brulinski called it urgent; owner asked Joe Hrgovcic whether the group had any materials. ←[[m00034]]
- [1999-12-16] Hrgovcic: the group sends out nothing on it; Susan Wood heads Enron's emissions marketing and recommends the Emissions Trading Handbook ($50, 3-week delivery, etei.org); also etei.org and emissions.org. Hrgovcic offered to buy it. ←[[m00035]]
- [1999-12-17] Owner forwarded Hrgovcic's reply to Brulinski. ←[[m00060]]
