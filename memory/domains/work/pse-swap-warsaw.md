# PSE swap transaction (Enron Warsaw)

Warsaw team: Sidney Cox, Jarek Astramowicz, Jarek Dybowski. ←[[m00058]]

- [1999-12-17] Request (made 7-12 Dec, forwarded by the owner to his AOL 17 Dec): justify a 20-year USD/euro volatility for the credit reserve (credit uses 9.5% fixed off 1-yr implied; USD/DM historically 7-11%) and analyse US CPI vs $/euro correlation (credit assumes zero, may double-dip the reserve); fast turnaround wanted. ←[[m00058]]
- [1999-12-17] Astramowicz: Enron's FX and rates desks quoted high and said there is no CPI hedge market, while PSE holds binding quotes from JP Morgan and Morgan Stanley; deal may close only in January with NPV near zero, so every dollar counts. ←[[m00058]]
