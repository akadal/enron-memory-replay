# Summer 2000 work in Houston for Michal

- [2000-01-28] Adam T. (chemat@softel.gda.pl), in a mostly family letter in Polish to Ludmila and the owner ("Wicek"), wrote that Michal and his fiancee, both third-year medical students, plan to come to America in the summer to improve their English and perhaps work. He asked whether they could find jobs in Houston and whether the owner could help; they do not want to burden the household. The owner forwarded it to himself. ←[[m00460]]
