# TrueHelp: Harry Arora's venture, the owner on its board

- [2002-01-17] Harry Arora, after talking with the owner the day before and handing him paper copies, sends the TrueHelp documents electronically; asks whether the owner will continue on TrueHelp's board or is still deciding, wants his home e-mail and phone, and expects him to be approached with many new opportunities. ←[[m11037]]
- [2002-01-18] Owner: "I shall be very happy to assist you with TrueHelp."; "I shall also ask my neighbor if he wants to work with us."; gives his home address in The Woodlands, home and cell phones and AOL e-mail. ←[[m11102]]
