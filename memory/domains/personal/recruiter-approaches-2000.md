# Recruiter approaches to the owner (2000)

Detail ←[[recruiter-approaches-2000-2000-08-w32]]

- [2000-06-28] Al Arfsten (BFL Associates Ltd., 713 965 2158, arfsten@bflassociates.com), confidentially (27 Jun): a leader to build a Real Options consulting practice for "one of the foremost financial advisory firms in the world" (board and C-level clients, credentialed staff; live anywhere, mainly New York or San Francisco), and recommendations; the owner called back: "Please, feel free to call me at the office (713) 853 3848", or at home. ←[[m02149]]
