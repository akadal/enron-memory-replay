# Recruiter approaches to the owner (2000)

- [2000-06-28] Al Arfsten (BFL Associates Ltd., 713 965 2158, arfsten@bflassociates.com), confidentially (27 Jun): a leader to build a Real Options consulting practice for "one of the foremost financial advisory firms in the world" (board and C-level clients, credentialed staff; live anywhere, mainly New York or San Francisco), and recommendations; the owner called back: "Please, feel free to call me at the office (713) 853 3848", or at home. ←[[m02149]]
- [2000-06-30] Arfsten (near Greenway Plaza) got the owner's resume as plain text and wants a Word copy or a hard copy at their Thursday meeting; owner: "Our E-mail systems may have compatibility problems." ←[[m02218]]
- [2000-07-07] Arfsten, after meeting the owner 6 Jul, wants a meeting with Tim Luehrman on PwC's global Financial Advisory Services practice after the owner's return from Australia; asks for his resume and the publication citing Luehrman's "tomato field" model; the owner forwards it to his AOL. ←[[m02286]]
- [2000-07-11] Arfsten keeps the owner's package and asks for a duplicate by US Mail to Tim Luehrman (PricewaterhouseCooper LLP, One Post Office Square, Boston), or forwards his own. ←[[m02355]]
