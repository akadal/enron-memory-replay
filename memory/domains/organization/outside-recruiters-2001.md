# Outside recruiters offering candidates (Jan 2001)

Detail ←[[outside-recruiters-2001-2001-01-w04]]

- [2001-01-08] Jeff Wesley (jjw@ziplip.com; 949 813 2241) proposes a call on 9 Jan about his students and candidates asking about Enron, sends two PDFs and hints at a controversial third; owner (9 Jan): "Please, send me your phone numbers. I shall call you at my convenience." ←[[m05222]] ←[[m05240]]
- [2001-01-11] Owner: "As soon as we have an agreement in place, I shall start working with you." Wesley works with MRI USA and Robert Walters UK (accounts PaineWebber, Merrill Lynch; job orders from CME, Alcoa); he accepts Enron's terms (20% fee, standard 30-day replacement guarantee, no deposit on contingency searches), wants fees wired to a multi-currency account (California or Bermuda bank). ←[[m05266]] ←[[m05287]] ←[[m05289]]
- [2001-01-12] Wesley sends Howard Haughton's CV (asked for the owner; in NY from the UK for 2 weeks) and offers two Michigan students as June-August interns, Howard Lin (4.0, graduating May 2001) and Sung (May 2002), whom he will own as graduate candidates: a 'test drive before committal'. ←[[m05300]]
- [2001-01-22] Wesley sends candidate Lawrence's resume and photo for a phone interview (Williams, Ford, SDCERA also court him). ←[[m05479]]
