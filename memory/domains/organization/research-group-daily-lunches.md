# Lunches for Research Group members who must stay at their desks

- [1999-12-20] Kevin Moore asked whether he could provide daily lunches for Trisha Tlapek of the Weather Team, who has to stay at her desk; other Weather Team members may need one from time to time. ←[[m00116]]
- [1999-12-30] The owner told Moore to ask Jeff Shankman's secretary to include all Research Group members on the 31st floor in the company-provided lunches (when available). Extra lunches are fine now and then but exceptional (owner, verbatim: "but it should be seen as a something done under exceptional circumstances."). Mike Roberts and [[shirley-crenshaw]] copied. ←[[m00116]]
