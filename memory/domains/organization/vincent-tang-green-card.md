# Vincent Tang job description (green card)

- [2000-01-04] Candace Womack asked the owner to edit the job description Tindall & Foster drafted for [[vincent-tang]]'s position (they had none from Enron and sent a generic Operations Research Analyst sample); needed for his green card. The owner promised it later that day (owner, verbatim: "I shall edit the job description and forward it to you later today."). ←[[m00136]]
- [2000-01-07] The owner apologised for the additional delay (owner, verbatim: "Sorry for additional delay. Editing took a long time."). ←[[m00181]]
- [2000-01-07] Womack passed the edited text to the legal counsel, who were very pleased: it showed what Tang actually does, so they can prepare his case. ←[[m00193]]
