# Holiday help and staff changes, Dec 1999

- [1999-12-13] Van Ngo (Rice student) offered to work at Enron over his Christmas break: available Mon 27 Dec to Fri 14 Jan (semester ends 20 Dec; may be away the week before Christmas). He asked which dates the owner most needs help and plans to contact Andrea at ProStaff once arranged; [[shirley-crenshaw]] forwarded it to the owner on 1999-12-14. ←[[m00013]]
- [1999-12-17] Martina Angelova's last day at Enron North America was 1999-12-17; she graduated from the University of Houston and moves to Enron Europe (UK) once her work permit is ready, expected early February. Yana Kristal, a University of Houston student, takes over her responsibilities in early January 2000. ←[[m00047]]
- [1999-12-30] Mike Roberts told the owner that Sam says he needs $30K to leave ProStaff. The owner answered he will talk to McBride about the comp levels (owner, verbatim: "Let me talk to McBride about the comp levels."); outcome not in the mail so far. ←[[m00120]]
