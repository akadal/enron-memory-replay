# Enron North America reorganization of 1999-12-15

- [1999-12-15] Cliff Baxter and Kevin Hannon (ENA Office of the Chairman) consolidated the Coverage/Origination groups under Dave Delainey, to pursue "One Enron" and widen industrial and midstream coverage. ←[[m00028]]
- [1999-12-15] Downstream Origination: Greg Piper (back from Portland) leads; Edward Ondarza Pulp and Paper; Jim Ajello Chemicals, Plastics and Refined Products; Bruce Garner (ex-Bankers Trust) Non-Integrated Industrials. ←[[m00028]]
- [1999-12-15] Midstream: Janet Dietrich Eastern Coverage/Origination; Chris Calger Western (Calgary to Portland); Mike Miller IPP Merchant plus Eastern power development; Dave Duran Eastern QF Restructuring. ←[[m00028]]
- [1999-12-15] Commercial Transactions Group: Ray Bowen (Upstream and Downstream Transaction Development) and Jeff Donahue (Midstream and Generation) co-head; Andrea Reed heads Capital Structuring and Portfolio Management. ←[[m00028]]
- [1999-12-15] Transaction Development: Midstream Lemmons, McCormick, Norris, Porter; Downstream Malcolm, Boudreaux, Biggerstaff, Helfrich; Generation Suffield, Kelemen, Mahmoud, House; Upstream Dunn, Curtin, Hilgert; more hires within weeks. ←[[m00028]]
