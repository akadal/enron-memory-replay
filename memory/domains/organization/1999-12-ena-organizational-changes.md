# ENA reorganisation of Coverage/Origination and CTG, Dec 1999

- [1999-12-15] Cliff Baxter and Kevin Hannon (ENA Office of the Chairman): Coverage/Origination consolidated under Dave Delainey. Greg Piper returns from Portland to run Downstream Origination (Pulp & Paper: Edward Ondarza; Chemicals/Plastics/Refined Products: Jim Ajello; Non-Integrated Industrials: Bruce Garner). Midstream: Janet Dietrich East, Chris Calger West (moves Calgary→Portland), Mike Miller IPP Merchant/Eastern power development, Dave Duran Eastern QF Restructuring. ←[[m00028]]
- [1999-12-15] Commercial Transactions Group co-headed by Ray Bowen (Upstream, Downstream) and Jeff Donahue (Midstream, Generation); Andrea Reed heads Capital Structuring and Portfolio Management. ←[[m00028]]
- [1999-12-16] A revised memo corrected the first, describing the Midstream groups' utility/municipal/co-op/ESCO/IPP focus. [1999-12-17] The owner forwarded both to his AOL address. ←[[m00036]] ←[[m00054]] ←[[m00063]]
