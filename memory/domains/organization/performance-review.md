# Officer titles and performance review

- [1999-12-15] Office of the Chairman: four officer levels (4 Office of the Chairman, 3 other Executive Committee, 2 managing directors, 1 VPs). Levels 1–2 are reviewed by a PRC twice a year (July feedback; year-end feedback plus bonus and compensation); Level 1 reviewed at business-unit level, then cross-calibrated by the Executive Committee and ~16 MDs. Promotions in January only. A new long-term incentive plan replaces unit plans; cross-functional PRCs also began for directors, senior directors and general managers. ←[[m00026]]
