# Company-wide announcements and systems, 2001

- [2001-12-18] Office of the Chairman: NETCO formed for the North American gas and power wholesale business under new ownership (auction filed this week, closing expected January, Enron keeps 49% or less); a 15-member Creditors' Committee; Jeff McMahon (CFO) joins Lay and Whalley in the Office of the Chairman; Ray Bowen EVP Finance and Treasurer; Mark Muller EVP Corporate Development; Wholesale (ENA, EBS, EIM, EGM) under Jim Fallon; Brown, Frevert, Kean, McConnell, Shankman leave by end-December; Lavorato and Kitchen leave to set up NETCO. ←[[m10321]]
- [2002-01-06] Office of the Chairman (Ken): on 3 Jan Enron settled the procedural dispute with Dynegy over Northern Natural Gas: Dynegy may exercise its option on the pipeline, its Texas lawsuit is dismissed, Enron's right to repurchase runs to 30 Jun; Enron pursues its $10 billion damage claim, rebuilding on some 9,000 miles of pipelines; Stan Horton to ETS: Dynegy takes interim control, uncontested.
