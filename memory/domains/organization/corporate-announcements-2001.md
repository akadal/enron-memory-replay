- [2002-01-06] Office of the Chairman (Ken): on 3 Jan Enron settled the procedural dispute with Dynegy over Northern Natural Gas: Dynegy may exercise its option on the pipeline, its Texas lawsuit is dismissed, Enron's right to repurchase runs to 30 Jun; Enron pursues its $10 billion damage claim, rebuilding on some 9,000 miles of pipelines; Stan Horton to ETS: Dynegy takes interim control, uncontested.
