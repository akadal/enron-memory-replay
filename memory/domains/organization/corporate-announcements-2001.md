# Company-wide announcements and systems, 2001

Detail ←[[corporate-announcements-2001-2001-04-w17]] ←[[corporate-announcements-2001-2001-05-w18b]] ←[[corporate-announcements-2001-2001-05-w21]] (EGM meeting 7 May, iBuyit live 1 May)

- [2001-04-20] iBuyit: invoice coding and approval move to iBuyit Payables on 1 May; the owner becomes approver for cost centre 107043 (SAP role confirmed, user ID P00503778). ←[[m06920]] ←[[m07053]]
- [2001-04-23] EES reorganisation (via Osman Sezgen): EES Risk Management and most Risk Controls and Operations become an EWS group serving EES, Don Black (gas, power, tariff, options desks) and Wanda Curry reporting to the EWS Office of the Chairman; DSM and IAM stay in EES under Ozzie Pagan, Services under Evan Hughes; Sean Holmes's Structuring becomes Deal Management; Anthony Dayao heads a shared IT function; Marty Sunde leads a Restructuring Group (key deals, mainly California). ←[[m07081]] ←[[m07108]]
- [2001-05-04] Lavorato and Kitchen merge EES and EWS risk functions into two Enron Americas groups serving EES: Rogers Herndon (Eastern Interconnect, still under Presto) and Don Black (Western U.S., Houston, under Belden). ←[[m07650]] ←[[m07684]]
- [2001-05-25] EnTouch (ENA PR; owner copies it to AOL): EnronOnline's one-millionth transaction on 23 May, over $600B notional across ~1500 products, ~60% of Enron trades online, processing cost down 75%; EIM's Forest Products Origination team under Rob Saltiel; Enron Freight Markets did 120+ spot trucking trades in month one (~$1.05-1.10 a mile). ←[[m08012]]
