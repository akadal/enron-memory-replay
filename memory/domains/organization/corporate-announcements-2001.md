- [2002-01-29] Jeff McMahon (President & COO): Ken Lay has resigned; the Board names Stephen Cooper (managing partner, Zolfo Cooper) interim CEO and chief restructuring officer; Greg Whalley leaves as president and COO for UBS Warburg, ahead of the NetCo sale; Office of the Chief Executive: Cooper, McMahon, Ray Bowen (EVP and CFO); a plan to emerge from bankruptcy, roles to follow. ←[[m11426]]
- [2002-01-23] Ken Lay steps down as Chairman and CEO, effective immediately (inquiries and investigations need his attention), stays a Director; the Creditor Committee seeks a restructuring specialist as interim CEO; he believes Enron can and will survive. ←[[m11252]]
- [2002-01-25] General Announcement: Cliff Baxter died that morning, "a tragedy"; many years of service; survived by his wife Carol and children J.C. and Lauren; the family's wishes to follow. ←[[m11329]]
