- [2001-05-01] Bob Butts: iBuyit Payables is live for Houston Accounts Payable; from 8 May invoices with paper coding sheets are routed back to be coded and approved in iBuyit (help: ISC 713-345-4727). ←[[m07423]]
- [2001-05-04] Lavorato and Kitchen merge EES and EWS risk functions into two Enron Americas groups serving EES: East under Rogers Herndon (reports to Kevin Presto), West under Don Black (ex-VP EES Risk Management and Sourcing; reports to Tim Belden). ←[[m07650]] ←[[m07684]]
- [2001-05-11] iBuyit set-up (Crenshaw to Diane Fellers): requestors and coders Crenshaw, Anita DuPont, Kevin Moore; the owner sole approver (Co 0413, CC 107043), though the iBuyit team wants more than one; invoices since 1 May not back for coding. ←[[m07831]]
- [2001-05-25] EnTouch (ENA PR): EnronOnline's one-millionth transaction on 23 May, over $600 billion notional across ~1,500 products; ~60% of Enron deals now online, processing cost down 75%; EIM's Forest Products Origination team under Rob Saltiel; Enron Freight Markets' 120+ trucking trades in month one (~$1.05-1.10/mile); owner copies it to AOL. ←[[m08012]]
