# Response to Abhay Mehta's 'Power Play' (Oct 2000)

Dabhol: memory/domains/work/dabhol-power-sale.md.

- [2000-10-09] Owner to Mark Palmer: Mehta is touring the US; "My wife went to the presentation and told me it was quite critical about Enron. About 40 people attended." (Stanford India Association; the group's page sent to Sandeep Kohli). Palmer was unaware and will check. ←[[m03467]] ←[[m03469]] ←[[m03508]]
- [2000-10-12] Cindy Derecskey (via Christie Patrick) looks for the book; the owner has one, another coming from an Enron officer in India: "I can lend you the book and you can makes copies of the most important chapters.", "I don't think we should be buying too many copies and increasing the sales of the book.", "In general, I think that we should counter the presentations made by Mr. Mehta." (the Stanford-graduate head of Dabhol to seek the same forum; "He should be here for the management conference.") ←[[m03564]]
- [2000-10-12] Derecskey and Palmer advise against a rebuttal (more attention; no trace on Amazon, Britannica, Yahoo, Dow Jones; no media calls): answer only when asked. Owner: "I had rather in mind targeting the same audiences to which Mr. Mehta spoke, not a general  press release.", "I agree that a general message to the world would attract attention." ←[[m03568]] ←[[m03579]]
- [2000-10-13] Derecskey: none of them (with Christie Patrick, Mike Rosen, Palmer) has read Mehta yet; option: an Enron-sponsored talk on a related topic, inviting his audience, so it does not look like a rebuttal. ←[[m03615]]
