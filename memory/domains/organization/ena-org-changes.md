# ENA organization changes

- [1999-12-15] Cliff Baxter and Kevin Hannon: ENA coverage/origination consolidated under Dave Delainey ("One Enron"). Greg Piper leads Downstream Origination; Janet Dietrich Eastern and Chris Calger Western coverage; Mike Miller IPP Merchant; Dave Duran Eastern QF Restructuring. Commercial Transactions Group co-headed by Ray Bowen and Jeff Donahue; Andrea Reed heads Capital Structuring and Portfolio Management. Full team lists in the source. ←[[m00028]]
