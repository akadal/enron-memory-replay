# Anjam Ahmad's resignation, Oct 2000

London team: memory/domains/organization/london-research.md

- [2000-10-25] Anjam Ahmad resigns from Enron Europe after 3½ years (hired Apr 97 by the owner, Sherriff and Lewis); farewell 26 Oct (citywhizkid@hotmail.com, mobile 0961 111 192; London SW18). ←[[m03855]] ←[[m03864]]
- [2000-10-26] Owner (Anjam had a very generous offer to move to Houston): "Anjam showed very poor judgment one more time.", "I recommend keeping him on the payroll, and at home, as long as possible." Leppard wants him off the trading floor and IT checks (h: drive, backups, Notes and mail, deleted files); Lewis will brief him on his contract's confidentiality (copy from Melanie Doyle). ←[[m03839]]
- [2000-10-26] Leppard's action list to Melanie Doyle, OK'd by the owner: pass removed, escorted in the building, Sharad sits with him while he catalogues his work, internet and Notes cut, print records pulled. Lewis: Anjam says he is not joining an energy competitor (weather derivatives the only overlap); low risk, guard weather information. ←[[m03854]] ←[[m03862]]
- [2000-10-27] Norma Villarreal to Margaret Daffin: per the owner, Anjam resigned Wednesday, so HR stops its activity on him; on 9 Oct Tara Rozen (with Melanie Doyle) had asked whether Grant left for a competitor and said Anjam had not yet accepted an offer. ←[[m03880]]
