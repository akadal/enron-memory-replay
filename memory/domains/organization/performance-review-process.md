# Officer levels and Performance Review Committee

- [1999-12-15] The Office of the Chairman announced four officer levels: 4 Office of the Chairman, 3 other Executive Committee members, 2 managing directors (incl. company presidents, some SVPs), 1 VPs (and grandfathered SVPs). ←[[m00026]]
- [1999-12-15] Level 1 and 2 officers go to a Performance Review Committee twice a year: July (feedback) and year-end (feedback, bonus, total compensation). The Executive Committee runs it for Level 2; Level 1 is reviewed first by business unit, then cross-calibrated by the Executive Committee and about sixteen managing directors. ←[[m00026]]
- [1999-12-15] The Executive Committee considers all promotions each January; exceptions will be infrequent. Individual evaluation drives base pay, bonus and long-term awards; a new long-term program replacing individual or business-unit plans was approved and will be communicated before bonus payments. Cross-functional PRC reviews for directors, senior directors and general managers have also begun. ←[[m00026]]
