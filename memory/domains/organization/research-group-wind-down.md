# Research Group wind-down after the bankruptcy

Job searches, references: memory/domains/organization/leavers-and-job-leads.md. Software and staff to NETCO: memory/domains/organization/ubsw-transfer.md.

- [2002-01-07] Wichai Narongwanich resigns (effective 8 Jan, a year at Enron) given the changed situation, to finish his Michigan Ph.D. in Ann Arbor (4-6 months); hopes to work with the owner again. ←[[m10651]]
- [2002-01-07] Maureen Raymond: her FX and Sovereign Risk group still supplies curves to corporate units (daily "Curves to be done" list); she and Mitra have no jobs yet, Gwyn is weeks from one. ←[[m10657]]
- [2002-01-08] Chonawee Supatgiat resigns as research manager, last day 8 Jan, offers hand-over help (chonawee@umich.edu); owner forwards it to Khymberly Booth. ←[[m10697]] ←[[m10704]]
- [2002-01-09] Owner to David Gorte: Raymond's unit (Mitra Mujica, Gwyn Koepke) forecasts I/R and F/X curves for asset valuations; unless his group absorbs it, "Maureen's employment with Enron will be terminated on January 15 (Jim Fallon's decision)." ←[[m10725]]
- [2002-01-09] Owner to Booth, after Fallon: "we can extend the deadline for termination till January the 30th" for model transfer (Mora, Chopra, Mellacheruvu, Rajan) and shut-down (Crenshaw, DuPont, Candella); she asks him to verify the release dates; he asks whether Paulo Issler, who resigned in December, is entitled to his Dec 16-31 paycheck, she looks into it. ←[[m10727]] ←[[m10741]] ←[[m10734]] ←[[m10753]]
- [2002-01-09] Shanna Funkhouser (HR): checklist for his one-on-one reduction meetings on 10 Jan (equipment, cards, expenses, contact form), she and Khymberly at his 3 pm meeting; owner to Crenshaw: "It outlines the process we have to follow." ←[[m10748]] ←[[m10759]]
- [2002-01-09] Owner thanks Kevin Moore for his farewell note: "I am thinking about organizing a reunion of the Group in a few months." ←[[m10760]]
