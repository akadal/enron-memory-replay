# Global Strategic Sourcing unit (1 Feb 2000)

- [2000-02-02] Office of the Chairman: Global Strategic Sourcing formed effective 1 Feb, merging the sourcing work of Gas Pipeline Group, Global Asset Operations and Enron Corp; it pools internal, joint-venture and partner demand for cash savings and a future business-to-business e-commerce venture. ←[[m00533]]
- [2000-02-02] Head: George Wasaff, Managing Director (14 years at Enron; lately Enron South America wholesale; earlier interim CEO of Elektro and CEO of TGS), reporting to Mike McConnell, CEO of Global Technology. Owner forwarded the memo to AOL on 4 Feb. ←[[m00533]] ←[[m00549]]
