# Candidates and departures, January 2000

- [2000-01-04] Jason Sokolov sent a petition for a summer internship with the Enron Analyst and Associate group. The owner recommended him to Celeste Roberts (owner, verbatim: "I am recommending Jason Sokolov for summer internship with Enron."): he works part-time for Mike Roberts and contributes to several projects. ←[[m00139]] ←[[m00142]]
- [2000-01-06] [[vasant-shanbhogue]] recommended Ray Spudeck (Sr. Research Associate, NAIC; recommended by Prof. MacMinn; quantitative and insurance background, left academics for hands-on work) and asked if the owner would phone him. ←[[m00165]]
- [2000-01-07] After a second call Vasant asked the owner to phone Spudeck next week and say when, and proposed a Houston interview if it goes well; Spudeck is not an actuary but can work with data (for the insurance group). ←[[m00178]]
- [2000-01-07] Craig Jimenez, a candidate for [[stinson-gibner]]'s group, accepted an amended offer from Southern Company Energy Marketing (higher pay, rotation through structuring, trading and research); he moves to Atlanta in March. The owner forwarded it to Greg Whalley (cc Brad McSherry, David Oxley, Elizabeth Grant) as a data point (owner, verbatim: "One of many data points I have the competition is paying more."). ←[[m00176]] ←[[m00184]]
