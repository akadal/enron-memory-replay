# Enron Credit Markets Group and credit white paper

- [1999-12-17] John Sherriff's 7 Dec announcement (owner forwarded it to AOL): the Credit Markets Group manages Enron's internal credit portfolio risk and sells third-party credit protection, first as bankruptcy protection on reference credits in Enron's portfolio, via EnronOnline and an external website; based in London with a Houston desk; public announcement expected late January 2000. Bryan Seyfried leaves RAC to lead it, reporting to Sherriff. ←[[m00072]]
- [1999-12-17] Seyfried asked [[pinnamaneni-krishnarao]] and the owner for a soft copy of "Actively Managing Credit Risk" (Risk Publications, co-authored by [[vasant-shanbhogue]]), to be updated by LSE intern Bijoya Banerjea into a white paper for the group's website; Katja Schilling asked Shanbhogue to help over the next few weeks. ←[[m00052]]
