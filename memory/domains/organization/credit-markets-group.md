# Enron Europe Credit Markets Group

- [1999-12-17] John Sherriff's Enron Europe announcement (dated 1999-12-07; Anjam Ahmad sent it to the owner, [[stinson-gibner]] and [[vasant-shanbhogue]] that day) formed the group to develop products managing Enron's internal credit portfolio risk and to offer third-party credit protection. It first makes markets in bankruptcy protection on reference credits already in Enron's portfolio, trades on EnronOnline, and plans an external credit-protection website (real-time prices, models, research); public announcement expected late January. ←[[m00072]]
- [1999-12-17] Based in London with an initial local desk in Houston. [[bryan-seyfried]], moving out of RAC (where he ran European Trading Controls and Research), leads it and reports to Sherriff. The owner forwarded the announcement to his personal address, no comment. ←[[m00072]]
