# Enron Credit Markets Group and credit white paper

- [1999-12-17] John Sherriff's 7 Dec announcement: the Credit Markets Group manages Enron's internal credit portfolio risk and sells third-party credit protection (first bankruptcy protection on reference credits) via EnronOnline and an external website; London-based with a Houston desk; public launch expected late January 2000. Bryan Seyfried leaves RAC to lead it, reporting to Sherriff. ←[[m00072]] ←[[m00117]]
- [1999-12-17] Seyfried asked [[pinnamaneni-krishnarao]] and the owner for "Actively Managing Credit Risk" (Risk Publications, with [[vasant-shanbhogue]]) for LSE intern Bijoya Banerjea to update into a white paper for the website; Katja Schilling asked Shanbhogue to help. ←[[m00052]] ←[[m00118]]
- [2000-01-03] Benjamin Parsons may post his "Measuring Credit Risk" article on the website unless the owner objects by Wednesday. ←[[m00129]]
- [2000-01-17] Parsons sent the owner, Seyfried, Masson and [[vasant-shanbhogue]] the pricing methodology for "Bankruptcy Swaps", launching the week of 24 Jan, for feedback. ←[[m00362]]
- [2000-01-06] Schilling wants external use of the credit article (Risk Books, 1998, "Credit Derivatives"); owner: "RISK magazine has the copyright" — ask Shan Millie, Risk Books London; Vasant: rewording does not allow reuse. [2000-01-07] Schilling is contacting Risk Books. ←[[m00170]] ←[[m00175]]
