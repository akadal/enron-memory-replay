# Japan merchant business: candidate search

- [1999-12-13] [[joseph-hirl]] wrote that Whalley, at the SA offsite, said the owner has or knows a person who could bring talent to evaluating an Enron merchant business in Japan. Hirl was in Sydney, planned Tokyo the following week, and asked when the owner could talk. ←[[m00012]]
- [1999-12-17] The owner forwarded Hirl information on Taiichi Hoshino, a Stanford PhD candidate (Engineering Economic Systems and Operations Research) based in California who wants to work in energy at Enron. ←[[m00066]]
- [1999-12-17] Hoshino had written in October 1999 that he expects to return to Japan or East Asia after graduating (family reason) and offered to help with a future Tokyo staff role combining risk analytics with local language and business knowledge; the owner answered him on 1999-10-20 that he would forward it to the Analyst/Associate program and other units. ←[[m00066]]
- [1999-12-22] The owner told Hirl that Hoshino is a research assistant of Prof. Darrell Duffie at Stanford, whom he met in that capacity: "A very bright fellow"; he could not assess his commercial skills, but Hoshino has the common sense to identify the winner, as his interest in Enron shows. ←[[m00089]]
