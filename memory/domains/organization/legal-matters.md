# Legal matters involving the owner

Detail ←[[legal-matters-2002-01-09]]

- [2001-12-31] E-mail retention: after Britt Davis's keep-all-mail notice the owner tells him (cc Robert C. Williams, James Derrick) that mail older than 30 days has vanished from his Inbox and Sent Items despite the decision to extend retention indefinitely: "It seems that the problem starts with our IT department."; associates interviewed by David Cohen for the Special Committee report the same; "I hope you can look into." Williams: the notice answers a recently received subpoena, a precaution until they speak with Wilmer Cutler, whose advice Enron follows; backup tapes keep a complete record, Cohen knows; deleted messages can be retrieved on request. Owner copies the exchange to AOL 31 Dec and 4 Jan. ←[[m10569]] ←[[m10570]] ←[[m10581]] ←[[m10582]] ←[[m10621]] ←[[m10622]]
- [2002-01-04] SEC deposition: owner to Robert C. Williams, confirming a voice-mail: "I decided that I don't really  need a counsel to assist me at my deposition before the Commission."; he may retain "a lawyer of my own choice to give me general advice about the procedures" and send Williams the bill; no judgement on Michael Levy: "I found him to be an exceptionally competent lawyer."; his "intention was to waive my right to a counsel" before meeting Levy; he faxes the SEC, copy to Williams (cc litigation.ljm), who confirms receipt. To Levy (Swidler), after their Wednesday meeting: "I decided to appear before the SEC investigators without a legal counsel"; Levy away until about 22 Jan (secretary Megan Morgan). ←[[m10629]] ←[[m10633]] ←[[m10635]]
- [2002-01-07] Michael Levy (Swidler, (202) 295-8414; cc Robert C. Williams, Amy Carpenter-Holmes) wishes the owner well in his SEC testimony, offers help on counsel or any government investigation; copied to AOL 8 Jan. ←[[m10652]] ←[[m10687]]
- [2002-01-08] Owner mails himself, subject "B-C", Bryan Cave's people page for Therese D. Pritchard. ←[[m10716]]
