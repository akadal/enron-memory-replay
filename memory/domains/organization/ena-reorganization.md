# ENA organizational changes, Dec 1999 and Jul 2000

Detail ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-07-w28]]

Announced by Cliff Baxter and Kevin Hannon (ENA Office of the Chairman). ←[[m00028]]

- [1999-12-15→] Coverage/origination (power development, Downstream, Eastern/Western Midstream) consolidated under Dave Delainey. Downstream: Greg Piper (back from Portland) with Ed Ondarza (pulp and paper), Jim Ajello (chemicals, plastics, refined products), Bruce Garner (non-integrated industrials). Midstream: Janet Dietrich (East), Chris Calger (West, to Portland), Mike Miller (IPP merchant, East power development), Dave Duran (East QF restructuring). ←[[m00028]]
- [2000-07-13] Ray Bowen leads ENA Industrial Origination (Jim Ajello metals/refining/petrochemicals, Edward Ondarza pulp and paper, Rodney Malcolm execution); CTG restructured, Transaction Development merged into the origination groups; portfolio investments to Jeff Donahue (capital book; Maffett/Lydecker, Andrea Reed); Commodity Structuring (Aucoin, McMichael) to Janet Dietrich; Technical Group and OEC to the Office of the Chairman; Joe Deffner ENA CFO and Treasury head. ←[[m02392]]

## History

- [1999-12-15→2000-07-13] Commercial Transactions Group co-headed by Ray Bowen (Upstream/Downstream) and Jeff Donahue (Midstream/Generation); Andrea Reed keeps Capital Structuring and Portfolio Management; restructured 13 Jul 2000. ←[[m00028]]
