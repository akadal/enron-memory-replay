# ENA organizational changes, Dec 1999 and Jul 2000

Detail ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-07-w28]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-08-w31]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-08-w33]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-10-w41]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-11-w45]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-12-w49]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2001-02-w05]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2001-07-21]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2001-10-28]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2002-01-09]]

Announced by Cliff Baxter and Kevin Hannon (ENA Office of the Chairman). ←[[m00028]]


- [2001-10-24] Enron Americas (Louise and John): Central and Eastern gas mid-market, origination and wellhead pricing join Fred Lagrasta's Derivatives group as one Eastern producer unit; West and Texas unchanged (Whitt, Redmond; origination Tycholiz, Redmond); Laura Luce leads Strategic Asset Development; Frank Vickers adds Central mid-market and origination. ←[[m09576]]
- [2002-01-09] EWS Office of the Chairman: EWS (ENA, EBS, EIM, EGM, Enron Europe, Enron Canada) reorganised under CEO Jim Fallon to maximise creditor value and pick the businesses for the reorganisation plan; Rich DiMichele COO, keeps EBS; heads Don Miller (ENA), Gary Hickerson (EGM), Rodney Malcolm (EIM), Eric Shaw (Europe), Kyle Kitagawa (Canada); functions Lance Schuler (GC), Barry Pearce (Chief of Staff), Dave Gorte (RAC), Kent Castleman (Accounting), Shanna Funkhouser (HR). ←[[m10742]]
