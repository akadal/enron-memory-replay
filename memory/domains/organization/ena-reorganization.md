# ENA organizational changes, Dec 1999

Announced by Cliff Baxter and Kevin Hannon (ENA Office of the Chairman). ←[[m00028]]

- [1999-12-15→] Coverage/origination (power development, Downstream, Eastern/Western Midstream) consolidated under Dave Delainey. Downstream: Greg Piper (back from Portland) with Ed Ondarza (pulp and paper), Jim Ajello (chemicals, plastics, refined products), Bruce Garner (non-integrated industrials). Midstream: Janet Dietrich (East), Chris Calger (West, to Portland), Mike Miller (IPP merchant, East power development), Dave Duran (East QF restructuring). ←[[m00028]]
- [1999-12-15→] Commercial Transactions Group co-headed by Ray Bowen (Upstream/Downstream) and Jeff Donahue (Midstream/Generation); Andrea Reed keeps Capital Structuring and Portfolio Management. ←[[m00028]]
- [1999-12-16] Revised memo: Midstream groups serve utilities, municipals, co-ops, energy service companies and IPPs. ←[[m00036]]
- [1999-12-30] Owner forwarded the 16 Dec memo to his AOL. It also names Transaction Development seniors: Midstream Billy Lemmons, George McCormick, Erin Norris, Russ Porter; Downstream Rodney Malcolm; Generation Thomas Suffield; Upstream Brad Dunn, John Curtin, Chris Hilgert. Duran's QF team: Greg Blair, Doug Clifford, Dick Lydecker. ←[[m00114]]
