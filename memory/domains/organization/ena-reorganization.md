# ENA organizational changes, Dec 1999 and Jul 2000

Detail ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-07-w28]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-08-w31]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-08-w33]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-10-w41]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-11-w45]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-12-w49]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2001-02-w05]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2001-07-21]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2001-10-28]]

Announced by Cliff Baxter and Kevin Hannon (ENA Office of the Chairman). ←[[m00028]]


- [2001-07-19] 'Changes at EBS' (12 Jul; via Cassandra Schultz, owner to Martin Lin and Shanbhogue): EBS becomes a unit of Enron Wholesale Services, support folded into EWS; commercial under Scrimshaw (Europe), Duenner (Asia), DiMichele (Americas), NA origination under Seeligson; Racicot keeps NA trading and risk plus the global portfolio; Portland closes by 1 Oct, staff told by 27 Jul. ←[[m08737]]
- [2001-10-24] Enron Americas (Louise and John): Central and Eastern gas mid-market, origination and wellhead pricing join Fred Lagrasta's Derivatives group as one Eastern producer unit; West and Texas unchanged (Whitt, Redmond; origination Tycholiz, Redmond); Laura Luce leads Strategic Asset Development; Frank Vickers adds Central mid-market and origination. ←[[m09576]]
