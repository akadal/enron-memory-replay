# ENA organizational changes, Dec 1999 and Jul 2000

Detail ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-07-w28]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-08-w31]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-08-w33]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-10-w41]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-11-w45]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-12-w49]]

Announced by Cliff Baxter and Kevin Hannon (ENA Office of the Chairman). ←[[m00028]]

- [2000-11-08] Enron Wholesale Services (after Sutton's exit): Frevert Chairman and CEO, Whalley President and COO; Louise Kitchen CEO of Enron Net Works; Enron Americas: Delainey CEO, Lavorato COO; Enron Global Markets: McConnell CEO, Shankman COO; Enron Global Assets: Rebecca McDonald, Jim Hughes COO; Sally Beck runs mid/back office (to Kitchen and Causey); Haedicke EWS General Counsel; EGEP and Enron Wind to Cliff Baxter. ←[[m04112]]
- [2000-12-04] EWS Legal reorganised (Frevert, Haedicke) to align with the units; policy group: Lance Schuler (Enron Americas), Mark Evans (Europe), Mark Taylor (Net Works), Alan Aronowitz (Global Markets), Julia Murray (Industrial Markets), Bruce Lundstrom (Global Assets). ←[[m04600]]
- [2000-12-07] Cathy Phillips circulates the latest EGM presentation; updates go to Mike McConnell. ←[[m04655]]
