# ENA organizational changes, Dec 1999 and Jul 2000

Detail ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-07-w28]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-08-w31]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-08-w33]]

Announced by Cliff Baxter and Kevin Hannon (ENA Office of the Chairman). ←[[m00028]]

- [2000-07-31] Enron Global Markets (Office of the Chairman): Mike McConnell President and CEO, Jeff Shankman COO, Mark Frevert Chairman; crude and products John Nowlan, coal George McClellan, currency, equities, rates and agriculture Gary Hickerson, insurance and weather Jere Overdyke; metals and Enron Credit.com stay with Enron Europe, paper and pulp in North America. Net Works: Jeff McMahon President and COO, Louise Kitchen CCO, Philippe Bibi CTO (with Whalley its Office of the Chairman). ENA: Delainey President and CEO, Lavorato COO (Shankman's former reports); Frevert stays ENA Chairman. ←[[m02625]]
- [2000-08-17] EGM realigned: Overdyke keeps Global Risk Markets (insurance); Mark Tawney (weather) reports to the EGM Office of the Chairman; Brent Price joins as VP Operations and CAO (to the Chairman's office, Sally Beck, Causey; controllers Sheila Glover, Todd Hall, Scott Earnest; Tom Myers accounting); Cindy Skinner HR. ←[[m02865]]
