# ENA organizational changes, Dec 1999 and Jul 2000

Detail ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-07-w28]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-08-w31]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-08-w33]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-10-w41]] ←[[ena-reorganization-2000-11-w45]]

Announced by Cliff Baxter and Kevin Hannon (ENA Office of the Chairman). ←[[m00028]]

- [2000-10-12] EGM additions (to its Office of the Chairman): Larry Lawyer (ex-EBS treasurer) leads new finance activities; Eric Gonzales (London) and Rick Bergsieker (now Dubai: Middle East, Puerto Rico assets) co-head LNG, Gonzales also Pool Markets origination under Joe Gold; Jennifer Fraser builds market fundamentals; Gary Hickerson chairs a Traders' Roundtable and a cross-commodity trading group. ←[[m03561]]
- [2000-11-08] Enron Wholesale Services (after Sutton's exit): Frevert Chairman and CEO, Whalley President and COO; Louise Kitchen CEO of Enron Net Works; Enron Americas: Delainey CEO, Lavorato COO; Enron Global Markets: McConnell CEO, Shankman COO; Enron Global Assets: Rebecca McDonald, Jim Hughes COO; Sally Beck runs mid/back office (to Kitchen and Causey); Haedicke EWS General Counsel; EGEP and Enron Wind to Cliff Baxter. ←[[m04112]]
