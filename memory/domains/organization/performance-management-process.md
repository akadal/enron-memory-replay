# Enron officer titling and Performance Review Committee process

- [1999-12-15] Office of the Chairman: four officer levels — 4 Office of the Chairman, 3 other Executive Committee members, 2 managing directors, 1 vice presidents. ←[[m00026]]
- [1999-12-15] Level 1–2 PRC reviews twice a year: July (feedback) and year-end (feedback, bonus, total compensation). Level 1 is reviewed in the business unit, then cross-calibrated by the Executive Committee plus ~16 MDs; the Executive Committee runs Level 2 reviews and considers all promotions each January. ←[[m00026]]
- [1999-12-15] Individual evaluation drives base pay, bonus and long-term awards; a new company-wide long-term program replaces unit plans, to be communicated before bonuses. PRC reviews for directors, senior directors and GMs started this year; questions go to HR leads (e.g. David Oxley for ENA/EEL). ←[[m00026]]
