# Hannon to Enron Global Risk Management (25 Jan 2000)

- [2000-01-25] Office of the Chairman, effective immediately: Kevin P. Hannon is chairman and CEO of Enron Global Risk Management. His main job is building a bandwidth intermediation business; he also oversees global risk management, reports to the Office of the Chairman (and that of Enron Broadband Services) and sits on the Executive Committee. ←[[m00445]]
- [2000-01-25] Greg Whalley becomes president and COO of Enron North America, focused on ENA's growth; he keeps ENA Risk Management and hands his global responsibilities to Hannon. Rationale cited: wholesale energy growth and a global market for premium broadband services. ←[[m00445]]
