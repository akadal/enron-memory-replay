# GRMS resource dispute (Jan 2000)

- [2000-01-28] "Credit Applications in GRMS", sent by Bjorn Hagelmann but signed by Ted Murphy, to Bill Bradford, Jonathan Le, Gary Hickerson, Philippe Bibi and the owner (cc Rick Buy, Mike McConnell). ←[[m00456]] ←[[m00463]]
- [2000-01-28] Murphy: GRMS is a risk warehouse for RAC, not a trading product, because front-office systems cannot give RAC timely aggregated risk; its plan took over a year; priorities are delegated to Debbie Brackett and Rudi Zipter (meeting Jonathan Le's team and Rick Buy). A meeting was set to divert GRMS resources to another project. ←[[m00456]]
- [2000-01-28] Murphy: delays came from resource diversion and outside dependencies (London IT); staff resigned over lack of support. Projects needing GRMS resources should submit detailed requirements to him and Bradford or self-fund; anyone changing what Market Risk relies on should consult him first. ←[[m00456]] ←[[m00475]]
- [2000-01-30] Owner forwarded the chain to his AOL address, no comment of his own. ←[[m00463]] ←[[m00475]]
