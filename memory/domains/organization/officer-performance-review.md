# Enron officer titles and Performance Review Committee

Detail ←[[officer-performance-review-2001-03-w12]]

- [1999-12-15→] Office of the Chairman: four officer levels (4 Office of the Chairman, 3 other Executive Committee, 2 managing directors, 1 VPs and grandfathered SVPs). Levels 1-2 rated by a PRC with standard criteria in July (feedback) and year-end (feedback, bonus, total pay); Level 2 by the Executive Committee, Level 1 at business-unit level then cross-calibrated. Promotions considered each January; the rating drives base, bonus and long-term awards; a new long-term plan comes before bonuses. HR lead for ENA/EEL/Global Trading: David Oxley (x33557). ←[[m00026]]
- [2000-10-20] Year-end 2000 Performance Management: PEP (pep.corp.enron.com) open 25 Oct-17 Nov for reviewer suggestions and feedback; PRC meetings from 20 Nov show employee profiles (update eHRonline). ←[[m03755]]
- [2001-03-19] Owner sends David Oxley a NYT piece on suits against forced ranking (Microsoft, Ford, Conoco): "I think we should follow the developments and adjust our processes to avoid legal exposures." ←[[m06281]] ←[[m06289]]
- [2001-04-16] Gina Corteselli (Global Performance Management): the generic PRC 360 forms shown to the PRC Committee a week ago suit commercial staff, not technical groups; add a job-specific block or descriptors?
