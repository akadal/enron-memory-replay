# Enron officer titles and Performance Review Committee

Detail ←[[officer-performance-review-2001-03-w12]] ←[[officer-performance-review-2001-05-13]] ←[[officer-performance-review-2001-05-18]]

- [1999-12-15→] Office of the Chairman: four officer levels (4 Office of the Chairman, 3 other Executive Committee, 2 managing directors, 1 VPs and grandfathered SVPs). Levels 1-2 rated by a PRC with standard criteria in July (feedback) and year-end (feedback, bonus, total pay); Level 2 by the Executive Committee, Level 1 at business-unit level then cross-calibrated. Promotions considered each January; the rating drives base, bonus and long-term awards; a new long-term plan comes before bonuses. HR lead for ENA/EEL/Global Trading: David Oxley (x33557). ←[[m00026]]
- [2001-05-09] Mid-year 2001 PRC (Anne Labbe, HR, x5-7809): PEP opened 7 May; Skilling's 9 May memo: feedback closes 25 May, PRCs 4-29 Jun, Executive PRC 23-24 Jul; ratings 1-5 (1 best, 1-4 bonus-eligible; aim 5/30/30/20/15%); post-2000 hires not rated; up to 7 reviewers; staff review supervisors. 11 May: training off, self-evaluations list 5 accomplishments. ←[[m07815]] ←[[m07840]]
- [2001-05-15] Owner is a mid-year reviewer for Steve Leppard (his last PRC as Research; also Herbelot, Lewis, Clarke): "It will be my pleasure."; for Naveen Andrews: "With delight." 16 May Labbe: pick at least 5 reviewers in PEP, which closes 25 May. ←[[m07875]] ←[[m07885]] ←[[m07900]]
