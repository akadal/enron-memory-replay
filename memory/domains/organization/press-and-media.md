# The owner and the press after Enron's collapse

Detail ←[[press-and-media-2002-01-13]]

Earlier PR advice (Mehta's book): memory/domains/organization/power-play-response.md.

- [2001-12-15] Owner's formal complaint to the Associated Press about Kristen Hays, who quoted him in 'Analyst: Enron Crash a Morality Tale': "I did not talk to Ms. Hays. She did not ask me any questions."; she overheard a private talk with a friend at a luncheon table: "Eavesdropping on a private conversation is not an acceptable behavior in a civilized society."; he offers a witness who sat next to him. ←[[m10300]]
- [2001-12-19] Crenshaw (17 Dec, to the owner and Anita Dupont): Kelly Summers gave the Financial Times an interview (FT archive id 011210000787); owner copies it to AOL. ←[[m10334]]
- [2002-01-12] Owner forwards Fortune's 'Caught Off Balance' (Herb Greenberg, 21 Jan issue): Egan-Jones cut Enron to junk about a month before S&P and Moody's; interest-coverage screens flag Ford, Hewlett-Packard, Gap. ←[[m10860]]
- [2002-01-13] Owner forwards John LeBoutillier's op-ed 'Biz School Blindness Sank Enron' (9 Jan), from Shalesh Ganjoo's post to enronresearch@umich.edu. ←[[m10876]]
