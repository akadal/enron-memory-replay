- [2000-05-05] Cliff Baxter leaves ENA for Corp as EVP and Chief Strategy Officer; Mark Frevert becomes ENA Chairman and CEO, stays Chairman of Enron Europe; John Sherriff becomes President and CEO of Enron Europe (Net Works' expansion, US-Europe coordination). ←[[m01607]]
- [2000-05-22] Jeff McMahon (EVP, Treasurer) becomes CCO of Enron Net Works; Ben Glisan (head of EGF Structured Finance; ex-Arthur Andersen) becomes Treasurer and joins the Executive Committee. ←[[m01760]]
- [2000-06-23] SAP time entry from 1 Jul via eHRonline or timekeepers: Crenshaw keeps the 19th floor, Osman and Samer; Sam Smith and/or Kevin Moore the 32nd-floor weather group; 16-30 Jun hours in SAP by 3 pm 30 Jun (manual forms to her by 28 Jun). ←[[m02092]]
- [2000-06-29] EnronOptions (Office of the Chairman), 2001-2005: non-qualified options worth 25% of base salary (5% a year over 5 years), Board grant on 29 Dec 2000 (approved 1 May, Board approval pending); eligibility fixed 29 Dec, some units excluded, later hires prorated; AESOP (1994-2000) participants gained 1,119% since inception (at a $70 stock price). ←[[m02192]]
