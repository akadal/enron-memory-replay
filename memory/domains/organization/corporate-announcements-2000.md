# Enron corporate announcements, 2000

- [2000-10-24] Vice Chairman Joe Sutton leaves after eight years (Lay and Skilling: he made Enron global), citing Enron's decreased emphasis on international asset development. ←[[m03788]] ←[[m03789]]
- [2000-10-31] Concur's XMS (intranet expense reports linked to SAP) replaces the Excel form: Corp (company 0011) from 30 Oct, other units through Jan 2001; training at IT Central or online (LEAP). ←[[m03944]]
- [2000-11-07] ENA retention policy (Frevert, Haedicke): inbox, sent mail, bulletin boards 30 days, trash 15, folders one year, voicemail 90 days; IT purges automatically; exceptions only from Haedicke or Richard Sanders. ←[[m04097]]
- [2000-11-14] Weekly Electronic Newsletter for ENA, Industrial and Global Markets (Frevert's request; ENA PR: Kathie Grabstald writes it up, Michelle Vitrella sends reminders, x3-9610): McConnell wants each group head to name a contact who sends bullet points. ←[[m04220]]
- [2000-12-10] eSource (Enron's research service) launches eSearch, esource.enron.com (8 Dec): research databases, report purchases, training, research requests and monitoring for all units. ←[[m04708]]
