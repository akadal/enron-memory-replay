- [2000-08-23] Enron Industrial Markets (paper, pulp, lumber, steel) within Wholesale: Jeff McMahon President and CEO, Ray Bowen COO, Mark Frevert Chairman; Bryan Burnett, Bob Crane, Greg Hermans, Rodney Malcolm (Transaction Development) report to it; outsourcing for other industries goes to EES; Louise Kitchen becomes President and COO of Enron NetWorks. ←[[m02965]]
- [2000-09-26] Memorial Hermann leaves United HealthCare in Houston/Beaumont (UHC refused a 40% fee rise); Enron has UHC pay its claims In-Network 28 Sep-31 Dec 2000 ($10 office copay, 90% otherwise); Cigna may follow; 2001 enrollment soon. ←[[m03313]]
- [2000-09-27] International accounting (CALME, APACHI, South America) merges into one Enron Corp group under Jeff Sommers; Cassandra Schultz joins Rick Buy's RAC as VP, Market Risk Management; Kent Castleman becomes VP and CAO of Enron Industrial Markets. ←[[m03345]]
