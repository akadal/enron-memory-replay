# Enron corporate announcements, 2000

- [2000-01-17] Global Risk Management Operations created under Sally W. Beck (VP; reports in this role to Rick Causey, EVP and Chief Accounting Officer): best practice, staffing, training, shared systems, a global internal-control audit plan; regional teams stay in their units; Sue Frusco's Houston team (South America, Australia) reports to her. Beck stays VP Energy Operations, ENA. ←[[m00363]]
- [2000-01-21] Analyst conference press release (owner sent it to his AOL): 2000 goals are Wholesale growth, Retail (EES) break-out and rapid build of Enron Broadband Services (the renamed communications business: bandwidth intermediation, content delivery); EnronOnline >450 customers, >10,000 trades since late Nov 1999; EES 16,500 facilities; Sun Microsystems broadband deal. ←[[m00419]]
- [2000-01-25] Office of the Chairman: Kevin P. Hannon becomes chairman and CEO of Enron Global Risk Management (mainly building bandwidth intermediation, plus global risk management oversight), on the Executive Committee and also reporting to Enron Broadband Services' Office of the Chairman; Greg Whalley becomes president and COO of ENA, keeps ENA risk management and hands his global duties to Hannon. ←[[m00445]]
