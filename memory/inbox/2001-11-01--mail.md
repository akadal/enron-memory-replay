From: Neale, Nelson </O=ENRON/OU=NA/CN=RECIPIENTS/CN=CNEALE> · Subject: Plastics Models

- Nelson Neale told Vasant Shanbhogue (cc the owner) that he and Mauricio are redeveloping forward curves for 7 plastics: ethylene contract, ethylene spot, propylene contract, propylene spot, polypropylene, HDPE, LLDPE.
- Nelson Neale and Mauricio dropped a single VAR model for the plastics because ~5 years of data leaves too few degrees of freedom; they are building individual models per commodity, using the initial VAR results as a guide.
- Plastics model approach (Nelson Neale): first-differenced prices; ethylene and propylene modeled on AR terms, WTI and natural gas; polypropylene on propylene RG/CG; HDPE and LLDPE on ethylene. Aware of cascading error; alternative is WTI and gas only.
- Nelson Neale said monthly supply/demand fundamentals exist for polypropylene, HDPE and LLDPE and will be incorporated into the plastics models.
- Alan Engberg sent the current model's 12-month plastics forward curves and his own 12-month projections; Nelson Neale will compare revised model output against both before deciding next steps.
