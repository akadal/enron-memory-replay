From: Brad Horn · Subject: Option P&L

- Brad Horn asked Stinson Gibner and Zimin Lu (cc the owner) to explain how the ERMS system calculates the gamma component of option P&L, which is reported in dollars; he says the risk management accounting group does not understand it well either.
- The ERMS system decomposes option P&L into new deals, curve shift, gamma, vega, theta, rho, drift and 2nd order adjustments.
- Brad Horn said Bridgeline has 2 books with option exposures (NYMEX and Gas Daily). Both were dynamically hedged during a large price move, but P&L on the underlying position did not offset the gamma P&L.
- Brad Horn hopes that understanding how gamma dollars are calculated in ERMS will help guide Bridgeline's hedging decisions.
