From: Michael Tribolet · Subject: Re: Default rates

- Michael Tribolet told Mark Ruane, the owner, William Bradford and David Gorte that Moody's June 1999 study 'Debt Recoveries for Corporate Bankruptcies' finds average time in bankruptcy of 1.30 years (median 1.15, std dev 1.20).
- Michael Tribolet noted a slide in firm-level recovery values from 1996 to 1998 and speculated that high-yield issuance tilted toward tech companies yields lower recoveries due to quick technological obsolescence.
- Michael Tribolet compared December 2000 credit spreads with December 1999 spreads. He expected a pronounced flight to safety, but found the figures mixed.
- Mark Ruane sent Michael Tribolet, William Bradford, David Gorte and the owner an analysis of the impact of assumed recovery rates on market-implied default rates, following their discussion.
- The default-rate analysis put market-implied cumulative default rates at multiples of S&P historical rates: AA 67.0x (1yr), 19.4x (5yr); BBB 5.6x/5.5x/6.2x (1/5/10yr); B 1.1x/1.2x/1.7x.
