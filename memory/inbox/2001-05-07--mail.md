- The owner's 05/07/2001 column, forwarded to Stinson for a look and for Sam Smith (wsmith2@enron.com), covers technical analysis. It reviews Carol Osler's (Federal Reserve Bank of New York) paper 'Currency Orders and Exchange-Rate Dynamics: Explaining the Success of Technical Analysis'.
- The owner's column reports that the Osler paper finds two successful technical-analysis predictions in FX. First, trends reverse at support/resistance levels. Second, trends accelerate when those levels are breached.
- The owner's column reports Osler's explanation: take-profit and stop-loss orders cluster around round rates ending in 00 or 50. Roughly 2.8% of stop-loss buy orders vs 10.5% of take-profit sales orders trigger at round numbers.
- The owner's view: orders may cluster because simplified rules help traders handle large volumes of information, or because traders follow technical analysis. In the latter case technical analysis is self-fulfilling. (owner, verbatim: "In this case, technical analysis becomes aself-fulfilling prophecy.")
