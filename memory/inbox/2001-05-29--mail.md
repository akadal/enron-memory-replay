## 07:29

(mail · received · m08032 · automatic capture)
From: Lu, Zimin · Subject: Options approach for EOL price setting

- Zimin Lu told the owner he formulated an approach using option pricing for EnronOnLine (EOL) price-setting rules over the weekend; initial results look interesting and he will write it up for the owner's input.
- Zimin Lu's EOL idea: the market maker is compensated for providing liquidity as if selling options; a hit ask is a trader exercising a call, a lifted bid a put, with the premium embedded in the bid-ask spread.
- Zimin Lu's model charges the market maker for two costs: risk-free interest for carrying the MTM inventory value to the next period, and a charge for risk capital at a RAROC rate reflecting the market maker's risk attitude.
- Zimin Lu derived a formula for EOL bid and ask prices as a function of last executed price, inventory level, price volatility, trade size, order arrival rate, risk-free rate and RAROC rate.
- Zimin Lu's EOL pricing model ignores limit orders, since he says very few limit orders trade on EnronOnLine.
