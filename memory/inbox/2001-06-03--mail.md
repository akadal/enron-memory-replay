- On 2001-06-03 the owner sent himself, from his AOL address to his Enron address, an article titled 'The Alchemy of Decision' by Anguel Grigorov, attached as 'The Alchemy of Decision_wjk.doc'.
- Grigorov's article lists 11 psychological traps in decision-making, such as anchoring, status quo, sunk-cost, framing and overconfidence. He says they come from a recent book by three Harvard professors.
- Grigorov proposes that before each decision, decision makers score themselves 1-5 on their susceptibility to each trap. They then record the outcome as 1 for good and 0 for bad, over many decisions (say 100 trading decisions).
- Grigorov suggests combining the pre-decision bias scores into a single index that predicts good outcomes, using logistic regression or neural networks. He would use questionnaires to keep the self-scoring sincere.
- Grigorov argues that an objective market-forecasting score built from public information may be doomed. In his view, trader judgment is the corrective factor between public opinion and reality, and that judgment can be educated and developed.
