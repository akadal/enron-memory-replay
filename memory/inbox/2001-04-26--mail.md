- Amitava Dhar told Mike Mumford, Scott Salmon and Eric Kirkpatrick (cc the owner) the final data spec for the Short Term Private Firm Model: bankrupt, default and nondefault accounts with 4 quarterly snapshots plus 12 months of monthly performance after the latest snapshot.
- For validation of the Short Term Private Firm Model, Amitava Dhar asked for rolling data (4 quarterly snapshots + 12 months performance) for 4 monthly shifts, covering 16 months of performance, with snapshots from 1998 December end through 1999 December end.

- Amitava Dhar said the team thinks it makes sense to spend the extra amount for the right data, which requires D&B to give a modified quote; he asked Mike, Scott and Eric for their thoughts so they could move forward quickly.
