## 03:22

(mail · sent · m01479 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: RE: Houston visit

- Soussan Faiz of Texaco (faizs@texaco.com) met the owner in Houston the week before 04/25/2000 and was given a copy of the 'Managing Energy Price Risk' book. Faiz hopes to meet him again on the next Houston visit.
- Soussan Faiz asked the owner to review a summary of take-aways from their meeting on Enron's risk practices, to make sure nothing was misstated, before Faiz conveys it to Texaco management. The owner forwarded it to his AOL address.
- Per Faiz's summary of the meeting with the owner: Enron uses no corporate price premises, only market price info with mark-to-market accounting. It models price dynamics with Heath-Jarrow-Morton methods on home-grown software.
- Per Faiz's summary, the owner's research group is made up of mathematicians, physicists and operations researchers who build Enron's risk tools. Investment proposals are screened and real-options valued by a separate corporate group working with the BUs.
- Per Faiz's summary, Enron does not use efficient-frontier portfolio concepts; it 'VC funds' credible opportunities and monitors them to 'fail fast'. Rewards are based on P&L and new business creation, and most employees hold stock options.

- Soussan Faiz views ROA as not everything but a powerful nucleus for the creation, protection, and delivery of value. She credited Navigant for a focal chart illustrating this.
- Recipients of Soussan Faiz's real options email included people at PwC (Adam Borison, Gardner Walkup), Duke, SDG (jmatheson), Stern Stewart (jmccormack), Univ. of Maryland, Bocconi, and Univ. of Cyprus.
