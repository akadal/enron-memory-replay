(mail · received · m03627 · automatic capture)
From: Zimin Lu · Subject: Re: Option P&L

- Brad Horn asked Zimin Lu and Stinson Gibner (cc the owner) on 10/12/2000 how the ERMS system computes the gamma component of option P&L in dollars, as Bridgeline's NYMEX and Gas Daily option books' underlying P&L did not offset gamma P&L after a large price move.
- ERMS decomposes option P&L into new deals, curve shift, gamma, vega, theta, rho, drift and 2nd order adjustments, per Brad Horn; he said the risk management accounting group does not understand the gamma component well.
- Zimin Lu told Brad Horn he believes ERMS computes curve-shift P&L as the change in market value from today's vs. yesterday's price curve (including delta, gamma and higher-order terms), then takes gamma as 0.5*gamma*(price change)^2 and delta as curve shift minus that.
- Zimin Lu said the ERMS theoretical gamma may poorly explain delta changes, especially for big price moves, because of higher-order contributions.
- Bridgeline has two books with option exposure: NYMEX and Gas Daily; Brad Horn works on them, with Jay Knoblauh, Robert Shiring and Vladimir Gorny also on the thread.
