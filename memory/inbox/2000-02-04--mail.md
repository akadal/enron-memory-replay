## 10:14

(mail · sent · m00552 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: El Paso / ENA Deal Completely Terminated Now

- Kimberly Watson sent the owner a summary (originally from Lorna Brennan, ET&S Business Intelligence) on 2000-02-04 that El Paso Natural Gas and Enron North America mutually terminated a $38M deal for 1.2 Bcf/d of capacity signed in December.
- The terminated deal covered three capacity agreements (Blocks I, II, III). Enron's Jan. 27 letter to El Paso sought exit only from Block II (614 MMcf/d), after a mid-January FERC order restricted its delivery rights.
- Per the summary, the capacity agreements were terminated effective Jan. 31. El Paso planned an open season for the capacity from Feb. 7 to Feb. 14, and it was unclear whether El Paso would continue seeking FERC rehearing.
- El Paso spokesman Mel Scott called the release a mutual agreement. John W. Somerhalder II, president of El Paso Energy's Pipeline Group, said it was released for fairness and good customer relations.
- The owner forwarded this El Paso/Enron termination news to his personal AOL address on 2000-02-04 with no added comment.
