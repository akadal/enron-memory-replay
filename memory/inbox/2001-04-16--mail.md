## 03:07

(mail · received · m06877 · automatic capture)
From: Winston Jia · Subject: RE: clustering for power

- Winston Jia told Tanya Tamarchenko that simulating secondary power curves needs correlated jump sizes, unlike the current secondary price curve simulation (assumes perfect correlation) and the secondary gas basis simulation (based on hedging ratio).
- Winston Jia said power basis curve positions are actually price positions and are hard coded by adding basis to the corresponding region curve; he wants to remove this by loading price curves for all basis locations.
- Winston Jia said Power F curves are currently mapped directly to corresponding region curves; he prefers loading price curves instead of price differences.
- Winston Jia asked Research (Tanya Tamarchenko's group, cc the owner) for jump size correlations for secondary power curve simulation in VaR.
- Winston Jia noted that these power curve changes involve much new development unless a simpler way to simulate secondary power curves is used.
