From: Enron Announcements · Subject: Important Information About United HealthCare - Please Read!

- Enron Announcements told Houston employees that Memorial Hermann Health Systems terminated its contract with United HealthCare (UHC) in the Houston/Beaumont area after UHC countered its request for a 40% fee increase.
- Enron authorized UHC to process claims incurred between September 28 and December 31, 2000 at Memorial Hermann as In-Network for UHC Network/EPO enrollees. Office visits carry a $10 copayment and other charges are covered at 90%. Hospital admissions still need UHC pre-certification.
- Enron Announcements said Cigna may also be in contract negotiations with the Memorial Hermann hospital group. Open enrollment materials for 2001 elections are coming soon.
- UHC claim forms can be obtained at 1-800-332-7979 or in Houston at 713-853-7979 (press 1). If a provider requests payment up front, submit an itemized statement with a claim form to UHC for reimbursement.
