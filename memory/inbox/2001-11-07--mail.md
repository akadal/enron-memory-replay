## 14:28

(mail · received · m09856 · automatic capture)
From: "Les Clewlow" <Les_Clewlow@compuserve.com> · Subject: RE: Please advice on the estimate of mean reversion rate for electricity price

- Les Clewlow (Lacima Group) replied to Visit Phunnarungsi about his mean reversion estimate for Queensland electricity prices. He said a positive OLS slope is usually caused by regressing the lagged return instead of the leading return.
- Les Clewlow's recommended method for estimating mean reversion speed: regress the return of the period following the log price, dx = x(t+dt) - x(t), against x(t).
- Visit Phunnarungsi's OLS results on half-hourly Queensland prices: intercept -0.3931 (0.0076), slope 0.1171 (0.0022), R square 0.0585. The positive slope prevented him from estimating a mean reversion rate.
- Les Clewlow's reply was cc'd to Chris, Julie and Michael at lacimagroup.com.
