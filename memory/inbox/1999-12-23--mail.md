From: Robert Brooks <rebrooks@earthlink.net> · Subject: GPCM Summary 1999

- Robert Brooks (RBAC) wrote in a 1999-12-23 year-end letter to GPCM licensees that the number of GPCM licensees doubled to 10 during 1999.
- Brooks said RBAC plans to convert GPCM from Office 97 to Office 2000 in 2000 and make sure it runs in the Windows 2000 environment.
- Brooks listed planned 2000 GPCM features: more flexible transportation zone price curves for pipeline users, a capability for modeling future FT contracting, and an upgraded Database Comparison program with selectable automatic updates.
- Brooks said RBAC will keep working with RDI on pipeline and storage infrastructure data, GASdat-GPCM interfaces, and optional database updates. Mike Farina and RDI were credited for GPCM data and GASdat improvements.
- RBAC acquired gpcm.rbac.com and gpcm.net for a planned licensee website. Brooks said licensee interviews on enhancements would be conducted in the 30-60 days after the letter. Liam Leahy, Charles Carr, Richard Berman, Richard McBride were thanked for 1999 work.
