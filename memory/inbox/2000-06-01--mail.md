## 02:19

(mail · sent · m01872 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: VaR

- The owner told John Arnold that his team has been working on VaR issues, focusing on Jim Schwieger's storage book as of 5/25 and 5/26, which showed counterintuitive results; he sees the book as a good candidate for a systematic review of the VaR process. (owner, verbatim: "This book is a good candidate for a systematic review of the VaR process.")
- The owner reported that the VaR problem appears to come from forward-forward vols used by the VaR system: cumulative VaR jumps on Jan 04, where an abnormal FF vol, much different from the previous day, hits a relatively large position. (owner, verbatim: "It seems that the problem arises from forward - forward vols used by the VaR system.")
- The owner named two possible causes of the abnormal FF vol: a bug in the code, which his team is reviewing with the IT person who wrote it, or a poorly conditioned forward vol curve, where a kink or discontinuity would do it.
- The owner proposed developing a fwd-fwd vol generator for traders so they can review the fwd vol curve before it is posted and smooth any weird fwd-fwd vol it produces. (owner, verbatim: "develop for the traders a fwd-fwd vol generator  allowing them to review the fwd vol curve before it is posted.")
- Tanya Tamarchenko was copied on the owner's VaR review email to John Arnold, Jeffrey Shankman and Jim Schwieger, which suggests she is involved in the VaR work.
