From: Bob Peebler <Bob.Peebler@Halliburton.com>@ENRON · Subject: RE: Proposed Fee Structure

- The GEMI draft proposes a set number of free certificate slots per membership level instead of discounts, maximum participation in seminars and forums with pro-rationing if needed, and conference discounts growing with membership level.

- GEMI fee structure discussion includes UH contacts warga, bobcasey and pkumar (uh.edu) and members from Andersen, Shell, ExxonMobil, Dynegy, Halliburton, EDS, Cinergy, Marathon, Duke and IBM.
