## 08:41

(mail · received · m04285 · automatic capture)
From: Naveen Andrews · Subject: Re: smoothing methodology for extracting forward forward volatilities

- Naveen Andrews reported that exponential fits (a+b exp(-cx)) to implied vol curves fit well but gave negative forward-forward vols in bootstrapping.
- Naveen Andrews tried a power law a + b(t+c)^(-1) on the 12 monthly curves; fits were good and forward-forward vols stayed positive. He prefers exp(-t) variants (mean-reverting vols) but sees the power law as a practical implementation alternative.
- Tanya Tamarchenko implemented a smoothing method for extracting forward-forward vols from implied vol curves: split the forward vol curve into 12 monthly curves, each fitted with y=a+A/power(x+b, beta).
- Tanya Tamarchenko found beta in (0, .5) keeps the fitted function suitable for bootstrapping ff vols (positivity condition holds); she showed an NG example and planned to try it for power as well.
- Forward-forward volatility smoothing work involves Tanya Tamarchenko, Naveen Andrews, Alex Huang, with the owner, Vasant Shanbhogue, Vladimir Gorny and Ted Murphy copied (Nov 2000).
