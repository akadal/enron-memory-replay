## 02:42

(mail · received · m00025 · automatic capture)
From: Clayton Vernon · Subject: gas prices

- Clayton Vernon proposed to the owner a gas price model built from two time series: interconnect flow rates (from telemetry) and spot-market prices, and said he would like to proceed with building it.
- Clayton Vernon told the owner the model could detect pipeline capacity hoarding as elevated basis against actual inflow, market power by separating physical gas cost from transmission cost (basis), and storage as inflow minus consumption.
- Clayton Vernon proposed calibrating price elasticities of demand for consumption, storage and transmission, plus the extent of hoarding, from flows and prices, treating storage and capacity hoarding as exotic call options at the consumption hub.
- Clayton Vernon said flows could be imputed from prices, yielding a purely stochastic model of the basis in terms of weather, and that he believes the problem is quite tractable.

## 10:05

(mail · received · m00026 · automatic capture)
From: Office of the Chairman · Subject: Performance Management Process

- The Office of the Chairman announced (1999-12-15) standardized Enron officer titling in four levels: Level 4 Office of the Chairman, Level 3 other Executive Committee members, Level 2 managing directors (incl. company presidents, some SVPs), Level 1 vice presidents and grandfathered SVPs.
- Level 1 and Level 2 officers are now evaluated by a Performance Review Committee (PRC) with standard criteria and ratings, twice a year: July for feedback, year-end for feedback plus bonus and total compensation.
- Under the PRC, the Executive Committee reviews Level 2 officers. Level 1 officers are reviewed first at business-unit level, then cross-calibrated by the Executive Committee and about sixteen managing directors.
- Per the Office of the Chairman, the Executive Committee will consider all officer promotions in January each year, with infrequent exceptions. The performance evaluation is the starting point for base pay, bonus and long-term awards.
- HR Business Unit Lead for ENA/EEL/Global Trading is David Oxley (X33557), named as the contact for questions on the PRC process. A new long-term program replacing individual or business-unit plans will be communicated before bonus payments.
