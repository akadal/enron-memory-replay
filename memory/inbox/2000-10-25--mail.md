## 08:07

(mail · sent · m03824 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: Matthew Patteson, Inventor

- The owner sent Cindy Derecskey the research group's preliminary review of documentation from inventor Matthew Patteson about a proposed generator, and said one more person will review it. (owner, verbatim: "This is our preliminary review of the documentation sent by Mr. Patteson.")
- Stinson Gibner (VP Research, Enron North America) told the owner Enron should not engage in testing, development, or licensing of Mr. Patteson's proposed generator: Enron is not in the generation-equipment business and lacks facilities and expertise to test it.
- Stinson Gibner judged the Patteson generator, which induces current from magnetic dipoles moving in a boiling medium, unlikely to produce significant or steady power, and risky to test because it boils flammable material under pressure.
- Stinson Gibner asked Martin Lin to review the Patteson documents and give an independent opinion; he also invited Kevin Kindall and Zimin Lu to review the proposal if interested.
- Stinson Gibner noted Enron's only exception to staying out of generation-equipment development is its ownership in entities developing wind and solar renewable energy.
