## 18:13

(mail · sent · m10877 · automatic capture)
From: VKaminski@aol.com@ENRON · Subject: Fwd: Doc Draft

- The owner forwarded to his Enron address a doc draft (from vincek@cs.stanford.edu, 13 Jan 2002) describing a stock-trading analysis application that reads a stock's trading history in Yahoo! Finance CSV format (date, open, high, low, close, volume).
- The application computes a fast or slow stochastic (%k over kN periods, %p as SMA of %k over pN periods; slow version uses 3-day SMA of %k) and searches the data for Japanese candlestick Hanging Man and Hammer patterns.
- Trading rule in the doc draft: buy when the day's candlestick is a hammer and %k is above a set value; sell when it is a hanging man and %k is below a second set value. It simulates ten years of trading with a constant share size.
- The doc draft computes RoR as (sale cost - purchase cost)/purchase cost and annualized RoR as (RoR+1)^(365/duration)-1 for long and short positions, and notes that a better method is needed for shorts. (owner, verbatim: "(a better method is needed for shorts)")
- The doc draft defines a Hanging Man as a very short top shadow, medium body and long bottom shadow, with adjustable ratios. A Hammer is a Hanging Man whose close is higher than its open.
