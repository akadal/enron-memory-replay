From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: GRI and Gas Storage

- On 03/27/2000 the owner forwarded to Mike Roberts a GRI gas storage study press release that Robert (Bob) Brooks of RBA Consultants had sent to his GPCM distribution list on 03/22/2000.
- The GRI study 'Natural Gas Storage Overview in a Changing Market Environment' (GRI-99/0200), by Energy and Environmental Analysis Inc., says lower 48 working gas storage capacity will grow from 3.8 Tcf in 1998 to 4.6 Tcf in 2015.
- The GRI study projects US gas demand rising 50%, from 21.3 Tcf in 1998 to 32.8 Tcf in 2015. Power generation and industrial use are expected to make up nearly 80% of the growth.
- The GRI study expects about 75% of storage capacity additions after 2005, needing about $5.0 billion (1998 dollars) in investment over 1998-2015.
- The GRI study found that more than half of firm storage contracts in place in spring 1999 expire by 2004, and 70% by 2006. Average remaining contract length fell from 6.8 years in 1996 to 6.2 years in 1999.
