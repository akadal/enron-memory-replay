(mail · received · m02894 · automatic capture)
From: Darrell Duffie <duffie@Stanford.EDU> · Subject: Re: Full version

- Darrell Duffie (Stanford) read the owner's volatility chapter (co-written with Grant Masson). He told the owner he likes it and finds it non-controversial and well written, though the topic is huge and some things are not discussed.
- Duffie told the owner the chapter gives too much attention to OLS and MLE estimation, since the results don't use it. He suggested stating the GARCH likelihood function so the iid estimation material works as a lead-in to the models actually used.
- Duffie told the owner that in equity markets, risk premia explain volatility smiles far more than fat tails do. He said the chapter discusses fat tails at length and mentions risk premia only briefly.
- Duffie recommended Jun Pan's paper, available from her web page, on the impact of risk premia on smiles. He noted that it covers only the S&P 500 and that Enron's markets are much different.
