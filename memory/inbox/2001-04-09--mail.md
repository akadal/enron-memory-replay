## 01:46

(mail · sent · m06607 · automatic capture)
From: VKaminski@aol.com <VKaminski@aol.com@ENRON> · Subject: Fwd: NumeriX Solutions for Enron

- Dean Tallam of NumeriX sent the owner a follow-up on NumeriX solutions for Enron after a phone conversation. He scheduled a call with the owner for Monday April 9, 2001 at 1:30 the owner's time (2:30 PM NY time). His phone is (212) 302-6225.
- Dean Tallam described the NumeriX energy solution as an Excel/C/C++ environment for electricity derivatives. It runs a Monte Carlo microeconomic model balancing utility demand against plant generation to get regional marginal prices. Transmission constraints yield monthly forward border (COB) prices.
- NumeriX product line per Dean Tallam: NumeriX Tools (C/C++ toolkit), NumeriX FI and NumeriX FX are available. NumeriX EQ is in development and scheduled for release in Q3 of 2001. NumeriX CR (credit derivatives) is scheduled for the later half of 2001.
- NumeriX analytics support tree, Monte Carlo, stochastic mesh and calibration methods. Models covered include Hull-White/Extended Vasicek, Black-Karasinski, Black-Derman-Toy, Black-Scholes, and multi-factor Hull-White and Black-Karasinski.
- The owner forwarded the NumeriX material, including the attachment 'NumeriX FI 2001.pdf', from his AOL account to his Enron account on 2001-04-09.
