- Ted Murphy asked the owner, David Port, Rick Buy, Rudi Zipter and Stinson Gibner for comments on his summary of the proposed 24x7 EOL trading of prompt WTI; after collecting them it may go to Greg, John, Jeff and others involved.
- Ted Murphy's summary recommends on-site human monitoring for 24x7 prompt WTI trading on EOL, citing public relations, operational bugs, and unforeseen market events during off-exchange hours and long weekends.
- Ted Murphy's summary advocates live trading simulation before the WTI EOL launch, with incentives to 'bust' the system, because the historical simulations used daily, not intra-day, prices.
- Ted Murphy's summary proposes a daily position limit for 24x7 WTI (a sub-limit of the overall Global Products limit), with the open position reduced under it at least once a day.
- Ted Murphy's summary also proposes a trigger widening the fixed spread after a number of consecutive same-side trades, and buying deep out-of-the-money puts and calls to protect against extreme events.
