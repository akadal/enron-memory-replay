## 03:32

(mail · received · m02346 · automatic capture)
From: Tom Arnold <tarnold@finance.lsu.edu> · Subject: Re: EFA meetings

- Tom Arnold (LSU finance) told the owner he will see what becomes of the EFA meetings session and keep the owner informed.
- Tom Arnold and Tim Crack have a revised version of the paper Arnold gave the owner; using certainty equivalence makes their option pricing model more robust.
- Arnold says their model prices options under the real-world measure, combining mean-variance-skewness asset pricing (Harvey and Siddique, JF June 2000) with a skewness binomial model (Johnson, Paulukiewicz, and Mehta, RQFA 1997).
- Arnold argues the model's benefit is in a mean-variance-skewness world: skewness likely differs between risk-neutral and real measures, and parameters can come from non-risk-neutralized historical data.
- Tom Arnold asked the owner for comments on the version of the paper the owner has and offered to send the new version if the owner wants it.
