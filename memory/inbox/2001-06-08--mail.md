(mail · received · m08487 · automatic capture)
From: Neale, Nelson · Subject: Housing Data and Stationarity

- Nelson Neale told Eugenio Perez (cc the owner and Vasant Shanbhogue) that he ran Dickey-Fuller and Augmented Dickey-Fuller unit root tests on housing starts data to check stationarity, following a meeting the day before.
- Nelson Neale tested housing starts over three periods: 1959-2001 (503 obs), 1984-2001 (201 obs), and 1996-2001 (62 obs), because some explanatory variables (e.g. % population change) have limited history.
- Nelson Neale concluded the housing starts series is stationary, with the possible exception of 1984-2001 (ADF 5 lags -1.158, ADF 1 lag -2.053, vs critical value -1.95 at p=0.05).
- Nelson Neale advised Eugenio Perez that since he will likely use the entire housing starts series in an AR framework, the stationarity assumption holds.
