## 09:48

(mail · received · m05604 · automatic capture)
From: "John D. Martin" <J_Martin@baylor.edu> · Subject: Latest draft

- John D. Martin (Baylor, Carr P. Collins Chair in Finance) sent Don Chew a heavily restructured draft of the Enron paper (Enron_Paper_1_26_01.doc), cc the owner, and asked for comments to wrap it up.
- John D. Martin's paper storyline: Enron moved from a regulated gas pipeline (mid-80s) to an unregulated trading unit built on a 'gas bank' model, which forced it to develop elaborate in-house risk management since no central bank supplied liquidity.
- Martin's draft argues Enron's model needed entrepreneurial staff and a flat, decentralized structure, managed via semi-annual performance reviews, pay tied to performance, and free internal movement of personnel.
- Martin's draft also cites asset optionality, network value for trading platforms, and mark-to-market accounting (for transparency and prompt action) as attributes of the Enron model.
- John D. Martin told Don Chew he looks forward to seeing him in February.
