## 01:25

(mail · received · m04398 · automatic capture)
From: Pinnamaneni Krishnarao · Subject: Re: American type swaption calculations.

- Eugene Zeitz wrote a memo on problems with American style options (American type swaption calculations) and sent it to David Draper (EES) on 11/21/2000; Draper asked Pinnamaneni Krishnarao and Paulo Issler for their opinion.
- Krishna (Pinnamaneni Krishnarao) told David Draper, cc the owner, that the swaption issue is illiquid market information, not the calculation; the gas desk supplies prices and volatilities and must decide how to account for illiquidity.
- Krishna's view: correlations matter less than volatility or price; since they are for the same underlying at the same location across time, reasonable assumptions can be made to compute them.
- Krishna disagreed with the concept of a market top: the desk hedges the options by hedging delta and exercising them optimally at the same time.
- Krishna said a 2-year swap cannot proxy a 10-year swap: longer-term prices and volatilities are driven by production costs and economic variables, not shorter-term issues.
