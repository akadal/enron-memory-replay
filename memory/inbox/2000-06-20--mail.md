## 08:39

(mail · received · m02042 · automatic capture)
From: Tanya Tamarchenko · Subject: Re: backtesting for different percentiles

- Tanya Tamarchenko sent Vladimir Gorny (cc Grant Masson, the owner) VAR backtesting results on real NG forward prices from 1/1/99 to 6/7/00, for NG-PRICE-PRC positions as of 6/6/00 and STORAGE-PRC positions as of 5/25/00.
- Tanya Tamarchenko's backtest: with implied vols, PnL fell below VAR95/90/85 on 2.93/4.11/5.57% of days (Portfolio 1) and 4.1/6.74/9.97% (Portfolio 2), well under the 5/10/15% theoretical rates.
- Tanya Tamarchenko's backtest: with historical vols, breach rates were about 6.5-7.6% at VAR95, 10.6-12% at VAR90 and 15.5-16.1% at VAR85, close to theoretical; she argues this favors using historical vols over implied vols.
- Tanya Tamarchenko found the backtest results did not depend on the decay factor (1, 0.97, 0.94) for historical vols.
- Tanya Tamarchenko noted VAR95 breach frequencies above 5% indicate fat tails in NG forward price changes.
