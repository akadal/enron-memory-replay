- Ron Baker said both sets of Raptor forwards were valued thoroughly back in March. He is certain the valuations of the 18MM-share forwards included their contingent nature. The 12MM-share valuations had no such component.
- Ron Baker said he believes there was a meeting with a couple of managers from Arthur Andersen to discuss the general methodology of the Raptor forward valuations.
