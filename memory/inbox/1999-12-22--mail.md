From: Clayton Vernon · Subject: Re: working gas price model

- Clayton Vernon told the owner he has a simplified version of Brad's working gas price model. Assumptions: no-arbitrage equates trading margins across the country; transmission costs rise with network congestion; wellhead supply is nearly perfectly price-elastic; burner-tip demand is nearly inelastic; storage is a perpetual call option.
- Clayton said the model has 8 parameters to estimate at each major node. Least time-variant: storage injection/withdrawal costs, then wellhead-to-pipeline delivery costs. Intermediate: capacity-dependent pipeline transmission charges above shrinkage. Most time-variant: trading margins and storage option valuations.
- Clayton said the 8 node parameters can be identified either from a short time series of the last 3 days' prices, or from point calibrations using only today's data, based on a node-based model of competition between pipelines.
- Clayton Vernon told the owner he will write up the simplified gas price model in Scientific Word and present it to the owner at his earliest convenience. Message dated 1999-12-22, cc Vasant Shanbhogue.

From: Anjam Ahmad · Subject: New Volatility Curve Generator for UK Power

- Anjam Ahmad announced (1999-12-22) a new UK power volatility curve generator, with curves down to EFA/monthly detail, marked to market up to 6 years out; beyond that, volatility decays to a long-term level.
- Anjam said swaption traders can fit the first 5-6 years of the curve to market baseload swaption implied vols (typically 3-12 month swap tenors), then price EFA-slot swaptions consistently with the curve.
- Anjam said the curves will be reset as the market moves, enabling mark-to-market for the volatility book, and may affect the daily VaR calculation.
- The spreadsheet model is saved at T:\READWRTE\ELEC_UK\MODELS\VOLCURVEGENERATORNEW.XLS and was attached for Houston staff to review.
- Anjam asked Stinson Gibner to give an opinion or audit of the new volatility curve model to check nothing was missed. Vince Kaminski was copied; Dale Surbey was also copied.
