From: Clayton Vernon · Subject: Re: working gas price model

- Clayton Vernon told the owner he has in mind a simplified version of Brad's gas price model: a no-arbitrage condition equating trading margins across the country, transmission costs rising with congestion, and storage treated as a perpetual call option.
- In Clayton Vernon's proposed gas model, wellhead supply is almost completely price-elastic while burner-tip demand is almost completely price-inelastic.
- Clayton Vernon's gas model has 8 parameters per major network node, identifiable either from a short time series of the last 3 days' prices or by point calibration on today's data using a node-based model of pipeline competition.
- Clayton Vernon ranks gas model parameters by time variation: storage injection/withdrawal costs least, then wellhead-to-pipeline delivery costs, then capacity-dependent transmission costs; trading margins and storage option values vary most.
- Clayton Vernon committed to write up his working gas price model in Scientific Word and present it to the owner at the owner's earliest convenience (cc Vasant Shanbhogue).
