(mail · received · m09862 · automatic capture)
From: "VISIT PHUNNARUNGSI" <visit_thailand@hotmail.com>@ENRON · Subject: RE: Please advice on the estimate of mean reversion rate for electricity price

- Visit Phunnarungsi wrote to the owner, Chris Strickland and Les Clewlow on November 02, 2001. He is modelling the Queensland electricity spot price with a Geometric Brownian Mean Reverting Jump Diffusion Model, using half-hourly prices from 13 December 1998 to 30 June 2001.
- Visit Phunnarungsi told the owner he had earlier asked the owner about his article "The Challenge of Pricing and Risk Managing Electricity Derivatives". He said the owner mailed him a copy.
- Visit Phunnarungsi followed the Clewlow/Strickland paper "Making the most of mean reversion" to estimate mean reversion speed. His OLS result had a positive slope of 0.1171 and an intercept of -0.3931 (R2 0.0585), so he got no usable mean reversion rate.
- Les Clewlow replied on 7 Nov 2001 that this result usually comes from regressing the lagged return instead of the leading return. He said to regress dx = x(t+dt) - x(t) against x(t).
- Les Clewlow copied Chris Strickland, Julie Brennan and Michael Booth (lacimagroup.com) on his reply. Visit Phunnarungsi said he would try the suggestion and report back.

From: Kaminski, Vince J </O=ENRON/OU=NA/CN=RECIPIENTS/CN=VKAMINS> · Subject: FW: OStrip Function

- John Griffith told Larry May (cc the owner, John Arnold, Paulo Issler, Zimin Lu, others) that the change to the Ostrip model's functionality caused larryoptmodel to misvalue gas daily options.
- John Griffith explained the old Ostrip model wrongly used NowToSet days for fixed price options (FwdStFlag=0); larryoptmodel relied on that, so the new Ostrip model, which ignores NowToSet, gives incorrect values.
- John Griffith said larryoptmodel must be changed: one date input (today for fixed price options, strike setting date for forward start options) used to compute NowToSet, BegDays and EndDays.
- John Griffith stated it is unexcusable to change models without consulting traders first; model problems must be reported to traders right away and changes communicated before they are made.
- The owner forwarded John Griffith's 2001-11-08 Ostrip function memo to his personal AOL address.
