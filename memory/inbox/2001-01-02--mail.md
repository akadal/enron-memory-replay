(mail · received · m05104 · automatic capture)
From: Zimin Lu · Subject: EOL WTI Historical Trade Simulation - more profitable trading strategy

- Zimin Lu told Greg Whalley and John Lavorato (cc the owner, Stinson Gibner) that in the EOL WTI historical trade simulation, a market maker is more profitable by reducing volume per trade and increasing the daily number of trades, keeping total daily volume constant.
- Zimin Lu's reasoning: in a trending market, smaller, more frequent trades follow the market more closely and lose less. Example: a $30 to $35 move with $1 half bid-offer spread gives -$150,000 MTM with 5 trades of 10,000 BBL vs -$250,000 with one trade of 50,000 BBL.
- Zimin Lu reran the scenario Stinson Gibner sent to Greg Whalley and John Lavorato on Dec. 27 (30,000 BBL per trade), using 10,000 BBL per trade and 3 times as many trades; profitability increased in almost all cases.
- Zimin Lu asked Greg Whalley and John Lavorato to send their thoughts on the smaller-trade EOL WTI strategy to Stinson Gibner or him.
