- Eric Groves (cc Scott Earnest, Eric Moon) told the owner he will be in the office Tuesday morning and hopes they can meet among themselves and then with Shankman to discuss progress and next steps on the models, including Eric's 'book' model.
- Marg built the Enron LNG Project NPV model. ENE cash inflow is (Henry Hub + basis at Elba) x unloaded volumes from Qatar, Algeria and Venezuela. Outflow covers Elba COS, Newbuild and Hoegh Galleon charter hires, terminaling, voyage costs and FOB. Lake Charles was left out.
- LNG NPV model assumptions per Marg: Henry Hub at $3.55 through 2003, then $2.90/MMBtu (customizable). FOB set at $1.00/MMBtu for Qatar and Algeria and $2.30/MMBtu for Venezuela.
- LNG NPV model shipping: instead of a 50/50 Algeria/Qatar split, Marg modeled 7.6 cargoes/yr from Qatar (Hoegh Galleon at max) and 13.9 cargoes/yr from Algeria (Newbuild, capped by Elba terminal capacity). Newbuild volumes start 1/1/03. Venezuela volumes go on the Newbuild because its voyage costs are cheaper.
- Known gap in the LNG NPV model per Marg: Newbuild voyage costs should drop on 1/1/04 when volumes switch from Algeria to Venezuela, but the model does not yet do this. Lake Charles would only be needed to model spot cargoes before Elba (pre-4/1/02).
