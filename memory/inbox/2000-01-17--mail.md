From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: Re: one more thing

- Clayton Vernon (2000-01-17) proposed a theory he calls "uncoupling" of basis: severe Midwest cold can lift Henry Hub spot prices while Northeast basis sits below the Louisiana-to-Northeast shipping charge.
- Clayton Vernon argues that the notion of equilibrium cannot always assume spot gas is flowing along all nodes of the gas network, because spot gas may not move between some nodes at a given time.
- The owner agreed with Clayton Vernon's uncoupling view. The owner said the price divergence happens when pipeline capacity is insufficient to move gas. (owner, verbatim: "This would happen when there is an insufficient pipeline capacity to move gas.")
- The owner told Clayton Vernon that such market developments happen quite often and are one reason the owner wants the model Clayton is working on. (owner, verbatim: "The market developments you describe happen quite often")
