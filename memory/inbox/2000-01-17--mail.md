(mail · sent · m00353 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: Re: one more thing

- Clayton Vernon proposed to the owner a theory he calls "uncoupling" of basis: e.g. severe Midwest cold can lift Henry Hub spot prices so Northeast basis looks smaller than the commodity charge to ship gas from Louisiana to the Northeast.
- Clayton Vernon's view: this happens when spot gas is not moving from Louisiana to the Northeast, so a gas network equilibrium model cannot always assume spot gas flows along all nodes of the network.
- The owner agreed with Clayton Vernon's basis uncoupling theory. He said it happens when pipeline capacity is insufficient to move gas. (owner, verbatim: "I agree. This would happen when there is an insufficient pipeline capacity to move gas.")
- Clayton Vernon is working on a gas market model for the owner. The owner said basis uncoupling events happen often and are one of the reasons the group wants this model. (owner, verbatim: "this is one of the reasons we want to have the model you are working on.")
