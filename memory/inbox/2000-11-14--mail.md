From: Margaret Carson · Subject: Re: Presentation slides

- Margaret Carson reviewed EP's slide presentation for John Goodpasture and the owner and judged it too optimistic: merchant plants built and run baseload by 2005, and offshore gas volume growth by 2005.
- Margaret Carson told the owner that EP's 1.4 BCFD of new western power-gen gas demand by 2005 ignores NIMBY siting hurdles that stalled all but 550 MW of 10,000 MW planned in the West; WEFA/ENE see 3.2/2.9 BCFD by 2010, mostly late.
- Margaret Carson noted EP sees West South Central TX/LA at 2.7 bcfd by 2005 vs WEFA -400 mmcfd and ENE +500 mmcfd by 2010, PIRA 3.5 bcfd by 2010; ENE/WEFA assume merchant plants shut in or repower old thermal units.
- Margaret Carson noted EP sees Northeast gas demand growth of 2.7 bcfd by 2005 vs WEFA, PIRA and ENE at 2.3/2.5/2.6 bcfd by 2010, calling EP's view way premature.
- Margaret Carson doubts EP's forecast of 3.6 bcfd offshore supply growth by 2005 (offshore fell 2.4 bcfd since 1995); Cambridge Energy sees 1.1-1.7 bcfd by 2005. Shell's 1999 forecast of +2 bcfd by 2000 instead saw a 500 mmcfd drop.
