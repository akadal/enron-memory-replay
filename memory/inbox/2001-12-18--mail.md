- Enron Office of the Chairman announced (2001-12-18) that NETCO was formed to rebuild the North American gas and power wholesale business under new ownership. Auction procedures are being filed this week, closure is expected in January, and Enron would keep a 49% or smaller non-controlling interest.
- The Office of the Chairman said the US Trustee formed a 15-member Creditors' Committee. It will monitor asset liquidations, review cash flow projections and approve Enron's reorganization plan.
- Jeff McMahon (EVP & CFO) was added to Enron Corp's Office of the Chairman alongside Ken Lay and Greg Whalley. He is the primary contact with the Creditors' Committee. Ray Bowen was named EVP, Finance and Treasurer.
- Mark Muller was appointed EVP Corporate Development, handling asset disposals. Wholesale Services (ENA, EBS, EIM, EGM) was reorganized under Jim Fallon.
- Michael Brown, Mark Frevert, Steve Kean, Mike McConnell and Jeff Shankman have left or will leave Enron by end of December. John Lavorato and Louise Kitchen resigned from the Management Committee to arrange NETCO's joint venture energy activities.
