From: Ted Murphy · Subject: Re: Copper curve

- Ted Murphy asked Tani Nath to contact Lloyd Fleming about who from RAC should review the copper curve methodology.
- Ted Murphy's view on curve roles: the commercial team posts the curve, and operations gathers objective information on its efficacy at least monthly and reports all highly sensitive curves.
- Ted Murphy stated that RAC will not approve or disapprove a curve. RAC will question methodology, motivations and later changes in a senior management forum, i.e. 'tell on' suspect curves or curve movements.
- Ted Murphy stated RAC wants curves to give the best estimate of product value in each time bucket today. RAC favors objective, consistent application of methodology within and across commodities.
