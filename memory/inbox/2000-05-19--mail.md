## 08:35

(mail · received · m01727 · automatic capture)
From: Chonawee Supatgiat · Subject: XPRESS and CPLEX Runtime Comparison results

- Chonawee Supatgiat tested CPLEX and XPRESS-MP optimization software on the EBS network design problem (test: 10 nodes, 77 cables, 5 cable speeds) and forwarded the runtime comparison to the owner on 05/19/2000.
- Chonawee Supatgiat found XPRESS more user friendly and cheaper with less development time, but CPLEX significantly faster in computation time, especially in integer programming; XPRESS had to be stopped on test cases 4 and 6.
- Chonawee Supatgiat recommended that the EBS group use CPLEX rather than XPRESS for the network design optimization work.
- Chonawee Supatgiat's 05/17/2000 comparison went to Samer Takriti, Stinson Gibner and Ravi Thuraisingham, cc Tom Halliburton, Pinnamaneni Krishnarao and Ming-Lung Lee (Sycamore), asking for their views.
