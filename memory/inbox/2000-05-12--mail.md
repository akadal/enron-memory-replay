From: Vincent Tang · Subject: Independent Variables for ENE Regressions

- Vincent Tang told Bradley Eixmann (cc the owner) that regression work on ENE stock drivers tested about 30 comparable stocks, 10 capital market indices and three energy commodity indices, with regular regressions plus lags and leads; most correlations were unstable over time.
- Vincent Tang reported that only the S&P Utility Index (SPXU) and Dow Jones Utility Index (UTIL) showed stable, significant relationships with ENE stock, but R2 was below 30% and Durbin-Watson statistics warned of autocorrelation.
- Vincent Tang concluded the regression analysis confirms it is hard, if not impossible, to find a real driver of ENE stock; since Enron added a new core business several months ago, the stock behaves more like a telecom stock.
- Vincent Tang said more time is needed to accumulate market data to capture ENE stock's characteristics in its new phase, and offered Bradley Eixmann to investigate more variables if requested.
