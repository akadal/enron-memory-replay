- On 2000-01-20 the owner told Shelley Corman that he and colleagues are working on a numerical estimate of the value of an incumbent shipper's right of first refusal (ROFR) in auctions. (owner, verbatim: "we try to come up with a numerical estimate of the value of this option")
- The owner said the ROFR gives the incumbent something of value it never paid for, with distributional consequences, and a numerical estimate could help argue against it. (owner, verbatim: "he has something  of value he never paid for")

- Clayton Vernon (economics background) wrote an essay arguing the ROFR harms efficiency: it deters bids, lowers offers, lets an inefficient incumbent win, and invites winner's curse and rule-of-thumb matching. Cc'd: Stinson Gibner, Zimin Lu.
