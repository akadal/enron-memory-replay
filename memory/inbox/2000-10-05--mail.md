## 07:48

(mail · received · m03445 · automatic capture)
From: Naveen Andrews · Subject: Re: implementing term-structure of correlations for power

- Tanya Tamarchenko proposed implementing a term structure of correlations for power in the VAR model: instead of one correlation matrix from prompt-month prices, use a separate correlation matrix for each prompt month.
- Tanya Tamarchenko found that power factor loadings estimated from prices 25-jun-00 to 25-sep-00 look messy, so joint estimation of factors across several regions does not look realistic.
- Tanya Tamarchenko reported that R10/R11 correlations are periodic with a one-year period, so 12 matrices from the first 12 forward contracts could be reused for later months; 3-5 months of history give close results and a decay factor smooths the curves.
- Tanya Tamarchenko said implementing multiple correlation matrices would not significantly affect the speed of calculations in the VAR model.
- Naveen Andrews replied that power correlations are seasonal (e.g. NP-15/SP-15), but NP-15 vs R1B (Nepool) may not share the same periodicity, so a correlation term structure for power would need different rules for different regions.
