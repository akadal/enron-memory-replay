- Joseph Hrgovcic told the team that Longitude has a new implementation (in place but withheld so as not to complicate initial auctions) allowing packages of digital spreads to be traded as one instrument, giving terraced payouts approximating calls, puts, collars and straddles.
- Joseph Hrgovcic's view: Longitude structures remain largely digital, so they are dirty hedges for traditional calls and puts; auctions last only a few hours and fills are not guaranteed, limiting suitability for hedging.
- Joseph Hrgovcic noted Longitude auctions are Dutch auctions, but limit orders assure a given payout ratio; posting of tentative prices is at the market maker's discretion.
- Chonawee Supatgiat wrote a paper 'Analysis of Longitude' (gave the owner a hardcopy) and believes the Longitude system will attract speculators more than hedgers, though real-time tentative price posting now lets hedgers participate.
