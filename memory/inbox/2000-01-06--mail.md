From: Clayton Vernon · Subject: Re: natural gas storage item

- Clayton Vernon sent the owner an item, noted as from a class, on natural gas storage in the 2000-01-06 message 'natural gas storage item'.
- Clayton Vernon said the industry is rapidly adopting Operational Balancing Agreements, under which the pipeline contracts with producers and LDCs to handle all shipping imbalances, so shippers book scheduled rather than actual flows.
- Clayton Vernon said storage in producing regions is operated to smooth weekday-weekend patterns (parking) and wellhead output variations, and to protect the integrity of the pipeline system.
- Clayton Vernon said estimated marketed production is likely a better estimate of actual flow at a pipeline's receipt meter than production less local storage.
