## 10:53

(mail · sent · m01473 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: Valuation of Internet companies

- On 2000-04-24 the owner sent himself, at vkaminski@aol.com and vincek@leland.stanford.edu, a Financial Times article by John Kay on valuation of Internet companies, "Stretching the figures".
- Kay's FT article argues that McKinsey-style new-economy valuations ignore competition driving margins down. Examples: McKinsey's $37bn for Amazon.com, and a mock valuation of Citigroup's forex business as "C.com" at $5,000bn.
- Kay criticizes a model by Paul Gibbs, head of M&A research at J.P. Morgan, that values Freeserve at £6.50 per share. The model implies £2bn of profit on £2.5bn of revenue in 2017, an 80% margin.
- Kay critiques Glassman and Hassett's book Dow 36,000. It assumes a 5.5% required return and 5% dividend growth. At 5.25% growth the Dow would be 72,000; at a 6% required return it would be 18,000. Kay says equities still need a risk premium.
