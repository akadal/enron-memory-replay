From: Sandeep Kohli · Subject: Dispatch within Maharashtra

- Sandeep Kohli told Jim Hughes that a Henwood model run on Dabhol plant dispatch within Maharashtra (one-part tariff, no power leaving Maharashtra) shows MSEB needs between 1200 and 410 MW seasonally out of the 2184 MW plant.
- Sandeep Kohli: Henwood run shows MSEB's average 2002 bill for DPC dispatch at $41 MM/month (Jan up to $69 MM, at 7.21 cents/kWh) vs. estimated MSEB payment capability of $38-46 MM/month; MSEB can pay if not liable for full 2184 MW capacity payment.
- Sandeep Kohli concluded that because Maharashtra demand varies seasonally, Dabhol's remaining power may need to be marketed to other states as seasonal products rather than long-term blocks.
- Sandeep Kohli said Vince (the owner), Stinson and he are on Rebecca's calendar for Friday to make the complete Dabhol pitch to her; he invited Jim Hughes to attend. The Dabhol presentation was also sent to Wade, who is to set a time to review it.
- Sandeep Kohli said no new Henwood runs are planned for now, though the team may continue work on the fuel and hedging side for Dabhol.
