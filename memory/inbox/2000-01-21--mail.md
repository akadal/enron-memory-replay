## 03:10

(mail · received · m00414 · automatic capture)
From: Zimin Lu · Subject: Template for pricing the right of first refusal

- Zimin Lu told Shelley Corman and Chris Gaskill (cc the owner, Clayton Vernon, Stinson Gibner) that he set up a template for pricing rights of first refusal (ROFRs) on pipelines.
- Zimin Lu's ROFR model prices the ROFR as a series of forward start options; the at-the-money strike set in the future mimics the 'matching the best bid' feature.
- In Zimin Lu's ROFR model the underlying is the 'best bid', closely related to the price differential between the two hubs the pipeline connects; volatility is estimated from best-bid price history.
- Zimin Lu noted that, as the owner pointed out, ROFR valuation is case dependent; Zimin Lu named himself and the owner as contacts for questions on the model.
