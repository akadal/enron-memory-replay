From: Sandeep Kohli · Subject: Mark-to-Market

- Sandeep Kohli asked Robert Butts to analyze how a new India transaction would be marked to market, and to send a note afterward. Krishnarao (Vince Kaminski's group) would explain the deal and seek an appointment with Bob.
- Sandeep said the India deal is structured through EIPL (Enron India), which reports under Indian GAAP. US consolidation uses US GAAP, where Sandeep wants mark-to-market accounting. EIPL is held through Mauritius and then the Cayman Islands.
- Sandeep raised a tax problem: US tax accrues in the year of mark-to-market (2000), while Indian accrual accounting shows no income until 2002 or 2003. He asked whether US tax credit is possible and whether US tax applies if income stays overseas (subpart F).
- Sandeep asked Bob to consider whether accounting can solve the problem that Indian corporates may not use a US fixed-for-floating swap. The team has worked on a fixed price contract for this. Krishnarao is to brief Bob on three structures.
- Sandeep said there are currently no such transactions from the Indian side, but the deal is important and may be repeated in coming months, so setting up an accounting system for it may be worthwhile. Vince Kaminski was copied.
