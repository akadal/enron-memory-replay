## 08:31

(mail · sent · m00125 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: Audit

- In an Oct 3, 1999 reply on the Model Risk project draft, the owner wrote that model risk is a very significant exposure: a wrong model leads to bad decisions, accounting errors and incorrect financial statements. (owner, verbatim: "a wrong model results in bad decisions and eventually leads to accounting errors and incorrect financial statements.")
- The owner's view: the main unlisted source of model risk at Enron is that final model approval and validation responsibility is dispersed, with no single unit or person authorized to audit, approve and monitor valuation models.
- The owner said the popular perception that the Research Group validates models is far from the truth. Research has become a group of internal consultants on specialized partial problems, rarely working a project from A to Z.
- The owner asked that the draft's inappropriate-application point be expanded with two sub-points: wrong inputs, and an incorrect model used for a given transaction. He cited TVA, whose inputs were not updated for an extended period.
- For the Control Strength section, the owner said several audits of the Research Group model by Arthur Andersen and Darrell Duffie were initiated at his insistence.
