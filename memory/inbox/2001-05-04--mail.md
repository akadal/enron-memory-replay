From: Kristin Walsh · Subject: California Update 5/4/01

- Kristin Walsh's California update of 5/4/01 reported that sources expect no vote on the bond issuance/bridge loan authorization by the May 8th deadline. Republican and Democratic caucuses are reportedly turning against Governor Davis.
- Kristin Walsh reported that some generators say certain long-term California power contracts require the bond issuance by July 1, 2001, or the state may be in breach. Without bridge loan legislation by May 8th, a July 1st issuance would be very difficult.
- Per Kristin Walsh's 5/4/01 update, the bridge financing is $4.125 B: JP Morgan $2.5 B, Lehman Brothers $1.0 B, Bear Stearns $625 M. About $1.6 B is tax-exempt, the projected blended rate is 5.38%, and maturity is August 29, 2001. Lehman may back out.
- Kristin Walsh reported two likely triggers for a SoCal Edison bankruptcy. One is legislative rejection of the SoCal–Davis MOU (approval deadline August 15th). The other is Senator Bowen's amendment paying DWR from ratepayer receipts before the utilities.
- Kristin Walsh reported that the Senate passed SB 6X, authorizing $5 billion to create the California Consumer Power and Conservation Authority. No implementation can proceed until it is clarified how the state and the utilities get paid for power.
