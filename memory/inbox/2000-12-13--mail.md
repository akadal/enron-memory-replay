From: Margaret Carson · Subject: Alliance May Test the System Up to 1.5 BCF/d in Next Two Weeks

- Margaret Carson forwarded to the owner (12/13/2000) an ET&S Market Intelligence note from Lorna Brennan, calling it a good heads up on the Alliance Pipeline.
- Per Alliance spokesman Jack Crawford, Alliance Pipeline has run flat out at about 1.325 Bcf/d since commercial service began Dec. 1; testing to raise flows (est. up to 1.5 Bcf/d with existing compression) likely in a couple weeks, extra capacity split among existing shippers.
- Per TransCanada's Klaus Exner, Alliance had less impact on western Canada outflows than expected: contracts at Empress changed since Nov 1 but physical flows (~6 Bcf/d) did not; Alberta-BC-PG&E leg runs at highest possible load factor.
- PG&E GT-NW (spokeswoman Sandra McDonough) is running full at 2.6 Bcf/d, delivering 900 MMcf/d to Pacific Northwest non-California load and turning back demand; Tuscarora is full.
- Exner said TransCanada mainline flows have become more volatile after Nov 1, with IT volumes up and frequent intraday nomination changes driven by the AECO–Dawn price differential.
