## 10:26

(mail · received · m02795 · automatic capture)
From: Zimin Lu · Subject: risk bucketing for P/L

- Zimin Lu told Ken Shulklapper and Greg Couch (cc the owner, Bob Lee, Stinson Gibner) that the current Taylor-series risk bucketing for P/L is fine for small market moves but could fail when the market makes a big move.
- Zimin Lu proposed an error-proof finite-difference method for P/L attribution by risk bucket and asked Ken and Greg for their views on how to implement it in the transport book.
- In Zimin Lu's finite-difference method, each bucket (delivery/receipt curve shift, Vega1, Vega2, Rho, Eta for correlation, Theta) is the premium repriced with only that input moved to today, minus last day's premium; gamma terms are subtracted from the curve shifts.
- Zimin Lu's method defines Gamma as 0.5 x last day's theoretical spread-option gamma x price shift squared, and daily Theta as the time-only repricing minus drift minus last premium, divided by 365.25.
- Zimin Lu noted that the sprdopt spread option function returns an annualised theta, so it must be converted to a daily theta for P/L attribution.
