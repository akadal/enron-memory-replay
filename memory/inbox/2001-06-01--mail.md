(mail · received · m08163 · automatic capture)
From: Hayden, Frank · Subject: Project X - Outside the box idea

- Frank Hayden floated a Project X idea (for discussion only, not implementation) to the owner and others: add natural gas curves to the power core curve list so power VaR uses NG factors estimated within the power factor group instead of borrowing them from the NG group.
- Frank Hayden said factor loadings are currently done separately for three groups (natural gas, power, liquids), with joint estimation within each group; there are ongoing discussions about adding factors and doing PCA across groups.
- Frank Hayden flagged a VaR problem: power desk hedges with NG, but the VaR simulation pulls NG factors from the separate natural gas factor group, possibly causing VaR measurement issues.
- Hayden's proposed patch: Enpower would create renamed duplicate NG-PWR curves (with 60 days of copied history), include them in power factor generation, and move positions onto them so VaR picks up power-group factors. He warned it could become a legacy.
- Frank Hayden is on vacation and plans to discuss the Project X NG-PWR curve idea seriously the week of June 11th, reassessing then whether the patch is needed.
