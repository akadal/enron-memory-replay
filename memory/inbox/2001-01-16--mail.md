## 00:02

(mail · received · m05330 · automatic capture)
From: Robert Johnston · Subject: California Power 1/15/01

- Robert Johnston (cc Scott Tholan) added the owner to his daily California power situation updates after Kristin (Walsh) mentioned the owner might be interested; Johnston offered additional information on request.
- Johnston's 1/15/01 California update: Governor Davis's aides want to pay about $50-$55/MWh for state-purchased power while suppliers want about $75; Treasury Secretary Summers suggested an auction, but California prefers negotiated long-term contracts.
- Johnston's sources believe Davis is positioning the state to sign long-term power contracts with generators that could be passed through to the utilities after a near-term bankruptcy, with state credit backing purchases.
- Per Johnston's 1/15/01 update, Davis said he would use state credit to remove the risky-debt premium charged to PG&E and SCE, extend the 10% rate increase beyond 90 days, and clear $12 billion of utility back debt over a 90-day forbearance via securitization or state-guaranteed repayment, but no direct state money.
- Per Johnston's 1/15/01 update, a preliminary state audit claims half of the $9-$12 billion the utilities say they owe is owed to their own affiliates; a Davis aide threatened condemnation and state takeover of plants if no progress by the Tuesday deadline.
