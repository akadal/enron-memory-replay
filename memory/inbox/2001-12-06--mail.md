From: De, Rabi </O=ENRON/OU=NA/CN=RECIPIENTS/CN=RDE> · Subject: FW: Margin Capital requirement -- back of the envelope simulation study

- Rabi De sent Jayshree Desai, at her request, a back-of-the-envelope simulation spreadsheet of margin capital required (and capital normalized by VAR) for three levels of underlying volumes; he forwarded it to the owner.
- Rabi De, Karthik Rajan, Tanya Tamarchenko and Vasant Shanbhogue were involved in the margin capital requirement simulation study; Rabi named Karthik, Tanya and himself as contacts for questions.
- Margin capital study assumptions: Natgas underlyings, flat $3.0/MMBtu term structure; 20 representative counterparties picked by MTM on a random day; one-month capital and VAR; volume split evenly over financial/physical and 60 forward months.
- Margin capital study: started from zero MTM, zero collateral and zero margin-call threshold; margin calls = exposure times a factor from 0 (AAA) to 1 (D); exposure per counterparty proportional to its MTM.
- Rabi De reported that the unnormalized results show the trend of capital required with the volume multiplier, but the absolute capital figure depends on the simulated VAR and thus on the assumed volume level.
