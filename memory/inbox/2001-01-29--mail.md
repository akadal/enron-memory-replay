## 02:43

(mail · received · m05621 · automatic capture)
From: Stinson Gibner · Subject: Erac Koch P+ spread options

- Stinson Gibner forwarded to the owner his note to Bob Lee, Paulo Issler and Zimin Lu: the Erac Koch P+ spread options are booked in O:\research\common\projects\exotic2001_0125.xls, on sheet "index deals".
- Mark Fondren (to John L Nowlan, cc Spencer Vosko, 01/25/2001) defined Koch P+ = Price 1 (WTI Cushing physical cash price, i.e. the WTI Nymex contract) minus Price 2 (Koch Oil posting for West Texas/New Mexico Intermediate).
- Per Mark Fondren, the Koch posting = WTI Nymex settle minus the Koch Posting/WTI NYMEX basis spread, minus 66.6% of the Apr01/May01 WTI Nymex spread, minus 33.3% of the Apr01/Jun01 spread.
- Mark Fondren's example with 1/24/2001 WTI settles (APR01 28.31, MAY01 27.72, JUN01 27.19) and a 2.75 Koch/NYMEX basis gave an April 2001 Koch posting of 24.794 and Koch P+ of 3.516.
- Mark Fondren sent the Koch P+ spread option definition to Stinson Gibner on 01/26/2001; Gibner's research group (Bob Lee, Paulo Issler, Zimin Lu) is handling the booking of these exotic options.
