- JB's commentary says the Friday August 10, 2001 natural gas low was only a 'Momentum Bear Market Low', followed by a 20%-plus short-covering rally.

- John D. Martin (Baylor University) sent the owner an SSRN asset-pricing abstracts issue (Vol. 4, No. 36, August 27, 2001) on August 28, 2001. He said the second paper, "Regime Jumps in Electricity Prices", looks interesting.

- The owner forwarded John D. Martin's note on the electricity regime-jump paper to his personal address, vkaminski@aol.com, on 2001-08-28.
- John D. Martin holds the Carr P. Collins Chair in Finance in the Finance Department at Baylor University, Waco, TX. He regularly sends the owner SSRN papers he finds relevant.
