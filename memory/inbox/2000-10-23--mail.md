From: "Leslie Abreo" <leslie.abreo@kalotay.com> · Subject: Hedge Effectiveness Test for Fair Value Hedges

- Leslie Abreo (Andrew Kalotay Associates) wrote to Roger Leworthy and Ron Baker, cc the owner, about AKA's Volatility Reduction Method for FAS 133 hedge effectiveness testing of fair value hedges, attaching their FAS133 article and asking for comments.
- Leslie Abreo reported that the consulting arm of a "Big Five" accounting firm indicated AKA's proposed method for generating test data points would be acceptable; AKA says it avoids statistical problems of overlapping (rolling) quarterly rate differences.
- AKA method: compute daily yield curve changes as ratios over ~2 years of history (~494 vectors, ~247 trading days/yr), randomly pick 62 ratio vectors, multiply them per tenor into one quarterly transformation, and apply it to today's curve.
- In the AKA method, the random selection is repeated until about 100 simulated quarterly yield curves are generated, which are then used in the hedge effectiveness testing process.
