From: Robert Johnston · Subject: California 1/17/01

- Robert Johnston sent the owner and others a California power crisis briefing (1/17/01): bankruptcy of the California utilities appears increasingly likely; SoCal Edison is likely first into Ch.11 after suspending payments to creditors and is in a 30-day cure period.
- Per Johnston's 1/17/01 briefing, AB 1X passed the California assembly with a 5.5 cent per KW/H long-term contract price; generators, consumer groups and creditors are unhappy, and it was expected to pass the Senate and be signed by Governor Davis.
- Per Johnston's briefing, bank exposure to California utilities: Bank of America $215 million, J.P. Morgan $202 million ($417M most immediate concern) out of $12 billion total loans; SCE loans subject to immediate repayment after downgrade to junk.
- Per Johnston's briefing, Southern California Edison suspended a $230M bond payment, $151M to renewable suppliers and $215M to the California PX; Dynegy threatened to take Edison to bankruptcy court if it defaults on a note due Thursday.
- Per Johnston's briefing, PG&E had $500M in cash and reserves with $580M due to the ISO on Feb 1 and $431M due to the California Power Exchange on Feb 15; PG&E's FERC-approved reorganization shielding assets is unlikely to be undone.
