- Krishnarao asked Anshuman Srivastav, Rajesh Sivaraman and Sandeep Kohli (ENRON_DEVELOPMENT) to get 2000 naphtha forwards and vols from John L Chismar (Singapore naphtha trader) and to install the Exotic Options Library on a common drive. Anshuman sent 1987-99 monthly FOB naphtha price data.
