From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: valuing caps on naptha (file included)

- The owner forwarded to his personal address, marked FYI, a 1999-12-10 thread from Pinnamaneni Krishnarao about pricing naphtha caps for Enron's Maharashtra power deal. (owner, verbatim: "FYI.")
- Krishnarao reported that Indian papers and politicians complain about high power prices the Maharashtra state government pays Enron. Naphtha prices more than doubled in 1999, and fuel cost is a pass-through. The Indian side asked for help pricing naphtha caps.
- Krishnarao's indicative monthly naphtha cap premiums for 2000, using 1999 prices as forwards: ATM 10.4%, 5-20% OTM 8.4% to 4.4%, $168 cap 15%, $200 cap 9%, $225 cap 6%, as % of average price. Historical 2-year vol is 34%.
- Krishnarao said the forward prices used are guesses and need real indications. He asked Anshuman Srivastav, Rajesh Sivaraman and Sandeep Kohli to contact John L Chismar, the Singapore naphtha trader, for 2000 forwards and vols.
- Krishnarao asked that the Exotic Options Library be installed on a common drive (G, J, M, O or P) for the Enron Development team. Other drive letters need reprogramming first.
