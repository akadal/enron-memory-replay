From: "Valery Kholodnyi" <vkholod1@txu.com> · Subject: My model for spikes

- Valery Kholodnyi, Manager of Quantitative Analysis, Research and Analytics Group, TXU Energy Trading, wrote to the owner about his model for spikes in power prices and valuation of contingent claims on power.
- Valery Kholodnyi told the owner he recently gave a talk on his spike model at the joint seminar of the Center for Energy Finance Education and Research and the Institute for Computational Finance at UT Austin.
- Valery Kholodnyi asked the owner whether he would be interested in receiving his preprint; he is forming a list of industry and academic specialists to receive it.
- Kholodnyi's method: spikes modeled directly as self-reversing jumps on top of a stochastic process for regular power price dynamics, giving a non-Markovian price process; allows explicit valuation and hedging of contingent claims with spikes.
- Kholodnyi's preprints (TXU-RAG, Jul-Nov 2000) cover: power price process with spikes and European claims, swing options, spark spread options, claims at two grid points, and transmission options, all with spikes.
