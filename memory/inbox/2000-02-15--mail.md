From: Zimin Lu · Subject: Zero curve generator for UK gilts

- Zimin Lu sent Anjam Ahmad (cc Stinson Gibner and the owner) a model for fitting the term structure of UK gilts, a zero curve generator, on 2000-02-15.
- Zimin Lu's UK gilt zero curve model prices each gilt's dirty price as the sum of semiannual coupons (C/2) times discount factors plus 100 times the discount factor at maturity.
- Zimin Lu's gilt model uses a five-parameter analytical form for discount factors, fit by minimizing the sum of absolute errors to derive a smooth zero curve.
- Zimin Lu noted the gilt curve model needs an initial guess for its parameters, which may take some experience, and that its log file shows how well the optimization works.
