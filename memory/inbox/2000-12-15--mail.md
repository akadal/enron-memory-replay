## 08:08

(mail · received · m04865 · automatic capture)
From: Tanya Tamarchenko · Subject: Re: UK Power/Gas

- Tanya Tamarchenko told the owner that mapping one commodity's factor set onto another is common in the VAR system when forward prices are poorly correlated due to poor price history, and she considers it OK since illiquid-price correlations can't be trusted.
- Tanya Tamarchenko's view: if a low-correlation matrix truly reflects the market and can't be reconstructed with the 7 factors, use more than 7 factors or the correlation matrix itself in the VAR engine; factors only speed up calculation for highly correlated prices.
- Oliver Gaylard (Risk Control, London) agreed RiskTrac should calculate all commodity VaRs (continental power/gas, UK power/gas, Nordic Power) but said positions, price/vol curves, correlations and factor loadings must be validated first; he wants milestones and timelines.
- Naveen Andrews reported that in RiskTrac all UK gas curves are mapped to NBP and all power curves to R4 (Cinergy); the UK spreadsheet VaR uses 2-3-year-old factors from US Nat Gas, and RiskTrac UK Power VaR has run 7-8MM above the spreadsheet.
- Naveen Andrews said factor loading analysis works only for NBP and Norwegian curves (liquidity), and urged that all VaR simulations run out of RiskTrac for aggregation; Ramesh's checks indicate positions tie in, though Enpower disconnects are possible.
