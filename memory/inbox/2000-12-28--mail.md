## 01:51

(mail · received · m05060 · automatic capture)
From: Greg Woulfe · Subject: Combinatorial Auction - Jan 11 mtg background

- Greg Woulfe forwarded to the owner, Jim Fallon, Marcello Romano, Paul Racicot and Russell Woody background on combinatorial auctions for a meeting on Jan 11.
- Chonawee Supatgiat reviewed the Steinberg and Kelly paper "Combinatorial Auction with Multiple Winners for Universal Service" (Management Science) and judged combinatorial auctions suitable for bandwidth, where bidders value link combinations. The method is not yet tested.
- Chonawee Supatgiat proposed that Enron run a combinatorial bandwidth auction or exchange to dispose of unused or unsold bandwidth from its long position. He believes no current bandwidth exchange allows combinatorial bids.
- Chonawee Supatgiat suggested Enron act as a dummy bidder in its own bandwidth auction, overbidding low winning bids to keep the product and so impose an indirect reserve price.
- Chonawee Supatgiat cited the Alberta government's PPA auction, in which Enron Canada took part 3-4 months before Dec 2000, as an example of a combinatorial auction in actual use.

## 02:30

(mail · received · m05061 · automatic capture)
From: Alex Huang · Subject: Seasonal factors and curve fitting

- Alex Huang told the owner and research group he wrote C++ code that filters seasonal factors from a forward curve and fits the deseasoned curve to f(t)=a+A/(t+B)^c (0<c<.5) using the Conjugate Gradient method.
- Alex Huang noted, citing Tanya, that the fitted functional form ensures f(t)^2*t - f(t-1)^2*(t-1) > 0, making it usable for calculating forward-forward vol; outputs are seasonal patterns (2 allowed) and the smoothed curve.
- Alex Huang said the seasonal curve-fitting procedure is not useful for VaR calculation but is useful for power plant valuation and credit exposure simulations, adding seasonality to forward-forward vols when simulating forward prices.
- Alex Huang circulated a spreadsheet and XLL file for the seasonal curve-fitting tool and offered to forward the C code to anyone interested.
