From: Kaminski, Vince J </O=ENRON/OU=NA/CN=RECIPIENTS/CN=VKAMINS> · Subject: FW: Argentina FX and CPI curves, dated 6/20/01

- Gwyn Koepke told the owner that updates on the Argentine situation will be provided in the weekly GMM, on which RAC is copied each week; she also shared an internal report with the forecast.
- Gwyn Koepke and Maureen Raymond-Castaneda sent Cindy Hudler the Argentine peso and CPI forecast dated 6/20/01; it assumes a one-for-one peso peg to the US dollar so quarterly revaluations can be completed in time.
- Koepke and Raymond-Castaneda noted the Argentina FX curve is subject to change after a pending Congressional vote to replace the peso-dollar currency board with an equal-weight euro/US dollar basket.
- Koepke reported Argentina's economy ministry introduced a dual-exchange rate for exporters (50% euro/50% USD basket), implying an 8% peso depreciation; Brazil has depreciated 21% vs USD since the start of the year.
- Koepke and Raymond-Castaneda warned that the basket regime change could erode investor confidence and add pressure on the peso, despite the successful government debt swap under economy chief Cavallo.
