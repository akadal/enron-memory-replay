## 07:03

(mail · sent · m08913 · automatic capture)
From: Kaminski, Vince J </O=ENRON/OU=NA/CN=RECIPIENTS/CN=VKAMINS> · Subject: FW: Direct Access 8/10/01

- Kristin Walsh sent a Direct Access summary on August 10, 2001 to Lavorato, Kitchen, Delainey and a wide cc list including the owner. She said her group will keep following the issue and send updates. The owner forwarded it to his AOL address.
- Kristin Walsh reported that the California PUC has stayed its decision on direct access and is expected to rule by August 23rd.
- Kristin Walsh reported on Fred Keeley's Edison bailout plan. The 3,600 largest businesses would pay $3.1 billion of Edison's debts over 15 years. In exchange they could buy their own power by 2003 after paying an exit fee whose terms are not yet set.
- Kristin Walsh reported that California plans to sell $13.4 billion in bonds to repay $8.2 billion of 2001 power costs, with banks led by J.P. Morgan. The banks are worried that direct access would let businesses escape the bond surcharge.
- Kristin Walsh reported that, per CEC statistics, about 88,000 California customers buy energy through direct access: about 10,000 large commercial/industrial customers and about 78,000 residences.
