(mail · sent · m08704 · automatic capture)
From: Kaminski, Vince J </O=ENRON/OU=NA/CN=RECIPIENTS/CN=VKAMINS> · Subject: RE: Request for Assistance from Risk Magazine

- Paolo Cadoni (Technical Assistant, Risk Magazine, Risk Waters Group, London) asked the owner to act as anonymous referee for a submitted paper, "Let's Swing Again (Pricing Non-Storable Commodity Options)"; the owner agreed to help. (owner, verbatim: "I shall be very glad to help you.")
- Paolo Cadoni said the referee report on the swing option paper would be needed by email within around four weeks of receiving the paper (request dated July 16, 2001).
- The paper under review prices a swing (capacity reservation) option on a non-storable commodity with a hybrid model: permanent shocks via binomial tree plus temporary shocks reminiscent of Black (1976), calibrated to futures prices and futures vol term structure.
- The paper's claim, per Paolo Cadoni: as temporary-shock volatility rises, that component dominates the swing option price, so failing to model transient shocks in electricity/gas swing pricing may be a serious modelling error.
