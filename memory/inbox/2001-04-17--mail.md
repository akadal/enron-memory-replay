## 04:05

(mail · received · m06929 · automatic capture)
From: Enron Americas - Office of the Chairman <Enron Americas - Office of the Chairman@ENRON> · Subject: Capital Book

- Enron Americas Office of the Chairman announced creation of a Capital Book, managed by Joe Deffner, with no profit target, to ensure all Enron Americas transactions with capital requirements are structured correctly and allocated the appropriate cost of capital charge.
- Per Enron Americas Office of the Chairman: cost of capital for all Enron Americas transactions will be set by Joe Deffner; this is separate from the RAC process for transactions, which continues unchanged.
- Per Enron Americas Office of the Chairman: on-balance-sheet capital investments and assets currently held keep a 15% capital charge; off-credit deals get a market pass-through of actual cost of capital; off-balance-sheet but on-credit deals priced by impact on credit capacity.
- Per Enron Americas Office of the Chairman: for trading-book deals deploying capital, the Capital Book will take a finance reserve per transaction (like a credit reserve) and charge origination and trading groups at actual cost.
- Questions on the Enron Americas Capital Book go to Joe Deffner (853-7117) and Alan Quaintance (345-7731).

- On 04/17/2001 Jeff Gray (EES, cc Michael Moore) asked the owner whether his group had reviewed Jeff's 04/09 DSM real-options email. He wants a quantitative solution, possibly GARCH or differential equations, before a Thursday meeting with another potential client who uses his own capital.
