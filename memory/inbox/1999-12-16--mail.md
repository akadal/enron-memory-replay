## 05:59

(mail · received · m00042 · automatic capture)
From: Clayton Vernon · Subject: (no subject)

- Clayton Vernon met Chris Germany, Capacity Trader at the East Desk, about gas transmission and sent a summary to the owner and Vasant Shanbhogue on 1999-12-16.
- Clayton reported pipeline capacity is typically leased monthly. Example South Texas to Brooklyn: $12.00/month per 10,000 decatherms ($0.40/day), fixed, plus variable charges of 6% fuel (shrinkage) and 6.5% overhead.
- Clayton's trader model: South Texas delivered at NYMEX - $0.10 basis, Brooklyn at NYMEX + $0.25, spread $0.35, variable transmission cost $0.125, so a trader could offer up to $0.225 for firm capacity tomorrow.
- Per Chris Germany, leaseholders know the firm-equivalent of interruptible capacity well, and many pipelines don't discount firm capacity from the tariff maximum. Secondary markets are a later issue for the model.
- South Texas to Brooklyn has several physical routes (Enron, Texas Eastern, etc.). Traders can cover Enron's positions at each end of a pipeline, freeing capacity for other contracts.
