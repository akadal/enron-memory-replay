From: Johnson, Jan · Subject: the way to look at energy supplies

- Jan Johnson sent the owner a June 4, 2001 New York Times op-ed, 'The Mirage of a Growing Fuel Supply' by Evar D. Nering, as an interesting way to look at energy supplies.
- Nering's article argues that with consumption growing 5% a year, a 100-year resource supply lasts about 36 years, a 1,000-year supply about 79 years, and a 10,000-year supply about 125 years.
- Nering's article argues that at 5% annual consumption growth, doubling reserves adds at most 14 years, while halving the growth rate to 2.5% nearly doubles a supply's life. He concludes supply-side energy policy cannot work.
