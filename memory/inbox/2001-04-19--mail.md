## 01:49

(mail · received · m06985 · automatic capture)
From: Stinson Gibner · Subject: New model for P+ options

- Jeffrey Shankman requested a new option valuation model for P+ options; Stinson Gibner (owner cc'd) is building it, after consulting Spencer Vosko on his needs for managing the options and analyzing historical posting prices vs. Nymex moves.
- Stinson Gibner advised against the Monte-Carlo model Jeff Shankman asked for, due to heavy recalculation time for trader and book administrator; instead he extends the current spread option model so one leg is a basket of commodities.
- For P+ options, one spread leg is the Nymex Swap plus the Posting Basis, which together give the Posting Price.
- Stinson Gibner reported (2001-04-19) the P+ valuation function is ready; remaining step is a test version of the book to calculate P&L impact, working with Spencer Vosko and Mark Fondren on correct inputs and positions.
- Stinson Gibner expects, based on initial experiments, that the new P+ model will not change the value of these options by much.

(mail · sent · m07013 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: EES revenue through customer DSM projects

- Jeff Gray (EES) asked the owner on 04/09/2001 to review a three-page PowerPoint arguing that, in high-volatility power markets, customers using their own capital could lower IRR hurdles for DSM projects because of reduced risk exposure.
- Jeff Gray's proposed method values a DSM project as additive options: the option to do the project (valued by NPV) plus a strip of put options to generate "negawatts" over the installation's life, which he calls "residual time value". He asked the owner whether it can be quantified.
- On 04/17/2001 Jeff Gray (cc Michael Moore) followed up with the owner. He asked whether the Research group's GARCH analyses or differential-equations experts could give a quantitative solution, ahead of a Thursday meeting with another potential client.
- Jeff Gray told the owner the DSM tool is meant for sales and marketing. A customer with an onerously high hurdle rate has set aside a large DSM budget but can spend only a small part, because his capital budgeting ignores forward price volatility.
- Jeff Gray told the owner that helping clients get more DSM projects approved lets EES share more DSM savings, which can turn a low-profitability retail commodity contract into a substantially more profitable one.
