# Dabhol, detail moved out of L1 at the 2000-02-27 consolidation

Moved verbatim from memory/domains/work/dabhol-power-sale.md; new detail of week 2000-W08 added.

- [1999-12-25] Kohli asked the owner and Vasant Shanbhogue for structuring help on selling Dabhol power to another state: a firm peak-period component plus monsoon infirm power. Sivaraman's issue list for a 50 MW firm Phase I sale is to be settled with MSEB; a simple tariff formula tracks the PPA tariffs. ←[[m00100]]
- [2000-01-24] Kohli wants EIPL's fixed-price contract plus US swap marked to market on US GAAP consolidation (EIPL keeps Indian GAAP books; US tax in 2000 vs Indian income 2002-03); Krishnarao briefed Bob Butts 27 Jan. ←[[m00427]] ←[[m00429]]
- [2000-01-28] Butts and Wess: payments tied to one plant cannot be m-t-m (US peakers: power sold outside and bought back elsewhere), only 50% of DPC cashflows would qualify, and an affiliate loan cannot. Options: no m-t-m, sell the contract at a profit, or securitise via an outside Indian party; Wade Cline floated DPC selling to MSEB with EIPL buying elsewhere on the grid. ←[[m00468]] ←[[m00476]]
- [2000-02-11] "Sandeep" (from Bridget D'Silva's address) thanked the owner for a book and Krishna's help; the effort is intensifying, so the training they discussed may wait some months. ←[[m00600]]

New detail, week 2000-W08:
- [2000-02-22] Pinnamaneni Krishnarao sent the owner "India: Enron aftermath" (Business Line, 21 Feb), Arun Ghosh's review of Abhay Mehta's book "Power Play: A Study of the Enron Project", highly critical of Dabhol. Per the article: MSEB must buy (or pay for) 2000 MW from DPC for 20 years; Mehta puts total payments at $35 billion (about Rs 1,25,000 crore at 1998 rates); late-1999 tariff about 11.25 US cents/unit (4.4 capacity, 6.85 energy), about $1.97 billion a year; MSEB dropped 200-250 MW of Tata power (Rs 1.80) and backed down Chandrapur (Rs 1.20) to take Enron power at Rs 5, a loss estimated at Rs 460 crore a year; Pakistan cancelled a similar Enron project in 1996 (782 MW residual-oil plant, $670 million, 6.4 US cents/kWh fixed for 30 years); the World Bank opposed Dabhol. Owner forwarded it to his AOL on 22 and 24 Feb. ←[[m00730]] ←[[m00738]] ←[[m00774]]
