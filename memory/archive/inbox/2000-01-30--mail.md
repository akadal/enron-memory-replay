## 12:13

(mail · sent · m00476 · automatic capture)
From: VKaminski@aol.com · Subject: Fwd: Mark-to-Market

- Wade Cline asked Sandeep Kohli (Jan 28, 2000) whether DPC could sell to MSEB, have EIPL buy an equivalent amount of power from MSEB at another spot on the grid, and then have EIPL sell to the 3rd party state.
- Pinnamaneni Krishnarao reported that Bob and Wess said payments tied to one particular plant cannot be marked to market. For US peaking plants, Enron sold the plant's power to an outside party and bought it back at different locations, and that buyback is marked to market.
- Krishnarao noted that even if Enron could mark-to-market a deal with DPC, it could do so for only 50% of the cashflows, because only 50% of DPC is owned by outsiders. He added that a simple loan to an affiliate also cannot be marked to market.
- Krishnarao said that, politically, DPC selling power to EIPL may not be the best solution. He mentioned a possible trip to Boston on Tuesday and/or Wednesday (Feb. 1-2) and offered to book an appointment with Bob.
- Sandeep Kohli asked Bob (Robert Butts) to send a note on how the India transaction would be marked to market under US GAAP on consolidation. EIPL is structured through Mauritius and the Cayman Islands. He called the deal very important, possibly repeated in coming months, and raised US tax/subpart-F concerns.
