## 08:08

(mail · received · m06436 · automatic capture)
From: Bryan Seyfried · Subject: Asset Swaps vs CDS's

- Bryan Seyfried forwarded to the owner a note from Martin McDermott (to John Sherriff and Bryan Seyfried, 23/03/2001) on asset swaps vs credit default swaps (CDS) pricing.
- Martin McDermott's view: asset swaps and CDS carry the same senior unsecured credit risk (same credit events and contingent payments), so pricing differences arise purely from supply and demand.
- Martin McDermott expects asset swap spreads generally below CDS because of liquidity: limited bond supply limits demand for asset swaps.
- Martin McDermott cited a JP Morgan note claiming CDS are pricier partly because convertible bond hedgers combine an equity call option with a CDS; a cheap call lets them pay more for CDS.
- Martin McDermott said he would try to synthesize a more complete note on asset swaps vs CDS the following week.
