From: Tanya Tamarchenko · Subject: Re: VAR for Niamh portfolio "Financial Products"

- Tanya Tamarchenko told Niamh Clarke (cc the owner) that VAR of Niamh's "Financial Products" portfolio (positions on 11/6/00) depends strongly on the term structure of correlations between BRENT and 61NY.
- Tanya Tamarchenko's VAR results for "Financial Products": $424,199 assuming correlation 1 everywhere; $1,868,949 using correlations from the first 18 contracts of Brent and 61NY; $1,106,773 using the first 12 contracts.
- Tanya Tamarchenko concluded that correlations depend on how many forward contracts are used, and that all liquidly traded contracts should be used since their prices carry statistically reliable information.
- Tanya Tamarchenko initially chose 18 contracts for the Brent/61NY correlation calculation and asked Niamh Clarke for her suggestion on the number to use.
