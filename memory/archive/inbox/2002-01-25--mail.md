(mail · received · m11343 · automatic capture)
From: "Robert E. Brooks" <rebrooks@earthlink.net>@ENRON · Subject: GPCM News: 1/25/02: Working with Power Model Forecasts: AGA Gas Storage

- Robert E. Brooks (GPCM Natural Gas Market Forecasting System, RBAC) reported in his 1/25/02 GPCM News that iterating between power models (Prosym, GE Maps, IREMM) and GPCM is impractical because the power models take too long to solve.
- Robert E. Brooks recommends running the power model three times instead of once, with high, medium and low Henry Hub forward price trajectories plus basis estimates. The resulting three gas-burn/price points per location form a demand curve for GPCM.
- Under Brooks' proposed method, GPCM solves for an interpolated expected price and gas-burn combination. A final power model run is then made using the GPCM prices, which should produce gas burns close to GPCM's values.
