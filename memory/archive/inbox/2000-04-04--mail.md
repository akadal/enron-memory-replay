(mail · received · m01235 · automatic capture)
From: Martina Angelova · Subject: UK RPI model

- Martina Angelova sent Zimin Lu (cc the owner, Anjam Ahmad, Trena McFarland) a UK RPI model for review, derived by bootstrapping RPI swaps; she described it as a very simple model.
- UK RPI swap structure in Martina Angelova's model: semi/semi Act/365F payments, YOYUKRPI = (UKRPI(p-2)/UKRPI(p-14) - 1)/2 with p = payment month; first payment is latest known RPI, February 2000, 2.32%.
- UK RPI model assumes constant cashflows between quoted years (not interpolated swaps), giving a rough raw curve that is smoothed with a macro written by Anjam Ahmad; mid point of swaps is used.
- UK RPI model discounts at LIBOR and solves for the coupon (RPI YOY rate) separately for each quoted period (e.g. 2 yrs, 5 yrs), determining RPI rates for the incremental portion; Martina says this lets Enron lock in and deliver forecast levels.
- Martina Angelova expects to see Zimin Lu in London.
