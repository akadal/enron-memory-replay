From: Zimin Lu · Subject: correlation matrix reduction

- Zimin Lu advised Zhiyong Wei (cc Stinson Gibner, Tanya Tamarchenko) to reduce the large correlation matrix between location indices using cluster analysis: pick major hubs and 'satellite' markets, then model hub-hub and satellite-hub correlations.
- Under Zimin Lu's proposed hub/satellite approach, traders would only need to input correlations between the hubs, which significantly reduces the size of the correlation matrix.
- Zimin Lu said the hub/satellite cluster technique for correlation matrix reduction is already used in Enron's Value at Risk system, and referred Zhiyong Wei to Tanya Tamarchenko for details.
- Zimin Lu sent Zhiyong Wei the wsprd code he had requested (April 2001).

From: Mike A Roberts · Subject: London Status Report - Research Weather Effort

- Mike A Roberts forwarded the owner Stephen Bennett's London Research Weather status report as background for a telephone conference with the owner the next day. Roberts called Steve 'really a home-run hitter'.

- Bennett said no second meteorologist will be in place to assist Tony by his planned departure on the 20th. He offered to stay in London until the 27th, or longer, to ensure the project succeeds. He named an upcoming desk move (hot desk system) as a key challenge.
