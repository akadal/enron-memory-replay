## 10:18

(mail · received · m00533 · automatic capture)
From: Office of the Chairman · Subject: Organization Announcement

- The Office of the Chairman announced that effective February 1, 2000, Enron is forming a new Global Strategic Sourcing unit to aggregate demand for goods and source them strategically on a global basis.
- Global Strategic Sourcing consolidates the existing strategic sourcing initiatives in the Gas Pipeline Group, Global Asset Operations and Enron Corp. Its goal is a future business-to-business e-Commerce venture.
- George Wasaff, Managing Director, leads the new Global Strategic Sourcing unit and reports directly to Mike McConnell, Chief Executive Officer for Global Technology (per Office of the Chairman, Feb 2000).
- George Wasaff has been with Enron fourteen years. His most recent role was Managing Director of Enron South America's Wholesale Operations. He was earlier Interim CEO of Elektro (July 1998 to June 1999) and CEO of TGS (June 1995 to February 1998).
- The Office of the Chairman cited the SAP implementation and internet/intranet technologies as the reasons Enron is moving from decentralized purchasing to aggregated global sourcing. It expects significant cash savings.
