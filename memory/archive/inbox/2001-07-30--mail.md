## 13:31

(mail · sent · m08798 · automatic capture)
From: Kaminski, Vince J </O=ENRON/OU=NA/CN=RECIPIENTS/CN=VKAMINS> · Subject: FW: From The Enron India Newsdesk - July 28 - 30 Newsclips

- Sandeep Kohli forwarded the owner India newsclips on July 30, 2001, noting it appears Enron has made public its desire to pull out of Dabhol; the owner forwarded it to his AOL account.
- Per the newsclips, Kenneth Lay told the Financial Times "We want out" of Dabhol; Enron said selling its DPC stake to the Indian government or lenders is the best approach, on terms giving complete recovery of capital costs.
- Per the newsclips, DPC is 65% owned by Enron, 10% each by GE and Bechtel, 15% by MSEB; phase I (740 MW) runs, phase II (1,444 MW) is 97% complete; MSEB stopped buying power in May and defaulted.
- Per the newsclips, the Supreme Court is hearing DPC's petition on August 6; DPC's 11 foreign lenders (incl. OPIC, ~$444 million in loans) say they lent only because the PPA provided for neutral London arbitration.
- Per the newsclips, the Godbole committee reports stalemate: states offered Rs 1.65-2.60 per unit for 800-1,000 MW of non-baseload DPC power, which DPC called totally unrealistic; next meeting expected in August.
