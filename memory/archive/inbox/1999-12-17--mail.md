From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: Credit Markets Group Organisational Announcement.

- John Sherriff announced in Enron Europe's general announcement (dated 07/12/99) the formation of the Credit Markets Group, responsible for developing products to manage Enron's internal credit portfolio risk and for offering third-party credit protection.
- The Credit Markets Group initially makes markets in bankruptcy protection on reference credits already in Enron's portfolio, and expects to add more credit products and reference credits quickly.
- The Credit Markets Group will use EnronOnline as its primary trade execution tool and plans an external credit-protection website with real-time prices, credit models and research papers. Public announcement expected late January.
- The Credit Markets Group is based in London with an initial local desk in Houston. Bryan Seyfried is transferring out of RAC (where he ran European Trading Controls and Research teams) to lead the group and reports to John Sherriff.
- Anjam Ahmad sent the announcement to Vince Kaminski, Stinson Gibner and Vasant Shanbhogue on 07/12/99. Kaminski forwarded it to his AOL address on 1999-12-17 with no comment of his own.
