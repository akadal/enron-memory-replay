From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: PSE Swap transaction

- Sidney Cox asked the owner (7 Dec 1999) to help with the PSE swap transaction, which was being worked again after the forex desk found a way to price CPI exposure in the market. Jarek Dybowski was copied.

- Jarek said PSE received two binding price quotes, from JP Morgan and Morgan Stanley. A PSE deal may close only in January 2000, and its NPV value to his team is shrinking to nearly zero, so every dollar of NPV matters.

From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: Credit Markets Group Organisational Announcement.

- John Sherriff (Enron Europe, 7 Dec 1999 announcement) announced the formation of the Credit Markets Group, which develops products to manage Enron's internal credit portfolio risk and offers third-party credit protection.
- The Credit Markets Group will initially make markets in bankruptcy protection on reference credits in Enron's current portfolio, with more credit products and reference credits expected soon.
- The Credit Markets Group plans to use EnronOnline as its primary trade execution tool and to launch an external credit protection website with real-time prices, filtering by reference credit, credit models and research papers. A public announcement is expected in late January.
- The Credit Markets Group will be based in London, with an initial local desk in Houston.
- Bryan Seyfried is transitioning out of RAC (where he ran the European Trading Controls and Research teams) to lead the Credit Markets Group, reporting to John Sherriff.
