## 02:37

(mail · received · m04030 · automatic capture)
From: Zimin Lu · Subject: Ethylene Margin Collar Model

- Zimin Lu told Douglas Friedman and Stinson Gibner (cc Bob Lee, Lee Jackson) that the Ethylene margin collars are priced using an Asian spread option, with forward and volatility curves provided by the desk.
- In Zimin Lu's Ethylene margin collar model, the correlation between Ethylene and Ethylene cost starts from the historical spot correlation of 50% and grows with maturity.
- Zimin Lu models the overall 40MM payout cap on the Ethylene margin collars separately: 10 years of historical spread data are fitted to a mean-reverting model with stronger mean reversion on the floor side.
- For the 40MM cap, Zimin Lu computes the expected payoff by simulating trajectories from the mean-reverting model, which he says are statistically similar to the historical data.
- Zimin Lu planned a meeting with Douglas Friedman and Stinson Gibner on the afternoon of 2000-11-06 to discuss the Ethylene margin collar model assumptions and results.
