- Enron Office of the Chairman (Ken) announced that on 2002-01-03 Enron settled a procedural dispute with Dynegy over Northern Natural Gas, allowing Dynegy to exercise its option to acquire the pipeline and dismissing Dynegy's Texas lawsuit.
