From: Zimin Lu · Subject: Volumetric Optionality Model Changes

- Natasha Danilochkina announced (forwarded to the owner by Zimin Lu) that, after early-June meetings with Zimin Lu (Houston Research) and Anjam Ahmad, the London gas desk and research will revise models for pricing volumetric (swing) options.
- The goal of the volumetric optionality model revision is one consistent method for all contracts: flat price gas swing options, indexed gas swing options, J-Block contract, modified Thermbank contract, and EnBank (virtual storage).
- A new feature in all revised volumetric optionality models will be real-time MTM, and prospectively sensitivities calculations.
- Roles on the volumetric optionality project: Natasha Danilochkina does problem definition, contracts/products summary and functional specification; Anjam Ahmad does modifications/implementation of Zimin's model and Houston/London interaction.
- The volumetric optionality model revision project starts June 16th, 2000; end date is TBC.
