## 07:49

(mail · received · m05249 · automatic capture)
From: Zimin Lu · Subject: Storage model change: Commodity Delta

- Zimin Lu changed the storage model output from unit value to total value: the total present value of the storage capacity at given initial conditions. Sent to Kenneth Parkhill, cc Stinson Gibner, 2001-01-10.
- Zimin Lu said the old unit value, (Total PV - S*capacity taken)/total capacity, could go negative when spot price S is very high; the correct S would be average injection gas price, but that is troublesome to compute.
- Zimin Lu said that with total PV as output, the storage model's delta can be read as the total commodity delta for the hedge.
- Zimin Lu reported that Duffie, in his audit review of the storage model, also suggested using total PV as the output.
- Zimin Lu attached the updated storage model as an XLL to his note to Kenneth Parkhill.
