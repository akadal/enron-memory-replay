## 08:03

(mail · received · m04994 · automatic capture)
From: Sandeep Kohli · Subject: LNG Hedging

- Sandeep Kohli told the owner that his team has been working with Krishna on LNG hedging; Anshuman prepared a draft note on it, which Sandeep sent to James A Hughes (Jim) on 12/21/2000.
- LNG hedging draft proposed two products: Volumetric Options (buy a call on 100% of volumes, sell a put on 50% to finance it) and a Ratio Derivative (buy two calls at a higher price, sell one at a lower price).
- Per Sandeep Kohli, traders reject the Ratio Derivative (bought calls just at the money, sold call in the money), so only the Volumetric Option is considered useful: high-price risk mitigation with some low-price participation.
- Volumetric Option test for March 2001: cost-neutral structure would buy a call at $26 and sell the put at $25.15.
- Sandeep Kohli urged close work with the Global Markets group on LNG hedging, since the team on the ground knows the contracts and what could be sold to MSEB; he asked Jim Hughes to discuss.
