From: Tom Halliburton · Subject: SDDP Model

- Tom Halliburton sent John (jobrien@snowyhydro.com.au, Snowy Hydro) the SDDP Methodology manual, cc the owner, and offered to discuss SDDP by phone in Houston (tel 001 713 345 8539).
- Tom Halliburton told John that SDDP was developed by Mario Pereira, proprietor of PSRI (Power Systems Research Inc), Rio de Janeiro (www.psr-inc.com), originally on a World Bank contract for Central America interconnections; PC-based, licensed widely.
- Tom Halliburton said that while at ECNZ in Wellington, New Zealand, his group purchased a copy of SDDP around 1994.
- Tom Halliburton described SDDP: weekly/monthly steps, 1-10 year horizon, 1-5 load blocks per period (non-chronological, cannot handle thermal ramp rates); stochastic dual DP with sampling yields reservoir water values; simulation phase gathers statistics.
- Tom Halliburton noted SDDP models most hydro and many transmission constraints, has a DC load flow option for bus nodal prices, and a Visual Basic interface reading/writing text files.
