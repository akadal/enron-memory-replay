(mail · received · m02470 · automatic capture)
From: Tanya Tamarchenko · Subject: Re: MG Metals: summary of VAR methodology and current status

- Tanya Tamarchenko told the owner that she and Anjam met Andreas Barkchis of MG Metals NY on Thursday 20th July in the NY office to gather information for MG Metals VAR; they expect an ongoing working relationship with him.
- Anjam, Kirstee (London) and Cantekin, Grant, the owner and Tanya Tamarchenko (Houston) have been building a spreadsheet VAR model for MG Metals; the spreadsheet is a prototype for the RiskTrac implementation.
- MG Metals VAR Version_1a covers 10 risk curves: forward prices for aluminium, copper, gold, lead, nickel, silver, tin, zinc and cocoa beans, plus the TC curve for copper concentrate; one price curve per metal for all product types.
- MG Metals VAR Version_1a uses a Risk Matrix approach: principal components per commodity along the term structure, cross-commodity correlations from 3 months of prompt-month prices, and delta-gamma for options.
- MG Metals VAR inputs: forward prices and positions from Mercur, implied volatilities from Reuters; positions roll up under AGG-METALS into four MG legal-entity sub-portfolios (e.g. MG Ltd., MG Metall Recycling GmbH) split by LME, Comex, physical, stock, options etc.
