(mail · received · m04001 · automatic capture)
From: V Charles Weldon · Subject: Hurricane Warning Derivatives

- V Charles Weldon (Charlie) updated the owner and others on progress toward a commercial hurricane warning derivative product or product line, to let entities hedge exposure to hurricane warning frequency and/or duration.
- Charlie Weldon's team built an Excel database of all U.S. hurricane warning data 1980-2000, sortable by year, storm, or coastline location; key data are total warning duration and number of discrete warnings.
- Weldon's hurricane warning data splits the U.S. coast into 11 regions: New England, Mid-Atlantic, Virginia, North Carolina, Georgia/South Carolina, East Florida, West Florida, Florida Panhandle, Orleans/Miss/Bama, Lousiana, Texas.
- Weldon said the 1980-2000 data may not yet suffice for price modeling; next steps are correcting for skew (political trends, satellite availability, population) and extending further back in time if accuracy holds.
- Weldon sees The Weather Channel as the most natural first counterparty for hurricane warning derivatives given Enron's positive existing relationship, and asked recipients for marketing ideas and comments.
