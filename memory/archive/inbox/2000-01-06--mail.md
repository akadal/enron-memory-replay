From: Clayton Vernon · Subject: Re: natural gas storage item

- Clayton Vernon told the owner (2000-01-06, from a class) that the gas industry is rapidly adopting Operational Balancing Agreements, under which pipelines contract with producers and LDCs to handle all shipping imbalances.
- Clayton said that under OBAs shippers book scheduled rather than actual flows, and storage in producing regions is operated to smooth weekday-weekend patterns and wellhead output to protect pipeline system integrity.
- Clayton said estimated marketed production is likely a better estimate of actual flow at a pipeline's receipt meter than production less local storage.
