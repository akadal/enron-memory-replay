- Melinda McCarty (x31641) told the owner that Harvard Business School is doing a 5-year business case study on Enron called Modern Giants, and is planning a research visit to Enron.

- Attachments to the Harvard visit memo suggest the faculty involved include Bower, Levesque, Roberto and Garvin.
