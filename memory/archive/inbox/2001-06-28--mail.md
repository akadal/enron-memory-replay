## 02:07

(mail · sent · m08641 · automatic capture)
From: Kaminski, Vince J </O=ENRON/OU=NA/CN=RECIPIENTS/CN=VKAMINS> · Subject: FW: Convolution

- Naveen Andrews sent a memo on June 27, 2001 to research/risk staff (Presley, Jia, Thomas, Nordstrom, Tamarchenko, Mao, Yu, Sokolov, Lew, De, Bharati), cc David Port and the owner, following up on questions from a talk that day on convolution.
- Naveen Andrews stated that a multivariate T random variable X = bZ (b = sqrt(v/y), y chi-square with v degrees of freedom) is uncorrelated if Z is uncorrelated, even though both components share the same scalar b; he attached a proof.
- Naveen Andrews said his group's P&L distribution approach is analogous to, but not identical to, the Glasserman et al. papers distributed to Research. It computes the full P&L pdf in one shot instead of computing the characteristic function of Pr(P/L > x) separately for each x.
- Naveen Andrews clarified that their convolution work does not compare full revaluation against delta-gamma approximation; he called that a completely different story.
- The owner forwarded Naveen Andrews' convolution memo to his personal AOL address (vkaminski@aol.com) on 2001-06-28.
