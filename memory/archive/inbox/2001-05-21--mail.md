- Steve Mitnick, CEO of Energy Leader Consulting (ELC) and former Co-Leader of PHB Hagler Bailly's energy consulting business, is seeking a one-hour meeting with the owner to present the EGE (Generation Evaluator) tool and an Allegheny Energy case study.
- Steve Mitnick asked Shirley Crenshaw that, if the meeting with the owner is set for Tuesday, May 29, it be scheduled for late afternoon.
- Steve Mitnick described EGE as a procedure (not software) that uses continuous emission monitoring hourly data to find utilities' inefficient unit commitment/dispatch, for use in wholesale deals, M&A efficiency estimates, or new plant valuation.
- Steve Mitnick proposed to Enron: Enron picks 1-3 target utilities, ELC runs EGE on them, ELC gives a one-day seminar teaching Enron staff the procedure, and optionally ELC supplies EGE data quarterly.
- Steve Mitnick said EGE data is updated through 4th quarter 2000, with 1st quarter 2001 data (through March 31, 2001) available approximately June 15, 2001.
