## 02:42

(mail · received · m00025 · automatic capture)
From: Clayton Vernon · Subject: gas prices

- Clayton Vernon told the owner that hoarding of pipeline capacity can be detected as an elevated basis relative to actual inflow, and that storage can be detected as the difference between inflow and consumption.
- Clayton Vernon proposed separating seller from buyer to detect market power, comparing the physical gas cost of running generators with the transmission cost (the basis).
- Clayton Vernon said a straightforward gas price model needs two time series: flow rates at interconnects (from telemetry) and spot-market prices. Flows could be imputed from prices if needed, giving a stochastic model of the basis driven by weather.
- Clayton Vernon proposed calibrating price elasticities of demand for consumption, storage and transmission, plus the extent of hoarding, from flows and prices. Storage and capacity-hoarding incentives are each like exotic call options at the consumption hub, for the basis trader.
- Clayton Vernon (2-3 am, 1999-12-15) wrote that he believes the problem is quite tractable and would like to proceed with a model. He wants the owner's agreement to start the gas price model.

## 10:05

(mail · received · m00026 · automatic capture)
From: Office of the Chairman · Subject: Performance Management Process

- Office of the Chairman announced (1999-12-15) a standardized officer titling and review system: four officer levels. Level 4 is the Enron Office of the Chairman, Level 3 the other Executive Committee members, Level 2 managing directors, Level 1 vice presidents.
- Performance Review Committee (PRC) reviews for Level 1 and Level 2 officers happen twice a year: in July for feedback, and at year-end for feedback, bonus and total compensation. The Executive Committee runs Level 2 reviews.
- Level 1 officers are reviewed first at the business-unit level, then cross-calibrated by the Executive Committee and about sixteen managing directors. The Executive Committee considers all promotions each January; exceptions will be infrequent.
- Individual performance evaluation is the starting point for base pay, bonus and long-term awards. A new long-term program replacing individual or business-unit plans was approved and will be communicated before bonus payments.
- Business-unit, global and corporate cross-functional PRC reviews for directors, senior directors and general managers started this year, with year-end as a benchmark for refining that level's process. HR Business Unit Leads answer questions (e.g. Mary Ann Long for GPG, David Oxley for ENA/EEL).
