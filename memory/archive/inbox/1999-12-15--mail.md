## 02:42

(mail · received · m00025 · automatic capture)
From: Clayton Vernon · Subject: gas prices

- Clayton Vernon proposed to the owner a gas price model: detect pipeline capacity hoarding as elevated basis against actual inflow, market power by separating seller from buyer, and storage as inflow minus consumption.
- Clayton Vernon said a straightforward gas price model needs two time series: flow rates at interconnects (from telemetry) and spot-market prices.
- Clayton Vernon proposed calibrating price elasticities of demand for consumption, storage, and transmission, plus the extent of hoarding, from flows and prices; he said each incentive for storage and hoarding acts as an exotic call option at the consumption hub.
- Clayton Vernon said flows can be imputed from prices, giving a purely stochastic model of the basis driven by weather. He told the owner he believes the problem is quite tractable.
- Clayton Vernon told the owner (2 Dec 1999 gas price message, sent 1999-12-15) that he wants to proceed with building the model. (owner, verbatim: "I believe the problem is quite tractable, and I would like to proceed with a model.")

## 10:05

(mail · received · m00026 · automatic capture)
From: Office of the Chairman · Subject: Performance Management Process

- Office of the Chairman announced (1999-12-15) a standardized officer titling and review process. Four officer levels: Level 4 is the Office of the Chairman, Level 3 other Executive Committee members, Level 2 managing directors (incl. company presidents, some SVPs), Level 1 VPs (and grandfathered SVPs).
- Starting this year, Level 1 and Level 2 officers are evaluated by a Performance Review Committee (PRC) twice a year: in July for feedback, and at year-end for feedback plus bonus and total compensation. The Executive Committee runs the PRC for Level 2.
- Level 1 officers are reviewed first at business-unit level, then results are cross-calibrated by the Executive Committee and about sixteen managing directors.
- The Executive Committee will consider all promotions in January each year; exceptions, internal or external, will be infrequent.
- Individual performance evaluation is the starting point for base pay, bonus and long-term awards. A new long-term program replacing individual or business-unit plans was approved and will be communicated before bonus payments. Cross-functional PRC reviews for directors, senior directors and general managers also began, with year-end as a benchmark.
