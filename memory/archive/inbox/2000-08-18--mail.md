## 02:52

(mail · received · m02903 · automatic capture)
From: Stinson Gibner · Subject: Re: Alex's paper

- The owner made minor changes to Alex Huang's paper (on a jump-diffusion option model vs Black-Scholes) and circulated it to Grant Masson, Stinson Gibner and Alex Huang on 08/18/2000. (owner, verbatim: "Minor changes I made to Alex's paper.")
- Stinson Gibner disagreed with the paper's claim that the results are counter-intuitive: the model naturally approaches Black-Scholes when a 'jump' is defined as a 1-sigma event, since jumps then become part of the main distribution.
- Stinson Gibner noted that the jump model's results are very sensitive to how a 'jump' is defined (2-sigma, 3-sigma events), which he sees as a practical difficulty in using the model.
- Stinson Gibner found the paper's hedging argument (buying a swap paying the difference between strike and Fs) nonsensical and said the last two sentences of the article need clearer explanation.
- Stinson Gibner suggested replacing 'annualized standard deviation' with 'annualized volatility' between eqn. 3 and eqn. 4 of Alex Huang's paper.
