## 01:58

(mail · received · m06322 · automatic capture)
From: Tanya Tamarchenko · Subject: Re: estimating tail of distribution and additional risk measures

- Tanya Tamarchenko told Naveen Andrews (cc David Port, the owner) that the analytical VAR approach (gamma-delta positions) is working for the Equity portfolio, enabling tail analysis and Expected Tail Loss calculation; same should be done for commodities.
- Tanya Tamarchenko reported that for the Equity portfolio, VAR at 5%, 1%, 0.5%, 0.1% from 100,000 simulations and from analytical VAR are very close, because the portfolio has few options.
- Tanya Tamarchenko tested scaling 5% VAR (from 1000 simulations) by normal-distribution factors (e.g. Norminv(0.001,0,1)/Norminv(0.05,0,1) for 0.1%) to estimate tail VAR; result was within about 5% of the correct number.
- Tanya Tamarchenko proposed using normal-scaled rough tail VAR estimates for the commodities portfolio until proper methods (analytical VAR) are implemented.

## 23:46

(mail · sent · m06348 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: California Energy Crisis

- The owner forwarded to his personal AOL address a 03/13/2001 all-employee memo from Ken Lay and Jeff Skilling setting out Enron's position on the California energy crisis.
- Lay and Skilling's memo says California's crisis was caused by regulation, not deregulation: no new generation was built, all buying was forced into the spot market, and retail rates stayed regulated.
- Lay and Skilling's memo says Enron is a market maker, not a generator, in California apart from a small amount of wind power, and that every megawatt it sold there was bought in the same market.
- Lay and Skilling's memo says EES chose to have California utilities supply power directly to its customers to cut market price risk, did not terminate any customer contracts, and denies this was a 'turnback'.
- Lay and Skilling's memo says California should streamline permitting, fully open its retail market, cut reliance on the spot market and let utilities recover costs; it opposes price caps and a state takeover.
