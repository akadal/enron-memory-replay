From: Kristin Walsh · Subject: California Power 1/19/00

- Kristin Walsh's California power update (1/19, to John Lavorato and Mike McConnell): SB-7X lets the Dept. of Water and Resources buy short-term power with no price cap through Feb. 2nd; AB-1X long-term contracts (5.5 cent cap) likely to be amended.

- Kristin Walsh reported the Bush administration opposes price caps but is working with Davis to split PG&E into separate gas and electric companies, the electric one going into Ch.11, to keep gas supply flowing.
- Kristin Walsh's assessment: absent an urgent bailout or long-term contract deal, utility bankruptcy remains the most likely scenario. Contacts: Robert Johnston (x39934), Kristin Walsh (x39510).
