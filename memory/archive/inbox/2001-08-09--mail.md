## 08:40

(mail · sent · m08896 · automatic capture)
From: Kaminski, Vince J </O=ENRON/OU=NA/CN=RECIPIENTS/CN=VKAMINS> · Subject: RE: Crude Oil for Oz

- The owner told Marc De la roche that Research already has a basket option model that can be adapted for the Oz crude oil basket transaction, and that he would talk with Sandeep and Vasant about it. (owner, verbatim: "We already have a basket option model that we can adapt for this transaction.")
- Randy O'Connor (Director, Origination, Enron Global Markets) asked Marc De la roche and Sanjay Patel to launch an effort with the research group (not fundamentals) to build an options valuation model for the Oz crude basket.
- Oz crude basket model assumptions per Randy O'Connor: 100 MBD on average, 25 MBD of each of 4 crude classes, with the option to substitute any grade within a class; he warned there may not be enough data to value them.
- Under the Oz MOU, Enron will supply a basket of crudes at fixed differentials to WTI, with 4 classes and markers: Light Sweet/Domestic Sweet, Heavy Sweet/Cabinda, Light Sour/Basrah Light (maybe Mesa 30), Heavy Sour/Maya. Other grades priced via relative refining value.
- Marc De la roche asked Sandeep Kohli and Vasant Shanbhogue to meet to kick off the Oz crude basket work, calling the transaction huge; Randy O'Connor wants FOB offers on an 8 year basis.

- John D. Martin (Carr P. Collins Chair in Finance, Baylor University) sent the owner a lead on Michael S. Gibson's paper "Incorporating Event Risk into Value-at-Risk" (FEDS Discussion Paper No. 2001-17, Federal Reserve Board, February 2001).
- Gibson's paper proposes a Jump-VaR model: a standard VaR model for ordinary price moves with a simple price-jump model grafted on, fattening tails and raising VaR; it flags correlated jumps and double-counting as practical issues.
- The owner thanked John D. Martin for the lead on event-risk VaR and said he has already seen other papers on this topic. (owner, verbatim: "Thanks for the lead. I have seen other papers on this topic.")
