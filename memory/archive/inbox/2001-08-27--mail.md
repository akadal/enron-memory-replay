## 09:27

(mail · sent · m09087 · automatic capture)
From: Kaminski, Vince J </O=ENRON/OU=NA/CN=RECIPIENTS/CN=VKAMINS> · Subject: FW: Meeting with Gordon on Raptor

- At Cassandra Schultz's request, Ding Yuan forwarded to the owner (Aug 24, 2001) his note on a meeting with Gordon about how the current Enron stock price affects the Raptor structure. The owner forwarded it to his vkaminski@aol.com address.
- Ding Yuan reported that after the March fix-up, Raptor hedged ENE credit capacity down to ENE $20/share using European collars transacted with ENE. Original collars for Raptor I, II and IV mature in 2003; new collars for Raptor II and IV mature in 2005.
- Ding Yuan reported that Raptor options settle by equity method: Enron hands over shares at maturity, capped at 3 shares per option, so below $20/share ENE delivers no more shares. Exercise causes share dilution but no impact on Enron income.
- Ding Yuan reported that Raptor liquidated a good portion of its public equities at a substantial discount. Swapped assets had $733.68 MM notional value at Raptor inception; assets remaining in the Raptors are currently worth $141.8 MM.
- Ding Yuan reported that Enron credit capacity in the Raptors fell from around ($36MM) after the March fix-up to around ($237MM) as of 8/20/01.
