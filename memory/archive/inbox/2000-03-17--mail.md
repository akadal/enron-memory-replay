## 08:06

(mail · sent · m01033 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: Half-Hourly Option Forward Volatilities

- Anjam Ahmad (London research) finished a study on half-hourly option forward volatilities, combining monthly, weekly and daily vols to price options on individual half hours; sent 03/17/2000 to London traders, cc the owner, Stinson Gibner, Grant Masson, Dale Surbey, Bjarne Schieldrop.
- Anjam Ahmad's method: a "reverse basvol" using half-hourly price return correlations gives a scaling factor of about 1.30 applied to EFA-slot monthly vols to convert them to half-hourly; the factors differ only slightly by EFA slot.
- In Anjam Ahmad's model, weekly and half-hourly vols come from historical analysis of several years of data, weighting recent standard deviations more; all half hours are assumed to sit 2 weeks and 3 days into the month for time-weighted aggregation.
- Anjam Ahmad put the spreadsheet HalfHourlyVolModel.xls in S:\RESEARCH\ANJAM\HHOPTIONVOLMODEL\ and proposed using its forward vol matrix now as semi-final numbers, with possible fine-tuning of the time-weightings.
- The owner forwarded Anjam Ahmad's half-hourly option volatility memo to his personal AOL address on 03/17/2000.
