From: Michael SCHILMOELLER · Subject: Henwood Stuff and Thoughts about Valuation

- Michael Schilmoeller told the owner and Stinson Gibner that utilities quantifying decisions usually use classical decision analysis (von Neumann utility curves): the President's curve for unregulated/shareholder decisions, the customers' or PUC's curve for decisions covered in rates.
- Michael Schilmoeller suggested valuing commodities where Black/Scholes assumptions fail by starting with CAPM for large industries in an area, using pro formas to translate to utility curves for input commodities, and adding the utility curves.
- Michael Schilmoeller argued Enron is lowest bidder because of its trader role: per Keynes, traders must be compensated for holding risk while hedgers pay a premium for certainty, so Enron traders have bigger bid/ask spreads and lower bid prices.
- Michael Schilmoeller forwarded to the owner and Stinson Gibner a Henwood (Heather Mason) notice about the Henwood ERCOT Symposium, following a conversation with them about Henwood and valuation.
