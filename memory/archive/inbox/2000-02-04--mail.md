From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: El Paso / ENA Deal Completely Terminated Now

- Kimberly Watson forwarded to the owner (who forwarded it to his AOL address on 02/04/2000) an ET&S Business Intelligence summary: El Paso Natural Gas and Enron North America mutually agreed to terminate the $38 million deal for 1.2 Bcf/d of El Paso capacity.
- The terminated El Paso/ENA deal, entered in December, covered three capacity agreements (Blocks I, II, III); Enron had asked in a Jan. 27 letter to exit only Block II (614 MMcf/d), but El Paso released it from all three, effective Jan. 31.
- Reason for exit: a mid-January FERC order limited Block II capacity primary delivery to PG&E-Topock, stripping primary access to SoCal Gas-Topock, which Enron had contracted for as a condition of the deal.
- El Paso said it would hold an open season for the released capacity from Feb. 7 to Feb. 14, and has sought FERC rehearing of the mid-January Block II order; unclear whether it will continue pursuing rehearing.
