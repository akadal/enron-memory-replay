## 08:35

(mail · received · m04178 · automatic capture)
From: Naveen Andrews · Subject: Re: looking for "Fat Tails" in time-series for NGI-SOCAL

- Naveen Andrews told Tanya Tamarchenko (cc the owner and Vladimir Gorny) that for the NGI-SOCAL fat-tails question the concern is portfolio value changes, not log-returns of a single contract.
- Naveen Andrews said a single NGI-SOCAL contract's log-returns have extremes and can be fit to a fat-tailed distribution.
- Naveen Andrews said a 1.20 basis move on a 500 BCF position is an extreme event any way you slice it.
- Naveen Andrews agreed that in the literature, single-contract log-returns are not divided by vols, which challenges Tanya Tamarchenko's normalization.
- Tanya Tamarchenko normalized NGI-SOCAL log-returns as [return(t)-ave(t)]/vol(t) and reported to Naveen Andrews that she could not find fat tails.
