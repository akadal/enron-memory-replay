## 13:02

(mail · received · m07150 · automatic capture)
From: Chonawee Supatgiat · Subject: calculating bid-ask prices

- Chonawee Supatgiat told the owner and Stinson Gibner about Enron's movie trading business, where Enron is market maker for futures on a movie's gross box office receipts; Rich (Richard DiMichele) circulated the idea and asked for feedback.
- Chonawee Supatgiat proposed a "Dynamic bid-ask price process": tentative bid-ask prices posted during a weekly trading period, customers submit/revise bids, final prices set at close, so Enron earns the spread without taking positions.
- Chonawee Supatgiat suggested the bidding period close when no new bid arrives within a set time, and that the method suits illiquid products traded via EnronOnline.com and other parts of Enron.
- Chonawee Supatgiat advised letting movie futures prices float with the market rather than forecasting them, since studios and exhibitors have inside information and can game prices (a point raised by Russ and Martin).
- Chonawee Supatgiat recommended weekly mark-to-market charging with margin calls to reduce credit risk in the movie futures business, and suggested offering options as well; Key Kasravi and Martin Lin are also involved.
