## 05:10

(mail · received · m00730 · automatic capture)
From: Pinnamaneni Krishnarao · Subject: News article on Enron

- Pinnamaneni Krishnarao sent the owner a Business Line article (02/21/2000) by Arun Ghosh reviewing Abhay Mehta's book 'Power Play: A Study of the Enron Project', which is highly critical of Enron's Dabhol power project.
- Per the article, MSEB contracted to buy, or pay for if unused, 2000 MW from Dabhol Power Company for 20 years. Abhay Mehta put total payments to Enron at $35 billion (around Rs. 1,25,000 crores at 1998 exchange rates).
- The article estimates the Dabhol tariff to MSEB in late 1999 at 11.25 US cents/unit: 4.4 capacity charge plus 6.85 energy cost. That implies about $1.97 billion a year, or $39.4 billion over 20 years.
- Per the article, MSEB stopped buying 200-250 MW from Tata Electric (Rs. 1.80/unit) and backed down Chandrapur (Rs. 1.20/unit) to take Enron power at Rs. 5/unit. The estimated loss is Rs. 460 crore per year.
- The article says Pakistan cancelled a similar Enron project in 1996: a $670 million, 782 MW residual-oil plant with a fixed 6.4 US cents/kWh rate over 30 years. It adds that the World Bank opposed Dabhol.
