(mail · received · m05816 · automatic capture)
From: Sandeep Kohli · Subject: Re: Good news - Cabinet approval for Power Trading

- K Seethayya reported (02/06/2001) that the Indian Union Cabinet approved the Ministry of Industry proposal "to allow foreign equity participation through automatic route upto 100% for trading in power sector, subject to prevailing laws."
- Seethayya noted the Cabinet approval came without the Draft Electricity Bill, 2001 being finalised; the proposal had been stalled at FIPB pending the Bill, then went to a Group of Ministers before Cabinet cleared it.
- Seethayya said FIPB will next either issue an approval to Enron LLC or advise it to use the automatic route for power trading in India.
- Seethayya suggested the Cabinet may have hurried the approval in view of DPC invoking the State Guarantee; Secretary (Power) conveyed the news in a meeting with Wade (Cline).
- Sandeep Kohli credited Ramu, Mr. Mehta, Jane, Heidi and Seethayya for working on the drafts of the note to Chidambaram that supported the power trading approval.
