## 06:40

(mail · sent · m01368 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: Re: Enron in Europe: Emerging Europe: Enron Plugs On in Poland --- Power Market's Vast ...

- Jarek Astramowicz, president of Enron Poland, forwarded to the owner a Wall Street Journal Europe article (04/13/2000) on Enron in Poland; the owner replied in Polish that it was very interesting. (owner, verbatim: "Bardzo ciekawe.")
- Per the WSJ Europe article, Enron spent $132 million building Nowa Sarzyna, a 116-megawatt gas-fired heat-and-power plant in Poland. It sells power to the Polish Power Grid (PSE) under a 20-year PPA won in 1997; commercial production was due to begin in April 2000.
- Per the article, Enron entered Poland in 1994 and had yet to earn a profit there. Net income from Nowa Sarzyna is at least five years away, and Enron hopes to post Polish power-trading profits by 2001.
- Per the article, a wholesale power bourse modeled on Nord Pool was due to open in Warsaw in July 2000. Jarek Astramowicz called its success 'one big question mark' for Enron Poland's trading profits.
- Per the article, Nowa Sarzyna was Jarek Astramowicz's own project; he sold his interest to Enron in 1998. Philip Davies is regulatory and government-affairs manager at Enron Europe in London.
