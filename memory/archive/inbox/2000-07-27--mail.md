## 08:08

(mail · received · m02540 · automatic capture)
From: Zimin Lu · Subject: Re: Aluminium Asian Digital Options

- Russell Placket of MG Metals asked Anjam Ahmad (Enron Europe) to price a strip of twelve monthly Asian digital options on LME aluminium starting Jan-01. Each pays a fixed cash amount if the monthly average close exceeds the strike; holidays are excluded from the average.
- Russell Placket suggested using the LME Jan-01 price of $1572.5 as a proxy for the monthly average. Anjam Ahmad asked Zimin Lu whether a model existed or whether to use Monte Carlo with a European digital as control variate.
- Zimin Lu advised Anjam Ahmad to use moment matching to get an effective volatility and dividend yield for the average, then apply the European digital option formula. He also suggested a Monte Carlo check of the approximation's accuracy.
- Zimin Lu sent Anjam Ahmad his C code for Asian spread options. It finds the effective vol and drift for both averages, then calls the European spread option formula, and he said the same approach applies to Asian digitals.
- Anjam Ahmad (Enron Europe, x35383) had recently visited Houston research. Zimin Lu told him the team was impressed by his work (July 2000).
