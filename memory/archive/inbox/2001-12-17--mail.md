From: Enron General Announcements </O=ENRON/OU=NA/CN=RECIPIENTS/CN=MBX_ANNCENRON> · Subject: FROM RAYMOND BOWEN, JR., EVP, FINANCE & TREASURER

- Raymond Bowen, Jr. (EVP, Finance & Treasurer) announced to all Enron employees that reimbursement of business expenses incurred after the Chapter 11 filing (after December 2nd) has returned to normal and will be paid promptly.
- Raymond Bowen, Jr. announced that Enron will reimburse pre-petition employee expenses (incurred before the December 2nd Chapter 11 filing) up to $5,000 immediately. Amounts above that, up to $15,000, will follow after a manual review.
- Per Raymond Bowen, Jr., the bankruptcy court's first day orders cap unpaid pre-petition compensation, benefits and expenses at $15,000 per employee. Enron has sought court clarification that this cap excludes medical, dental and vision expenses.
- Employees with pre-petition business expenses over $5,000 were asked to email a brief summary, including pending pre-petition medical/dental/vision claims, to expensereport@enron.com.
- Raymond Bowen, Jr. said new travel and entertainment guidelines for business travel and employee expenses are forthcoming. Until then, travel should proceed as approved by direct supervisors, with discretion given Enron's financial situation.
