## 01:03

(mail · sent · m01890 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: US Gas Markets Reach New Heights: Where Will It Stop? - CERA Alert

- On 06/02/2000 the owner forwarded to Mike Roberts, as FYI, a CERA Alert of June 1, 2000 titled "US Gas Markets Reach New Heights: Where Will It Stop?" from CERA's North American Gas Team.
- CERA's June 1, 2000 alert reported Henry Hub gas at $4.30-$4.40/MMBtu, the highest level since deregulation, up from under $3.00 in April. It attributed the rise to weak production and surging demand from power generation.
- CERA raised its Henry Hub price outlook to $4.18/MMBtu for the third quarter of 2000 and to $3.67/MMBtu for the 2000 annual average (June 1, 2000 alert).
- CERA puts the minimum acceptable working gas in storage at 2.65-2.7 Tcf by October 31. It said June-October injections must average 8.1 Bcf/d to approach 2.7 Tcf, and spring injections were running 1.5-2.0 Bcf/d below the prior year.
- CERA sees a tenuous gas price resistance point at $4.25-$4.50/MMBtu. Warmer-than-normal weather could push prices to $5.00-$5.25. Mild weather plus more OPEC output would only bring prices back to $3.50-$4.00.
