## 07:13

(mail · received · m00581 · automatic capture)
From: Steven Leppard · Subject: Gaming of NETA Prices - Constraints on The McCoy Strategy

- Steven Leppard told the owner he is pressing Eklavya Sareen to produce a white paper analysing possible trading strategies under NETA and their implications for prices and volatilities; he will forward it when received.
- Eklavya Sareen (London) described the 'McCoy strategy' for gaming NETA imbalance prices: take a large forward position, drive the system long/short physically, then submit extreme bids/offers to the balancing market to push SSP high or SBP low.
- Eklavya Sareen noted that under NETA's proposed constraint-tagging rule (DISG 24, Option 3(a), 21 Dec 99), arbitrageable accepted bids/offers are eliminated from imbalance price calculation, making the McCoy strategy harder to pursue.
