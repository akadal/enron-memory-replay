## 03:22

(mail · sent · m05842 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: New Merchant Asset VaR Model

- Eugenio Perez told the owner, Stinson Gibner and Rudi Zipter (02/08/2001) that the Merchant Asset portfolio has a vertical bear spread hedging its Hanover Compression holdings, which are about 10% of the portfolio.
- Because of the Hanover Compression hedge, Eugenio Perez switched the Merchant Asset VaR model from parametric VaR to Monte Carlo simulation, which he judged more accurate.
- After talking with the owner, Eugenio Perez moved the Merchant Asset VaR parameter estimation to the RiskMetrics standard: zero mean, daily volatilities and correlations by exponential weighting with lambda 0.94.
- Eugenio Perez's Merchant Asset VaR model uses Cholesky decomposition for correlated normals and simulates GBM prices. It runs in Excel/VBA without Crystal Ball.
- In the model, Hanover stock plus the bear spread is treated as a synthetic short put at 34 and long call at 92, fully revalued with Black-Scholes; vol and rates held constant. The owner forwarded the memo to Tanya Tamarchenko.
