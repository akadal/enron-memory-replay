## 10:05

(mail · sent · m02579 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: VaR numbers for the 26th

- Kirstee Hewitt (Enron Europe, London) ran the VaR model for 26th July for Andreas Barschkis (mgusa.com): total VaR $4,170,653, Cu (copper) position $1,852,876. Bjorn Hagelmann and Grant Masson were copied.
- Kirstee Hewitt explained that Andreas Barschkis's Capital at Risk method uses a 5-day adjustment period, while Enron's VaR model uses a one-day holding period, so his risk factor shrinks by sqrt(5) (2.24): 3.99% becomes 1.78% daily (std approx 1.1%/day).
- Kirstee Hewitt considers the ~1.1% daily vol estimate reasonable, since the annualized Cu spot vol Enron shows is approx 19%, about 1.2% daily.
- Per Kirstee Hewitt, using the daily risk factor the Cu positions for the 19th give VaR of approx $2m, below Enron's estimate of $3,100,568; she noted that netting positions collapses term structure into a one-factor model, making numbers hard to compare.
- Kirstee Hewitt apologised to the owner for not sending him the 26 July VaR mail earlier; the owner forwarded it to his personal AOL address on 07/28/2000.
