## 02:41

(mail · received · m05163 · automatic capture)
From: Alex Huang · Subject: Re: The consultant's model for Gary Hickerson's group

- Alex Huang told the owner that the consultant's model for Gary Hickerson's group is a real option model valuing Genco power plants, using GARCH-simulated one-year daily prices, unit dispatch logic, and the average of 100 simulations; it takes about 16 hours to run for about 200 units.
- Alex Huang judged the consultant's methodology OK if GARCH is modelled correctly, but said it misses most optionality, 100 simulations are far too few, and it is unclear how traders will use the output or why the horizon is one year.
- Alex Huang has his own GBM simulation model (start-up cost, fixed O&M, ramp-up delay) that runs 10,000 simulations for one unit over ten years in 2 min 40 s; he estimates a full rebuild would run a couple of hours for 200 units and needs help mainly with database interfacing.
- Alex Huang asked the owner, Stinson Gibner and Vasant Shanbhogue whether Research should only oversee the consultant (RDI) or redo the model, noting the business-unit separation, and said he never promised to oversee a programmer, contrary to Jeff M Gray's memo.
- Jeff M Gray's project timeline for the Genco model: kickoff 1/4, Enron Research to complete coding of Pricemaker by 1/17 and Dispatch by 1/22, complete code testing by 2/9; training, testing and final QC to finish 2/16.
