(mail · received · m03842 · automatic capture)
From: Leann Walton · Subject: Magic 15,000 level on Nikkei

- Leann Walton forwarded to the owner and Gary Hickerson (cc mraymon) Darren Delage's 10/16/2000 question to Maureen Raymond about a possible Japan central bank intervention at the Nikkei 15,000 level.
- Darren Delage's understanding: around Nikkei 15,000, Japanese banks fall below BIS reserve criteria and cannot extend new loans with BIS approval, giving the central bank an incentive to intervene.
- Darren Delage cited a Goldman Sachs article putting Japanese banks' break-even on their stock holdings at roughly Nikkei 12,000-12,500, a different level than the one Maureen Raymond had mentioned.
- Darren Delage sees two Nikkei trigger levels to watch, 15,000 (BIS criteria failure) and 12,500 (banks take actual losses on equity holdings), and asked Maureen Raymond to confirm this.
- Darren Delage thanked Maureen Raymond for her research coverage, saying he values what it adds; his team is trying to understand how such a trigger would affect Japanese capital markets.
