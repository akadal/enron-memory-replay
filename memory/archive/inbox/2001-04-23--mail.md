From: Sandeep Kohli · Subject: From The Enron India Newsdesk - April 23rd newsclips

- Sandeep Kohli told the owner that, judging from the April 23rd India newsclips, Enron (ENE) appears ready to exit India or alternatively to get into an arbitration battle over Dabhol.

- Per Financial Express (forwarded newsclip), the DPC board meeting April 25 in London planned to authorise Enron India MD K Wade Cline and DPC president/CEO Neil McGregor to issue PPA termination and transfer notices to MSEB and governments.
- Per Business Standard (forwarded newsclip), DPC sought approval from its 25 lenders in London to pull out of the $3 billion Dabhol project; a preliminary termination notice starts a six-month cooling-off period.
- Per forwarded newsclips, the Godbole Energy Review Committee found MSEB overpays Enron/DPC about Rs 930 crore a year, recommended a judicial probe, and Maharashtra plans an expert panel to renegotiate DPC tariffs.
- Sandeep Kohli circulated the Enron India Newsdesk newsclips for April 23rd, 2001, covering Dabhol Power Company (DPC) and MSEB.
- A press timeline in Sandeep Kohli's April 23rd newsclips says MSEB defaulted on payment in Oct 2000 and then stopped paying monthly bills. It says Enron invoked the Central government's counter-guarantee on Feb 6, 2001.
- The same press timeline says Dabhol Phase I was commissioned May 13, 1999, and financial closure for Phase II came in Jul 1999.
- A press piece in the April 23rd clips says a committee recommended publishing all Dabhol documents and restructuring DPC financially to lower power costs. It also recommended allowing DPC power sales outside Maharashtra, re-examining PPAs, and reforming MSEB.
- Business Standard (April 23, 2001) reported record MSEB revenue collections of Rs 968 crore in March 2001, driven by disconnecting defaulters. It said the DPC monthly payment issue is now before arbitrators at the negotiating table.
