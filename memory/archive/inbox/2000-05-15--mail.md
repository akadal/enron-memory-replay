From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: Re: LNG May 19 decision

- John Sherriff (temporarily overseeing Eastern Hemisphere trading plus LNG after Greg moved to a new role) asked the owner for status on LNG curves/ship values ahead of an LNG ship decision on May 19.
- The owner is working with Doug Rotenberbg, Brad Hitch, Scott Earnest (Sally Beck's organization) and RAC to set up the book for the Elba Island LNG transaction, later to be expanded to capture all Enron LNG positions.
- Elba Island deal covers 17 years with a 5-year extension option; plan is to source marginal fob cargos. The owner flagged significant logistical/operational risk and no ability to lock in US gas prices for that tenor. (owner, verbatim: "we have a significant logistical and operational risk in this transaction.")
- The owner views Elba Island as essentially a tolling deal on the LNG-nat gas spread, advises against locking in only one leg, suggests covering ~50% of LNG volumes for the first 5 years with the US gas side locked, and a big prudence reserve. (owner, verbatim: "I would not recommend locking-in only one leg of the spread.")
- The owner's group will value Enron's options in the Elba Island deal and work on the LNG hedging strategy; he noted long-term LNG contracts are based on the Japanese Crude Cocktail, which correlates well with Brent. (owner, verbatim: "My group will work on valuation of some options we have in the Elba Island")
