## 06:01

(mail · sent · m09065 · automatic capture)
From: Kaminski, Vince J </O=ENRON/OU=NA/CN=RECIPIENTS/CN=VKAMINS> · Subject: Weather group seats on the trading floors

- The owner told John Lavorato (cc Greg Whalley) he understands the new plan is to seat the gas and power weather groups at one location on the trading floors, and he recommends against it. (owner, verbatim: "My recommendation is against it.")
- The owner's view: two separate weather groups exist to provide checks and balances and independent views; co-locating them would nullify that, as the power weather guys would defer to Mike. (owner, verbatim: "The power weather guys will defer to Mike who has unquestionable authority and experience.")
- The owner said that if the new seating arrangement goes ahead, it makes sense to combine the gas and power weather groups under Mike's leadership. (owner, verbatim: "it makes sense to combine the two groups under Mike's leadership.")
- The owner asked John Lavorato to let him know what he thinks about the weather group seating and possible merger. (owner, verbatim: "Please, let me know what you think.")
