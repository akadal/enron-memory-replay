## 10:55

(mail · sent · m09794 · automatic capture)
From: Kaminski, Vince J </O=ENRON/OU=NA/CN=RECIPIENTS/CN=VKAMINS> · Subject: FW: ERCOT TCR votes

- Martin Lin told Vasant Shanbhogue (cc the owner) that he and Lance attended the ERCOT Wholesale Market Subcommittee (WMS) meeting on the proposed TCR design (Oct 30, 2001); most votes went Enron's way, and the one lost doesn't matter much to Enron.
- Martin Lin reported that Enron opposed TCR derating; WMS chose to issue 100% of the commercially significant constraint (CSC) limit as non-deratable TCRs, 60% in the annual auction and the rest auctioned monthly.
- Martin Lin reported WMS allowed closely related elements (CREs) set in advance of the year like CSCs, and barred certain units between a CSC and a CRE from the balancing energy market; Enron was satisfied.
- Martin Lin reported Enron lost the WMS vote to let a single entity acquire all TCRs in a primary auction; Enron favored it, arguing TCRs are worth no more than realized congestion, and he said the limit has little market effect.
