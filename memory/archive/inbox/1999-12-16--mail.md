## 05:59

(mail · received · m00042 · automatic capture)
From: Clayton Vernon · Subject: (no subject)

- Clayton Vernon sent Vince Kaminski and Vasant Shanbhogue a summary of his meeting with Chris Germany, Capacity Trader at the East Desk, on gas transmission pricing.
- Per Germany, pipeline capacity is typically leased monthly. Example South Texas to Brooklyn: about $12.00 per month per 10,000 decatherms (about $0.40/day), plus variable charges of 6% fuel (shrinkage) and 6.5% overhead.
- Trader spread example: South Texas delivered at NYMEX minus $0.10 basis, Brooklyn at NYMEX plus $0.25, spread $0.35. With variable transmission cost $0.125, a trader could offer up to $0.225 for firm capacity for tomorrow.
- Germany said leaseholders know the firm equivalent of interruptible capacity well, and many pipelines don't discount firm tariff maximums. Several physical routes exist (Enron, Texas Eastern, etc.); traders cover pipeline positions at each end to free capacity.
