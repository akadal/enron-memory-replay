(mail · received · m06734 · automatic capture)
From: Stinson Gibner · Subject: P+ option valuation model

- Stinson Gibner (V.P. Research) reviewed the booking of the P+ options and told Mark Fondren (cc Scott Earnest, the owner) that they are valued with a standard two-commodity spread option model assuming correlated Geometric Brownian Motion, i.e. Black-76 extended to two commodities.
- P+ call option payoff per Stinson Gibner: Max(0, A - B - K), where A = NXWTI (Nymex delivery price), B = Posting Price = WTI Swap - Posting Basis, and K = fixed Posting Bonus.
- Stinson Gibner noted leg B of the P+ spread is a basket of the two futures prices making up the WTI swap plus the average posting basis; he handles it by setting leg B's volatility and correlation with NXWTI to reflect the basket.
- Stinson Gibner said this two-leg approach is more straightforward than a three- or four-commodity model with its volatility and correlation matrices, and concluded the model is appropriate for P+ options if inputs are set correctly.
