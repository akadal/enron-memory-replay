## 05:18

(mail · received · m00820 · automatic capture)
From: Ravi Thuraisingham · Subject: Material for Thu 2 mar WG meeting

- Ravi Thuraisingham (Enron Communications) forwarded to the owner and Stinson Gibner, FYI, material for the CAIDA Internet metrics working group meeting in San Jose on Thursday 2 Mar. Ravi is on the metrics@caida.org mailing list.
- Nevil Brownlee, Visiting Researcher at CAIDA in San Diego, is organizing the CAIDA metrics WG. He proposed that each group give a short presentation (10 minutes, at most 5 slides) of its Internet measurement work at the 2 Mar meeting.
- Daniel McRobb proposed passive measurement of TCP and DNS response times. For example, client-to-server round-trip time can be read from the TCP three-way handshake while seeing traffic in one direction only.
- Paul Krumviede noted that it is hard to agree on how to measure availability, reliability, throughput and latency for SLAs. One compromise defined throughput at access link loads of no more than 50% of the bit rate.
- Topics raised for the CAIDA metrics WG included QoS/DiffServ and IPSEC metrics (Nevil Brownlee), bridging RTFM and IPPM work and IP telephony metrics (Carter Bullard), and DoS traceback (Curtis Villamizar).
