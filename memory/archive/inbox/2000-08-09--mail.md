## 08:20

(mail · received · m02770 · automatic capture)
From: Mike A Roberts · Subject: pdo index update

- Mike A Roberts forwarded to Joseph Hrgovcic and the owner the Pacific Decadal Oscillation (PDO) index update from Nathan Mantua (University of Washington), updated through July 2000.
- Nathan Mantua said his next PDO index update will be in November; the full series is at ftp://ftp.atmos.washington.edu/mantua/pnw_impacts/INDICES/PDO.latest.
- PDO index 2000 values per Mantua: Jan -1.99, Feb -0.82, Mar 0.29, Apr 0.35, May -0.05, Jun -0.43, Jul -0.66. Jan and Feb were revised because of changes in the Reynolds OI SST data.
- The PDO index is the leading PC of monthly North Pacific SST anomalies poleward of 20N, with global mean SST anomalies removed. It uses UKMO historical SST for 1900-81 and Reynolds OI SST from 1982 on (Zhang, Wallace, Battisti 1997; Mantua et al. 1997).

From: Mike A Roberts · Subject: THE ANSWER!!!

- Mike A Roberts forwarded to the owner, Gary Taylor, Jose Marquez, Joseph Hrgovcic and Mark Tawney a reply from Nathan Mantua (University of Washington atmospheric sciences) on whether the PDO is moving into a cold phase.
- Nathan Mantua told Mike Roberts there is no credible way to determine what will happen with the PDO; ENSO forecasts, which show some demonstrated skill, give more insight into the next few months and seasons.
- Nathan Mantua said in August 2000 that observations showed a trend to near-normal ENSO with slightly warmer-than-normal tropical ocean temperatures, implying a slight El Nino-like bias over North America that works against continued cold-phase PDO.
