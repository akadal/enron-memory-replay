## 01:23

(mail · received · m03466 · automatic capture)
From: Tanya Tamarchenko · Subject: Re: FYI: UK Var issues

- Tanya Tamarchenko told the owner that UK VaR breached the limit the week before 2000-10-09; UK traders asked the research group to review correlations across UK gas and power and across EFA slots; next step is updating correlations from historical prices.
- Oliver Gaylard reported UK VaR by gas/power correlation: at 0.0 Power o10.405MM, Gas o3.180MM; at 0.3 Power o10.030MM, Gas o3.245MM; from 0.4 up the Cholesky decomposition failed (matrix not positive definite).
- Tanya Tamarchenko explained that gas/power correlation should only affect aggregate VaR, not separate book VaRs; observed differences come from Monte-Carlo simulation noise. Matrix 1 (trial and error) and Matrix 2 (Naveen Andrews, Finger's algorithm) were tested; Matrix 2 gives higher VaR.
- Richard Lewis (London) proposed allocating to UK gas and power books a share of the portfolio VaR reduction from gas/power correlation; at 30% correlation combined VaR falls ~o1mm, and at 55% (Matrix 1) ~o1.8mm. UK power is still over its limit, which was set assuming no correlation.
- Richard Lewis agreed Tanya Tamarchenko should rerun historical EFA-to-EFA correlations from forward curves (first 2 years suggested); final review of the UK long-term curve waits until he and Simon Hastings return (Monday week after 10/08/2000).
