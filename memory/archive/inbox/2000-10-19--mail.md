(mail · received · m03730 · automatic capture)
From: Vasant Shanbhogue · Subject: Re: Option Pricing Challenge

- On 10/17/2000 Zimin Lu emailed the research group (the owner, Stinson Gibner, Vasant Shanbhogue, Pinnamaneni Krishnarao, Alex Huang, Kevin Kindall, Tanya Tamarchenko) an 'Option Pricing Challenge' he had been puzzling over since 1995.
- Zimin Lu's question: for a traded underlying following a mean-reverting process, delta hedging still gives the Black-Scholes PDE and formula. He asked how that fits with the different distribution, and whether each SDE's distribution can be derived from its option PDE.
- Vasant Shanbhogue replied that for any process dS = Mu(S,t)*S*dt + Sigma(S,t)*S*dz, delta hedging yields the Black-Scholes PDE. In the risk-neutral world the actual drift is irrelevant, so this covers GBM and mean reversion.
- Vasant Shanbhogue's view: a mean-reverting process gives a different option price only if the asset is not hedgeable. For a non-traded factor, the PDE must include the market price of risk, and the actual drift then enters the PDE.
