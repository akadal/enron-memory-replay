(mail · received · m00384 · automatic capture)
From: Clayton Vernon · Subject: a basic idea of price-offer matching clauses

- Clayton Vernon sent the owner the idea he had alluded to earlier: price-offer matching clauses (e.g. dealers promising to 'match any advertised price') remove the incentive to cut prices, since rivals match instantly and market share stays unchanged while revenues fall.
- Clayton Vernon argued to the owner that a 'right of first refusal' clause in a limited partnership seriously devalues a partner's shares: outsiders won't pay for due diligence because good offers get matched and only overpriced offers go through.
- Clayton Vernon's view: right-of-first-refusal clauses make partnership shares essentially illiquid and block economically efficient rebalancing of portfolios.
