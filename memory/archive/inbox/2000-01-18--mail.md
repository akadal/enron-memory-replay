(mail · received · m00384 · automatic capture)
From: Clayton Vernon · Subject: a basic idea of price-offer matching clauses

- Clayton Vernon sent Vince an explanation of the basic idea behind price-match clauses: 'match any advertised price' promises lead dealers to avoid price cuts, since rivals match and no one gains share.
- Clayton argued that right-of-first-refusal clauses in partnerships deter outside bids, because rivals can match good offers and bidders overpaying would lose. This devalues partners' shares and makes them effectively illiquid.
