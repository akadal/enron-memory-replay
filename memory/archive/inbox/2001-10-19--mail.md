- Frank Wolak told the owner he firmly believes in locational pricing and pricing congestion, but that PJM's implementation suppresses price volatility and reduces market transparency.

- Frank Wolak reminded the owner of their earlier dinner discussion: he sees demand-side participation and more efficient risk management as the biggest benefits of restructuring, both requiring prices that reflect actual market conditions.
