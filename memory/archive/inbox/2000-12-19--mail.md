From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: Updated WTI Trading Simulation

- Stinson Gibner sent John J Lavorato an updated WTI trading simulation model (12/19/2000), cc Zimin Lu, Vince Kaminski and Hector Campos; the owner forwarded it to his AOL address.
- Stinson Gibner said the updated WTI simulation fixed handling of contract rolls and some roll dates, and gives results not too different from the earlier version.
- Stinson Gibner reported the WTI simulation sometimes shows higher profit with a 1,000,000 bbl position limit than with a 5,000,000 bbl limit, e.g. the 5 MM bbl case over 3 yrs out of Nov 95.
- Stinson Gibner's explanation: a higher position limit gives a larger position to profit on trend reversals but loses much more in long trending markets; when limits are hit, losses aren't fully recovered on reversal.
