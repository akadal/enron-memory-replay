## 14:20

(mail · sent · m08010 · automatic capture)
From: Kaminski, Vince J · Subject: FW: "Convolution VAR"

- Tanya Tamarchenko (Enron Corp Research, Houston) contacted Dr. Claudio Albanese about his paper "Fast convolution method for VAR and VAR gradients", asking whether convolution/analytical VAR has been applied to non-normal distributions.
- Claudio Albanese told Tanya that his convolution VaR method is meant for non-normal distributions, that the independence assumption works well, and that agreement with empirical VaR/backtesting is excellent.
- Albanese said his principal component analysis is portfolio dependent, ranking risk-factor directions by impact on portfolio returns; he sent a test Excel spreadsheet (mpvartst.zip) for his VaR software library.
- Tanya told the owner that Albanese's spreadsheet comparing Monte-Carlo, Historical and Convolution VAR on real data gave differing results, making it hard to draw conclusions.
- Tanya proposed testing convolution VAR by generating prices with non-normal log returns and comparing simulation-based VAR with convolution VAR for chosen non-normal distributions.
