## 02:57

(mail · sent · m03077 · automatic capture)
From: Vince J Kaminski · Subject: Re: EPRM article

- The owner reviewed Chris Strickland's draft EPRM article on VaR (EPRM_01_VaR.doc), called it very well written, and sent comments Chris may use or not. (owner, verbatim: "This is very well written and will serve the reader well.")
- The owner classifies VaR models into three categories: variance/covariance, historical simulation, and Monte Carlo simulation. He sees the delta approach as a way of representing a nonlinear position, not a separate category. (owner, verbatim: "I typically use the classification based on 3 categories:")
- The owner's view: the main benefit of Monte Carlo VaR in energy markets is capturing price gapping in a straightforward way. MC revaluation can be approximate (delta, gamma Taylor expansion) or exact (the mark-to-market model). (owner, verbatim: "the gapping behavior of the energy markets in a straightforward way")
- The owner's view: historical simulation may break down in fast-evolving markets. Aggregating large portfolios into elemental instruments can cause big errors, while netting same-underlying swaps cuts MC cost with no error. (owner, verbatim: "historical simulation may break down in the markets that are evolving quickly")
- The owner promised to send Chris Strickland references on incorporating jumps into the variance/covariance VaR model. (owner, verbatim: "I shall send you the references from home")

(mail · received · m03079 · automatic capture)
From: Zimin Lu · Subject: Transport Fuel P/L

- Zimin Lu forwarded to the owner and Stinson Gibner his analysis for Colleen Sullivan (cc Andrew H Lewis, Greg Couch) of fuel P/L on NBPL long-term transport deals 1, 2, 25 and 26.
- Zimin Lu found positive fuel P/L on NBPL deals 1, 25 and 26 and negative on deal 2. He attributed this to the NYMEX curve as of 8/23/00 moving up short term and down long term versus 8/22/00.
- NBPL transport deal tenors per Zimin Lu: Deal 1 01-Sep-00 to 22-May-09; Deal 2 01-sep-00 to 28-feb-02; Deal 25 01-may-02 to 28-feb-09; Deal 26 01-mar-09 to 31-may-09.
- Zimin Lu concluded that the transport book appears to be correct on the fuel P/Ls for the NBPL deals.
- Zimin Lu noted that fuel cost is tied to index price (NYMEX+basis+index premium). He said fuel P/L must be interpreted from changes in the index curve, not NYMEX alone.
