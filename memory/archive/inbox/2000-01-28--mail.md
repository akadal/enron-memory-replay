- Krishnarao reported (1/27/2000) that Bob Butts and Wess said payments tied to one specific plant cannot be marked to market. Power sold from the plant to an outside party and bought back at multiple unrelated locations can be marked.
- Krishnarao said that even if DPC deal were marked to market, only 50% of its cashflows qualify, since outsiders own 50% of DPC. A simple loan to an affiliate also cannot be marked to market.
- Bob suggested EIPL buy options from DPC and other plants, then sell power to AP or Karnataka, to create a case for mark-to-market. Krishnarao said DPC selling power to EIPL may be politically difficult.
- Krishnarao listed three options for the India deal: (1) proceed unmarked, with income only in 2002-03; (2) sell the contract to a third party at a profit; (3) securitize revenue via an outside party in India. Bob to think it over that week.
