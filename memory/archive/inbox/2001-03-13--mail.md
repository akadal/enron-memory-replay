(mail · received · m06215 · automatic capture)
From: Sandeep Kohli · Subject: LNG by Rail

- Sandeep Kohli suggested topics the owner could discuss with Jeff Shankman in the Dabhol LNG context, proposing transport of LNG by rail as a way to collect on regas charges while finding alternate gas customers.
- Sandeep Kohli told the owner he had earlier looked into moving LNG by train: cryogenic containers that can be trucked and transferred from trucks onto train bogies, to build an LNG market for commercial customers in India.
- Sandeep Kohli noted the Konkan Rail line is about 1 hr 45 min from the Dabhol plant; trucks could carry LNG to the rail line at Guhagar for distribution up and down India's west coast.
- Sandeep Kohli argued that selling LNG directly to end-consumers at retail-type prices would give large margins even at small volumes, since their alternative fuels (diesel, naphtha) are expensive and trucked by road.
- Sandeep Kohli said the aim is to keep India income targets and activity up so analysts do not hit Enron's stock, and offered to explore the LNG-by-rail idea further if it appeals to the owner.
