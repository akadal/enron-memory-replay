- Per the July 9, 2001 India press clips, Enron chairman Kenneth Lay visited New Delhi to meet PM Vajpayee, finance minister Yashwant Sinha and power minister Suresh Prabhu about the Dabhol Power Company dispute with MSEB.

- Per the July 2001 clips, Maharashtra's BJP backed a judicial probe into the Enron deal; the DF committee deferred that decision to July 11. The Godbole panel recommended privatising MSEB generation and distribution.
