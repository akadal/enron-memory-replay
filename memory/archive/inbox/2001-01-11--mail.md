## 01:08

(mail · received · m05273 · automatic capture)
From: Zimin Lu · Subject: Re: Costless Collar for Hanover

- Costless collar valuation for Hanover (HC): Research (Bob Lee) used actual 3-year historical vol of 47.2%, giving a call strike of 97.978; Bob Lee confirmed the swap has no impact on the collar's value.
- Bob Lee (Research) said a vol estimate of 40-50% could be justified for the Hanover collar, based on traded HC options and expected fall-off in vol for long-dated options.
- Chris Loehr and Ryan Siurek adjusted the research collar model: treasury rate 5.127% at 12/28/00 (research used 4.6%) and maturity 6/30/03, 2.5 years (research used 3 years).
- With those adjustments and 47.2% vol, Chris Loehr reported the Hanover collar at ceiling 92 103/256 and floor 34 7/8 (Bloomberg and research model agree); Bob Lee said the parties have converged.
- Hanover collar work involved Ron Baker, Wes Colwell, Anne Yaeger, Andrea V Reed on the accounting side; Zimin Lu (Research) supervised Bob Lee and praised the result.
