## 01:08

(mail · received · m05481 · automatic capture)
From: Vasant Shanbhogue · Subject: Risk article

- Vasant Shanbhogue forwarded to the owner and Tanya Tamarchenko a Ben Parsons (London) note on a Risk article about calculating default probabilities from CDS quotes; Steve Leppard had pointed the article out.
- Ben Parsons said the Risk article closely agrees with Enron London's own methodology for CDS valuation and reverse-engineering default probabilities, and is essentially the same as his credit pricing paper plus one excellent idea.
- Per Ben Parsons, the article's extension: rather than linearly interpolating CDS quotes, minimise an error measure combining smoothness of the default probability curve and model-vs-market price differences.
- Ben Parsons said that with extra time, resources and brainpower, the London group could publish similar work themselves.
