## 05:45

(mail · received · m01176 · automatic capture)
From: Zimin Lu · Subject: UK Swap RPI Model

- Zimin Lu forwarded to the owner and Maureen Raymond (2000-03-31) a UK RPI model developed by Martina Angelova (London, x34327), built by bootstrapping RPI swaps; she had sent it to Zimin on 03/22/2000 asking for comments.
- Martina Angelova's RPI swap structure: semi/semi Act/365F, YOYUKRPI = (UKRPI(p-2)/UKRPI(p-14) - 1)/2, p = payment month; first payment is the latest known historical RPI, February 2000.
- In her RPI model Martina Angelova assumed constant cashflows between quoted years rather than interpolating swaps, which she said distorts the curve a lot; she compared the swap-derived RPI curve with the one from the GILT market.
- Anjam Ahmad and Trena McFarland (London) were copied on Martina Angelova's UK Swap RPI Model memo, indicating they are involved with the London RPI work.
